After school & hot springs PART - 3

BrutalBot September 27, 2021

The next story will be a new chapter.

Following Kirasaka, we visited the hot spring, or perhaps it would be more appropriate to call it a traditional inn.

The inn evoked a sense of nostalgia, yet it was kept remarkably clean and pristine.

It was easy to imagine that the employees maintained it daily.

As expected to a facility tied with Kirasaka Corporation.

Since it was after school when we visited the inn, we didn't relax in the lobby, but instead split up into men and women and headed for the bathhouse.

I went into the men's bath with Yuuto following behind.

 The first thought that came to mind was, "Is this really okay for us to enjoy such a luxury?"

"There's an open-air bath too!? Let's check it out later."

"...Yeah."

Maybe Yuuto next to me didn't care, he said with a little excitement with his eyes shining.

Well, for today, let's just think of it as coming to relax our bodies and not dig too deep into unnecessary things.

I decided to enjoy the bath, which was completely empty except for me and Yuuto.

As I finished washing my body and took my first dip in the bath, Yuuto muttered.

"In this kind of scenario, hearing the conversation over from the women's bath is the how the cliche goes rihgt...?.

"You've been reading too much manga..."

All I could hear was the sound of hot water pouring and our conversation.

The fact that it was just the two of us in such a large hot spring made me feel uncomfortable.

There was also a sauna and a cold water bath, but I didn't like them much.

After warming up for a while, we went to the outdoor bath.

"Man, this feels amazing"

"..."

I leaned back and looked up at the sky as I soaked in the hot water.

As summer approached and daylight hours increased, though tinged with crimson still remained bright.

It felt strange to me that I was here on a weekday, on my way to home from school.

If I had come on a weekend, I would have been able to relax from the bottom of my heart.

If I listened carefully, I could even hear the sound of the river flowing nearby.

Maybe I'll come back next time with Kaede when Mom comes home.

If I do that, I have to ask Kuroi-san about the way here.

In the worst case scenario, I can ask Kirasaka and she should tell me right away.

"I.. I'm heading out now."

"What? Come on, Minato. Why don't you stay and relax a little longer?"

Somehow, I felt like if I stayed in here any longer, I’d end up dizzy.

After saying a few words to Yuuto, I left the bathing area.

When I went out to the changing room, I found a simple change of clothes that hadn't been there when I came in.

There was also a yukata for rent, but considering that we would be leaving in a bit, changing into it would be a hassle.

So I thought I could just change into my uniform again, but I wondered if Kuroi-san had prepared this for me.

The Т-shirt, which was just the right size, had a pattern printed on it with letters.

[Rei LOVE] (Tl/n - 怜LOVE)

"...Like I could ever wear that."

I immediately threw it on the floor.

When did Kirasaka prepare such a stupid thing!?

As I let out a sigh of dismay, I saw another shirt under it.

She had her fun with the prank, yet she’d also had the foresight to prepare a perfectly normal alternative, thoroughly amazed by her level of preparedness, I unfolded the second shirt.

[I ♥ Shizuku] (Tl/n - 雫愛)

"...I said, like I could ever wear that!"

Pincer attack, who could've though.

There I was, alone in the changing room, angry retorting loudly at this ridiculous prank which even Shizuku even joined in. It turned out, there were no normal clothes available for me to change into, so I put on my uniform and went out to the lobby.

There, I found massage machines, vending machines, a simple dining area, and a place where we could play table tennis and other games.

"Oh, just give it up already!"

"You’re the one being annoyingly persistent, Kirasaka-san!"

Having loosened their yukatas to let off a little steam after their hot bath, were playing table tennis against each other as they had talked about at the school.

I had no idea what was fueling such intense passion between them, but it seemed neither was willing to accept defeat.

They'd already gone well past ten points, yet the gap in the score hadn't changed. I wasn't familiar with the rules, but were they still going because of deuce or something?

(Tl/n - I ain't a ping pong expert so here's what I found on the internet - "In tennis, a deuce is a tie that must be broken by someone scoring two points in a row.")

Kuroi-san was acting as a referee, watching with a gentle smile but I wondered what what it that he was enjoying.

I bought a cup of coffee milk a little further away and watched them play their game.

"Whoa, look at them go!"

"Doesn't look like that match is going to end anytime soon, though."

Yuuto appeared with a towel on his head and sat down on the chair next to me, wiping his wet hair.

He also has a cup of coffee milk in his hand, just like mine.

You really can't beat coffee milk after a dip in the hot springs.

You could say it's a classic.

I turned my gaze away from Yuuto for a moment, and returned it to the ping-pong table.

I don't know anything about the techniques, skills, rules, or format of the game except for the simple ones we had in class, but I still know something at least.

They were playing at a level so advanced that it was utterly impossible to believe they were mere beginners.

To a serious ping-pong player, it's probably just a rally.

(Tl/n - Consecutive back and forth shots between players)

But to me, their ability to keep rallying at such a high speed is nothing short of amazing.

It was enough to make the phrase "a complete waste of talent" actually cross my mind. For a while, Yuuto and I stood side by side, waiting for the girls to finish their game.

It's a good way to pass the time after a bath.

Both of them were absorbed in the game with a serious look in their eyes, as if they were participating in a tournament.

About ten minutes after we started watching the game, we ran out of drinks and were wondering what to buy next when Kuroi-san, who was supposed to be a referee, came up to us and offered us some tea.

"Thank you very much. "

I thanked him and accepted the tea.

Next to me, Yuuto also accepted the tea.

Kuroi-san smiled and turned his eyes to them, especially Kirasaka.

"The young lady really seems to be enjoying herself these days"

"I suppose so..."

After all, she went so far as to prepare a change of clothes for someone else, complete with her own personal logo stitched right onto them.

I think she's enjoying this situation well enough.

Hearing my reply, Kuroi-san gave a small bow to me.

"It is all thanks to meeting you, Mr. Shinra... Thank you."

"...Please don't say that."

I stood up and asked Kuroi-san to raise his head.

As he reluctantly lifted his face, I spoke to him with complete honesty.

"...To say that it happened since you meeting me, that is merely a conclusion drawn in hindsight."

Even if he met met me, there might have been some point along the way where things turned out completely differently than they have now.

It's easy to look at the outcome and say that it was somehow inevitable.

It's against my way of thinking to assume that the current situation was created by a series of coincidences and was just because that he met just a single individual.

That said, Kuroi-san is likely right, it is certainly true that Yuuto and I have played some part, however small it may be, can be a part of the reason why Kirasaka is able to enjoy her life so much these days. Still, I couldn't quite bring myself to simply nod along when he phrased it as if the gratitude were directed solely at me.

I'm not sure if he understood my intentions, but Kuroi-san still smiled like before.

"I know you're going to say that, Shinra-sama, but please consider this just a thank you from an old man"

 "....Well, if you put it that way..."

I'm not sure if he understood how twisted people like me were, so he thanked me in a way that was hard to refuse.

I'll take it as an honest thank you for now.

From that point on, I watched the girls, along with Kuroi-san.

The two of them certainly had the worst chemistry.

When their eyes meet, they glare at each other, it's hard to win in a battle of wills like this.

Given that their capabilities were so evenly matched, perhaps such friction was inevitable.

However, it may be that they are somehow similar to each other.

Precisely because they're like cats and dogs, and because they only ever notice each other's flaws, they're able to be honest with one another.

There was probably a reason words like frenemies existed. (Tl/n - 悪友)

I think Kuroi-san should be thanking Shizuku, not me.

I looked out the window of the strange building, where despite being a large facility, there are only the four of us, Kuroi-san, and the employees.

The sun had completely set, and another day was coming to an end.

Once the girls' game is over, we'll go home and our normal routine will continue.

It was a nice break and a change of pace.

Even if I called it ordinary now that we had advanced to our second year, things were distinctly different from previous years.

Also, there could be unexpected events and happenings waiting for us.

Aware that the sense of refreshment I had just felt was already beginning to fade, I simply sat there for the moment, resting my body in the quiet of that place

This is the end of the side-story and the next story will be a new chapter.

I was wondering if I should write about the result of the ping-pong match between Shizuku and Ren, but I decided to end it without writing about it this time.

I'll try my best to update the story so that you can enjoy the development from the next episode.
