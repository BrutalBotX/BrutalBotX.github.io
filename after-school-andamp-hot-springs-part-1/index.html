After school & hot springs PART - 1

BrutalBot September 11, 2021

If you haven't checked yet, pls go and check out my first post: here.

<Author's notes>

I decided to post a short story before proceeding to the next chapter of the story.
I would appreciate it if you would read it after taking a break.

Not just the high school, but the main events in student's life often occur at school.

The reason for this is none other than the fact that, as the title ideal student implies that most of their day is spent at school.

Early morning greetings, lunchtime gossips, after-school amusement, and sometimes avoiding teacher's gaze during class to make small talks.

The same should be the case for me, without exception. But... most of the time while I am at school something irregular happens.

Even today, my neighbor made an unexpected comment.

"I want to go to the hot springs..."

"...Hot springs. I haven't been to hot springs lately, but I used to like them."

I don't hate hot springs.

Not only do they clean or bodies, but also relieve us from stress and fatigue. But most importantly, I can enjoy my time alone.

There is no need for conversations. It's just nice to take a bath in the tub and rest my body without thinking about my daily worries.

In the town where we live, there are only small public bathhouses and no extensive hot spring facilities, but one's not too far if you have a car to go with.

Moreover, if it's Kirasaka, she can easily go there with a driver. Considering the location and time, Hakone would be a safe bet.

In my brain search, Hakone was the only one that came up which was under a day long trip. As I was pondering over her comment, she raised her voice slightly.

"Well, that's good to know. Let's go after school today, shall we?"

"Absolutely not."

Word of denial just came out like a reflex. What the hell is this girl talking about?

Kirasaka, who had her smartphone out during class, had already several hot spring facilities displayed on the screen.

Perhaps she had the same idea because Hakone was entered in the search field. Even though I don't mind hot springs, I'm not as active as to get ready to go around after school.

Besides, I have things to do after school too.

As a member of the homegoing club, which competes to be the first to go home, I have the mission to get home as fast as I can.

It's no exaggeration to say that this mission is more important than the classes. Moreover, if I had to add, I'd rather lay on the lie in the living room after sweating in the bath bath, while waiting for Kaede to cook dinner.

I immediately rejected Kirasaka's suggestion and turned my gaze to the blackboard.

There, I saw Shizuku answering the questions posed by the teacher with ease. She was able to solve the problem that many of the students had not been able answer.

While most of the boys in the class were staring at Shizuku with a look of admiration, she turned around to go back to her seat after solving the problem and her gaze met mine.

"..."

"..."

A sharp gaze, like a carnivore eyeing its prey.

Were you listening the conversation between me and Kirasaka? I don't think it was possible to hear it from here or maybe she has surpassed human hearing capabilities?

There is saying that some people have sharp hearing, but that may be more true than I thought. As I felt a chill running down my spine, Kirasaka still didn't give up and continued.

"It can't be helped, I'll make some compromise ...In the neighborhood today, how about here?"

"Why are you assuming that I'm going...?"

Why is she acting as if she is compromising because of me? I don't remember being a part of it in the first place.

I averted my gaze sideways until I saw that Shizuku had sat at her place, and was able to avoid the comments of my neighbor by nodding appropriately.

"Then, the next problem is..."

The students listened as the teacher finished explaining the problem and moved on to the next one.

I wonder if they were really listening or if they were just letting it flow out the other ear.

Normally, I would just copy the questions and answers in my notebook and ignore most of the lessons, but today I made an exception and listened to the teacher.

Otherwise, the after-school hot spring presentation from the girl next to me would become too much to bear.

After school, I promptly left my seat when I heard the bell ringing to signal the end of the day in the school.

I got off to a good head start, and I was sure I was almost in the hallway before anyone else. The next thing I knew, my vision had flipped.

That's right, I was literally flipped about.

I felt a strong shock and pain in my back and a choking sensation in my chest.

"Ugh!"

A stupid voice came out from the sudden shock. Then, Kirasaka's voice reached my ears from above my head.

"I'm sure you're aware of it. You can't have a student council member breaking the school rules."

I'm not sure if she anticipated that I would run away in advance, but when I got up, she put her body on my back and restrained me.

It wasn't just a matter of getting caught; I was certain I’d moved faster than anyone else in the classroom, so how was it that she was already standing right in front of the door, blocking my path?

Maybe if it were a shonen manga, I could accept being overpowered by someone with superhuman physical abilities. But in reality? Having someone beat me to the punch like that, it stung my pride a little as a guy.

Well, I don't have a great amount of pride that would be hurt anyways...

Kirasaka and I, who created a sort of spectacle in the classroom, gathered the eyes of our classmates, even if we didn't like.

Naturally, Yuuto was also looking at us. I'll never forget the way he raised his eyebrows, lowered his shoulders as if he was sighing, and looked at me.

I'm sure I'll get back at you for that. Next, I turned my gaze to the side where Shizuku was sitting, but she wasn't there.

Instead, I heard a voice from above.

"It's a good idea to keep your hands off of Minato-kun and refrain from making a scene in the classroom."

"I'm sorry to hear that. I'll be more careful in the future... But, you should also be careful not to get too close to him."

Scary ...Girls' fighting are scary.

They are both talking completely above my head angrily. They were also whispering lines at the end of their sentences which would be only heard by those two.

Their smiles, which I caught peripheral vision, was even more scary. Why are women able to smile and talk with their natural enemies?

"If you're going to take Minato-kun out, you'll have to get my approval first."

"Why do I have to ask you? What I do with him is my business, isn't it?"

"No. ...You have to ask me first before you can proceed with any plans."

The fundamentals are wrong. I tried to utter a few words to them about what they said as if I was their property, but they couldn't seem to hear me.

The girls' conversation continued to heat up, leaving me completely in the background.

As if to stop it, Yuuto intervened.

"It's not the kind of thing you want to talk about in a place like this, right?"

I wonder why. I'm not sure why, but Yuuto seems very reliable.

I can see a strong halo behind him, like an Prince Charming.

...is what I thought, but it was the sunlight coming in through the window behind him.

Ogiwara Yuuto, who uses natural light into his own power ...a terrifying guy.

Such thoughts come to my mind in this place that has become entirely blank. More importantly, though, couldn't they please release me from this hold?

It is at the limit, and if they're not careful, It'll even bend the other way..

"Then, we will decide the game with table tennis after the hot spring."

"No problem! There's no way I'm going to lose."

"I don't see how I can lose - No choice, let's go with that."

...Go with what?

I could hear a plan which had nothing to do with my opinions. I don't know how it happened, and I don't understand why the decision was made decide it through a game.

However, the three were strangely convinced, and I knew from experience that I couldn't avoid it.

"Well, we have a car waiting, shall we go?"

"No, I'm not going let me go, oi!"

Kirasaka started walking after undoing the joint lock and grabbed the neck of my uniform.

Shizuku and Yuuto follow me as I was dragged like a cat. Shizuku smiled to relieve me with a smile, but it'll just have the opposite effect for me now.

I wonder what they're going to do with me. If there's one thing I understood, it's that we're definitely going to a hot spring.

As the main story continued to take a serious turn, I decided to make the small story a little more relaxed.
