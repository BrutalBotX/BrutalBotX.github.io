<h1 style="text-align: center">#310</h1>
<hr />
<p> </p>
<p>Holding hands—it sounds simple enough when you put it into words.</p>
<p>You just reach out and grasp each other’s hands. But what seems simple can surprisingly be difficult.</p>
<p>With a hundred people, there are a hundred different ways it could happen. Everyone has their preferences and dislikes. So even when you extend your hand, hoping to connect, sometimes they pass each other without touching.</p>
<p>Even when you want them to meet, it’s not that straightforward.</p>
<h6>&lt;BBX: Idk what this was all about&gt;</h6>
<p> </p>
<p>Standing alone in the corridor, I stare down at the screen of my smartphone held tightly in my right hand. A notification from the messaging app is displayed there, naturally from Miyashita.</p>
<p>She left with a typical tsundere-like line, “Don’t you dare tell anyone I told you this!” before returning to the classroom ahead of me.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>That’s not your character… But I won’t say anything.</p>
<p>What she sent me was a single URL. It’s probably a link to a video site. I don’t immediately open it. Pressing it would probably start the video immediately. Watching it now might give others a chance to reject my offer if they happen to see or hear it.</p>
<p>They say ‘haste makes waste’, but being too slow can also backfire.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Calming my rushing heart, I hurriedly stuff my phone into my pocket and return to the classroom. The discomfort from being overly full just moments ago is now gone.</p>
<p>The classroom is bustling. My classmates are chatting discontentedly, knowing that afternoon classes are about to start soon.</p>
<p>Glancing at them from the corner of my eye, I cross the classroom.</p>
<p>Despite the gazes from Shizuku and Kirasaka, who were still near my desk during lunch break, I quickly return to my seat and rummage through my bag. Pulling out my earphones, I quickly leave the classroom again, heading back to the corridor.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Minato-kun? Class is about to start soon, you know?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…I’ll be back in a moment.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As Shizuku asked me curiously, I replied briefly and retraced my steps. Slipping through the students, I passed by the spot where I had just exchanged words with Miyashita, climbed the stairs, opened the door at the far end, and stepped onto the rooftop. There, I sat on the bench placed there, immediately putting on my earphones.</p>
<p>Finally, I opened the link sent by Miyashita, and as expected, the screen of a well-known video-sharing site automatically popped up.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>In the center of the “MUGIch” screen was the account name.</p>
<p>‘ch’ probably stands for ‘channel’.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It’s simple and appealing to me, but I wonder if it’s trendy.</p>
<p>I’m not very familiar with these kinds of sites, so I have no idea about the mainstream trends.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>A lively BGM and opening sequence played, with a circular icon bouncing and a photo of the video uploader flashing inside the circle, making a peace sign with her eyes.</p>
<p>She wasn’t wearing a school uniform, but she looked like a modern high school girl.</p>
<p>Her hair was long and loosely curled, and in simple terms, she had a gal-like appearance. But her face seemed familiar.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Isn’t this Nakayama?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The person on the screen was Nakayama, a classmate of mine. She was the girl Miyashita had been walking behind until recently.</p>
<p>She’s one of the top-ranked students in our class, almost like a boss among the girls.</p>
<p>…So even students like her upload videos, huh? Surprising. That’s my honest reaction, followed by bitter memories.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Though our interactions are minimal, her impression of me is undoubtedly not favourable. In fact, she might consider me among the worst in the class.</p>
<p>Is this why Miyashita made me promise not to mention she told me about it? It makes sense, unexpectedly.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Even though our interactions have dwindled, having a top-ranked student in the school view me negatively could be troublesome in various ways, especially among female students’ social circles.</p>
<p>But people are always told not to judge solely based on appearances, though there might not be many cases as challenging to grasp as hers. I can understand short videos popular among high school girls, like TikToks or whatever.</p>
<p>But actively operating on a solid video-sharing site—that’s quite different from the image I had of her.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>…Anyway, the problem lies in the content.</p>
<p>To move forward, I need to determine if her video editing skills meet my requirements.</p>
<p>I refocus on the playing video and start examining it. After the self-made opening, Nakayama appears and delivers an introductory phrase.</p>
<p>The main categories seem to be makeup and beauty-related. The videos are typically under ten minutes, covering essential points and making them easy to watch as a viewer. The use of sound effects and occasional flashy effects seems to reflect her personality. But I could finish watching it without feeling uncomfortable, which is likely because she’s accustomed to it.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Judging by the frequency of her uploads, she’s been active for over a year.</p>
<p>Ignoring my personal preferences, her slightly flashy editing might be suitable for a graduation video.</p>
<p>Removing my earphones and looking up at the sky, I gently close my eyelids.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>…She surely has her own PC, and if I’m looking for someone within a short period, she might be the most suitable candidate. Whether I can invite her or not is not something to think about right now.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I have no choice but to invite her.</p>
<p>It’s one of the few tasks left for me to do.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As a precaution, I also check the channel’s description and related URLs. Her SNS account is listed there, just in case.</p>
<p>I should confirm this much before proceeding.</p>
<p>Opening the app I recently installed, her account had posted something just five minutes ago.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Shall I go see the cherry blossoms on my way home?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The attached photo showed a cherry blossom tree along a roadside embankment known locally as “Sakura Avenue.”</p>
<p>If I want to contact Nakayama, this might be my only chance.</p>
<p>She’s always at the center of attention in school. This is something to discuss in private. Since there are two more classes in the afternoon, I still have at least two hours to think about it.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Oh…!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Since there’s no clock on the rooftop, I check the time on my smartphone screen and accidentally let out a sound. It’s already been twenty minutes since the start of class.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Oops… What’s the first class in the afternoon again?</p>
<p>Returning to the classroom, the cold stares I received upon my arrival are something I’ll never get used to, no matter how many times I experience them. Fortunately, it seems Yuuto or someone else made up a reason for my absence, citing stomach pains from overeating lunch. Despite feeling apologetic for the concerned inquiries, I assured them I was fine and took my seat.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>She’s not someone I’ve ever really bonded with. With our limited interactions, convincing her would likely be difficult using my usual approach.</p>
<p>But it’s okay, there’s still time. I clasped my hands together on the desk, took a deep breath, and calmly submerged my thoughts into the sea of contemplation.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>When was the last time two hours felt this short? The classroom had transitioned into an after-school atmosphere, with students chatting and preparing to head home. Where were they planning to go afterward? Were club activities tough today?</p>
<p>Amidst the various conversations, Nakayama’s presence was absent. She must have already left for home, taking photos of cherry blossoms along her route.</p>
<p>Swiftly gathering my belongings and rising from my seat, I turned to face Shizuku who had approached me.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Sorry, could you let the student council know I won’t be attending today? I have something I need to take care of before the day ends.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Eh, okay… but…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Sorry. I appreciate it.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Hurrying to deliver the message to Shizuku and then swiftly exiting the classroom, I should be able to send a message to the committee.</p>
<p>After talking with Nakayama, there should still be some time left to assist with the preparations upon returning to school.</p>
<p>Taking cautious steps forward with my somewhat rusty legs, I head towards my destination, knowing that even if she’s not as agile as before, I should still make it on time.</p>
<p> </p>
<p> </p>
<p>Underneath the rows of cherry blossom trees along the embankment, a high school girl holds her phone, stretching her arm out as if trying to get closer to the branches, completely absorbed.</p>
<p>Seeing her figure, I obtained the ultimate confirmation that the person in the video was indeed her.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I’ve thought through the structure of the conversation. After devoting all my class time to contemplation, I should have found a plausible sequence and reasoning.</p>
<p>Now, all that’s left is to gauge her reaction and respond flexibly.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…Can you take good pictures?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Though my lungs felt tight and sweat trickled down my forehead, I maintained composure and addressed the girl engrossed in her photography.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Oh, um, I can’t seem to get the focus right… Wait, it’s you.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Hello there.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As Nakayama turned around, her expression noticeably shifted upon seeing my face.</p>
<p>There’s no reason for her to want to meet after school.</p>
<p>I fully agreed with that sentiment. However, I needed to humble myself and make the request.</p>
<p>Setting aside personal feelings, I lined up the words as planned.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I have a favour to ask…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>With a bowed head, I proceeded to make my request while displaying Nakayama’s channel on the screen.</p>
