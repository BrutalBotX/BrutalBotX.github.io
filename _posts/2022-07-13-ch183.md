<h1 style="text-align:center;">#183</h1>
<p></p>
<hr>
<p>The three of them, except for me, were standing there with a shocked expression on their faces.</p>
<p>It seems that the announcement of my candidacy for the student council was a shocking statement even to them.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"I'm going to be running for the president's assistant position, ...since it's currently that, and I don't think anyone will even run for it."</p>
<p></p>
<p>Many students wouldn't even know that such a position existed in the first place.<br>Also, if I get in as an assistant, I don't have to worry about being in the same position as Hino and the rest of the current council members.</p>
<p>Yuuto didn't seem to be aiming for any particular position either, so when I suggested this, he nodded his head.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"But Minato, are you serious about this?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Super duper serious, you know..."</p>
<p></p>
<p>If it was a joke, it would've been a seriously bad one.<br>For once, there were no lies, and it was a genuine announcement of my candidacy for the for the student council.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The number of students in the student council of Sakuranaoka Academy is originally five.</p>
<p>However, since the current president has created the role of an assistant, the number of members has increased by one, to six.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Let's assume that Shiraishi and Koizumi will be the president and vice president, respectively, and Hino-kun will continue as the general secretary.</p>
<p>Miura has also indicated that she would continue as the treasurer, which leaves two slots, one for a secretary and the other for an assistant.</p>
<p></p>
<p>At first, I thought about joining in as the secretary, but then I decided not to and continue on with my current position.</p>
<p>Well, I will explain the reason for this later.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Anyway, when Yuuto and I were arguing, I decided that the role of assistant was the best for me, considering the trouble I would cause around me.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"But that's too much..."</p>
<p></p>
<p>Yuuto said with a difficult expression on his face.<br>But after saying that, he hesitantly slurred his words.</p>
<p></p>
<p>He might be trying to say that I was at a disadvantage.<br>Also, he probably hesitated to say so because he was being challenged to a contest.</p>
<p></p>
<p>It is true that in this kind of election, name recognition greatly affects the outcome.<br>Naturally, there is a huge difference between me and Yuuto, so it would seem to be a reckless contest if you think about it.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"First of all, Yuuto, is the assistant's position fine?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Aah."</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Then, I only have one thing to say, ...don't be shy, go all out so that you can thoroughly beat your opponent."</p>
<p></p>
<p>I fearlessly threw out words mixed with provocation.<br>Yuuto smiles as he realizes that I am neither afraid nor desperate to compete with him.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"If that's the case, I'll do it. It's my first time going up against Minato, and to be honest, I'm looking forward to it.</p>
<p></p>
<p>With that, Yuuto turned back with his bag in hand and walked out of the classroom.<br>Did he reflect on himself that his back looks bigger than it did yesterday?</p>
<p></p>
<p>I sighed alone in the classroom after Yuuto left, and turned to the direction of the two waiting at the back.<br>As I was thinking about what to say, I was hit on my head lightly.</p>
<p>There was no pain, just a nimble sound.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Nice shot!</p>
<p>And when I looked at the hand that hit me, I saw that Kirasaka was holding prints folded up like a harisen (large paper fan).</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Are you out of your mind? Of all the things you challenged him to a game convenient for him."</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Even so, you don't just suddenly hit me..."</p>
<p></p>
<p>Kirasaka understands.<br>She also believes that the student elections are merely a popularity contest just disguised with another name.</p>
<p>So, let's say, the game is completely in Yuuto's favor.<br>She said I should have tried to compete in my area of expertise.</p>
<p></p>
<p>However, that's not good enough.<br>Both Yuuto and I think that fighting in a new situation for the first time is meaningful...</p>
<p></p>
<p>Was it a mistake?<br>I said it confidently out of momentum and my peculiar ideas, but now, when I am calm, I indeed do feel that it's way over the top...</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Well, I guess we'll manage."</p>
<p></p>
<p>Positive thinking is the best.<br>I believe someone once said that if you live your life with the right mentality, you might be able to make it work.</p>
<p>Jokes in moderation, I looked at the two of them and saw their anxious expressions.<br>Even I, the one who challenged them, couldn't see the odds of victory at a level that I have no idea what to do next.</p>
<p>If I were to judge the situation as it is now, I would say that the odds are completely stacked against me.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"If Minato-kun is going to be on the student council, I wouldn't mind running for it either..."</p>
<p></p>
<p>She said that. And her eyes were like, 'what should I do?'</p>
<p>As she said the other day, if my presence is the basis of Shizuku's action, it is of course inevitable that she will be in the election as well.</p>
<p>However, it is the consequences she worries about.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The reason why I didn't go along with the secretary's position.</p>
<p>It was so that a position would be available when either Shizuku or possibly Kirasaka ran for the student council.</p>
<p></p>
<p>However, if she runs for that slot, she will naturally take that position if no one else comes forward against her.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Here, the problem is if I am not elected.</p>
<p>She would have to join the student council without me, which is supposed to be against her principle of action.</p>
<p>From her point of view, she would be sitting in an organization for a year that she has no motive in and so she would just be wasting her time.</p>
<p>So I shook my head in denial at what Shizuku said.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"I haven't heard anything about anyone running for the secretary's position as of now, and in case Shizuku is thinking participating, it should come after the results are in."</p>
<p></p>
<p>"After the results?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>"...Ah."</p>
<p></p>
<p>There is no presidential nomination slot for which Hiiragi Akane forced me to join the student council.</p>
<p>However, the nomination quota does exist.</p>
<p>The system works by recommending the nominated student to join the student council if all the student council members agree and the teacher in charge also agrees.</p>
<p>If a student as good as Shizuku could use this system, she could join the student council without any problem.</p>
<p>When I explained this, Shizuku nodded a few times in agreement, and Kirasaka seemed to be thinking about it with her hand on her chin.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Anyway, the stage was now set for the competition, but here comes the problem.</p>
<p>The problem is that I'm going up against the prince of the school.</p>
<p></p>
<p>I would need to come up with an appropriate strategy.<br>I don't have the confidence or the experience to win on my own.</p>
<p>It's obvious that I cannot even compete with him without help from someone.<br>But, who should I ask help from?</p>
<p></p>
<p>The two people in front of me might agree if I ask them.<br>However, if I rely entirely on these two people, it will not be a game between me and Yuuto.</p>
<p></p>
<p>I want them to help me create an opportunity.<br>That person should be the only one who can create a connection between me and the current students.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"I understand the whole story of your candidacy and how you handled it afterwards, so how are you going to deal with that clunker of a prince, Shinra-kun?"</p>
<p>"A clunker..."</p>
<p></p>
<p>What a wonderful naming sense.<br>I was put into silence, but I quickly stated my answer to her question.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"I'll bring Shiraishi over here."</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Shiraishi-san?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>Shizuku asks back at my proposal.<br>Her expression was a little grim, perhaps in response to the fact that her name wasn't mentioned first...</p>
<p>Next to her, Kirasaka was glaring at me silently.<br>Why do these children suddenly become so scary?</p>
<p></p>
<p>"There are things I want you two to help me with, but if I ask you two to help me with everything, it'll be you two versus Yuuto, not me, that's why it's Shiraishi."</p>
<p></p>
<p>Regardless of the outcome of Shiraishi and Koizumi, if they can bring in the next student council members, they will have a big impact.　</p>
<p>If the next student council president is in support, there will be at least a bit of interest.</p>
<p>That's how you have to start using short, thin connections to get recognized a little by little at a time to compete.</p>
<p></p>
<p>But ...the biggest problem is that there's not something that someone has ever faced in the past.</p>
<p>There is only one person in this school who has the potential to bring everything I assume to nothingness.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Can we go somewhere for a minute?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>"?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>"I don't mind."</p>
<p></p>
<p>I told them that and left the classroom with my bag.<br>Shizuku and Kirasaka also followed.</p>
<p></p>
<p>I moved from the second building to the first building for the seniors and walked up the stairs to the front of the heavy door.</p>
<p>I wonder how that person, who is supposed to be in this building, would judge my action.</p>
<p></p>
<p>It is meaningless. No matter what or how I think, I can't predict it.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p></p>
<p>I take a step forward and knock on the door a few times.<br>Voices inside this thick, soundproofed door cannot be heard down the hallway.</p>
<p></p>
<p>So, before waiting for a reply, I put my hand on the door and pulled it open.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"...Excuse me."</p>
<p></p>
<p>After greeting, I turned my gaze to my usual seat.<br>It is the seat where the head of this room sits.</p>
<p></p>
<p>There was a woman sitting there as a matter of course.<br>With a smile on her face she sat there as if she knew we were coming.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Shinra, Kanazaki and Rei are with you this time. Sit down for now and I'll go get some tea."</p>
<p></p>
<p>I sat down on my seat and the two behind me sat down on the guest's sofa when urged.</p>
<p>The president opened her mouth when the cold barley tea that she had brought from the back of the room had been passed around to everyone.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"What interesting story are you going to tell me today?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>The sunlight shining through the window made the president's light brown hair shine even brighter.</p>
<p>The unique presence she exudes and the way she stands there raises the tension to another level.</p>
<p></p>
<p>It was not only me but also Shizuku and Kirasaka too.<br>They too looked a bit more stiff than usual.</p>
<p></p>
<p>...Please don't expect something.<br>To the senpai, whom even Kirasaka could not get her head around, I started speaking with a wry smile.</p>
<p></p>
<p>I started explaining the conversation we had just had to the senpai who was looking at me expectantly.</p>
<p></p>
