<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#142</h1>


<hr />
<p>Momiji Shiraishi.<br />First-year student at Sakuranaoka Academy.</p>

<p>Blood type: B<br />Hobbies: Reading and taking a walk with her dog.</p>
<p>2nd of three sisters and a member of the yearbook committee at our school.</p>
<pre>&lt;TL: Sorry(＞﹏＜), I've been translating 'yearbook committee' as 'grade committee' in previous chapters, I'll correct them soon.&gt;</pre>
<p> </p>
<p>Not a member of any clubs, but is a candidate for the next student council.</p>

<p>She is highly trusted by the teachers, and they hope that she will become a role model for the next generation of students similar to Hiiragi Akane.</p>
<p>In order to meet their expectations, Momiji Shiraishi, who was running for the student council president, found the answer to her question: she should be disciplined and bold.</p>

<p>Just as Akane Hiiragi is, she should be as well.<br />As a result, the usually calm and friendly student turned out to be the one who announced that she was going to transform the student council.</p>

<p>In reality, she was not far from the president, but she wanted to get as close as possible. <br />Which resulted in the idea of Shizuku and the others joining the student council is what she told me in a low voice.</p>

<p>"In fact, I wasn't even going to join the yearbook committee ...but since I had the best grades in my class, my homeroom teacher encouraged me to join and..."</p>

<p>"So you didn't decline?"</p>

<p>"...Yes"</p>

<p>As a result, Shiraishi, who was basically excellent, was able to handle the yearbook committee without difficulty. It would have been more convenient for her if she wasn't able to.</p>

<p>"If I don't have the right words and the answers to the questions people might ask ...I get nervous and have trouble communicating further."</p>

<p>"So, did you also prepare questions  for today's conversation?"</p>

<p>"Um ...I made a lot of predictions of the situations that may occur. ...But I hadn't expected Kanazaki-senpai and Kirasaka-senpai to be present there."</p>

<p>Complete waste of talent.<br />She is constantly just trying to compensate for her weaknesses in human relations, but if an unexpected situation arises like this, she can't deal with ti.</p>

<p>It's more correct to say that she can't handle it flexibly.<br />That's why she was so upset and worried when I came to the canteen.</p>

<p>I guess she's just clumsy.<br />But I'm sure she has the talent to make up for it.</p>
<p>Preparing all the questions and answers correctly is not something I would normally think of doing.</p>
<p>It is different from the questions asked in a job interview.</p>

<p>To be honest, it's not worth the effort to do it for everyday conversations.</p>
<p>However, there are students I know who can't hold a conversation without doing so. Once again, I was reminded that there are many peculiar students enrolled in this school.</p>



<p>"I understood what you were saying about the yearbook committee ...but wasn't it your own decision to run for the student council?"</p>

<p>"Of course it was! It's also true that I want to make the school a better place! But, but..."</p>

<p>She said the end part so quietly that I couldn't hear it.<br />Shiraishi, who was looking slightly embarrassed with blushed cheeks as she as she fiddled with her fingers, looked up and asked.</p>

<p>"Y-you won't laugh, right?"</p>

<p>"Heh"</p>

<p>"That's the part where you have don't have to laugh!"</p>

<p>"How am I supposed to know if I have to laugh or not if I don't even know what it is?"</p>


<p>Who do you think I am?<br />I'm not so nice as to give you the answer you expect, little girl.</p>

<p>I'm not sure if she understood my intentions or not, but Shiraishi muttered, "That's true." and then moved her face closer to mine, concerned about her surroundings.</p>
<p>As if she didn't want anyone else to hear, she closed the distance like a small child who wants to talk in private.</p>
<p>Just right in front of my eyes.</p>

<p>When her breath hit my face and she was just about to mutter something, right at that moment,,,</p>
<p>"It" passed between me and Shiraishi.</p>

<p>The wooden desk creaked and a roar echoed throughout the room.<br />There was a slight pain in my nose, but Shiraishi was uninjured.</p>

<p>She just sat with her eyes wide open, stunned.</p>

<p>Of course she was, and so was I.<br />I don't know what happened.</p>

<p>All I know is that something passed between us at a really high speed from a blind spot. But I soon found out...</p>
<p>It was the voices of the two girls whispering in my ear...</p>

<p>"It looks like you're having fun, Shinra-kun."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"It looks like you're having fun, Minato-kun."</p>

<p>The word "demon" flashed through my mind.<br />A figure that seemed to be at the end of its life was standing beside me.</p>

<p>"Ah, no, this..."</p>

<p>"Hiiiik!! Demon!?"</p>

<p>""What?""</p>

<p>"I-I-I'm sorry!"</p>

<p>It was Shizuku's and Kirasaka's hands that intervened between me and Shiraishi.</p>
<p>They should have been just hands, but they were looking like katanas.</p>

<p>They were gleaming with deadly energy that could have decapitated me at any moment. In fact, it was grazing my nose right now.</p>
<p> </p>

<p>But then again, even if Shiraishi were to be honest, there are some things she couldn't say.</p>
<p>No, ...I couldn't help it since my mind was also distracted for a second.</p>

<p>The two who were called demons looked at her with anger that you was not normally imaginable.</p>
<p>Shiraishi, completely frightened and shrinking, cowered to hide under the table.</p>


<p>They turned their gaze from Shiraishi to me and brought their hands back down. They grabbed me by the sides and dragged me towards the exit.</p>


<p>"Why would you just buy a drink and talk to that girl?"</p>

<p>"You can never be too careful."</p>

<p>"You seem to have misunderstood something ...I was just checking the situation."</p>

<p>I tried to explain, but they didn't seem to listen and continued asking their questions. Shiraishi, who was still hiding under the table, seemed to have something to say.</p>

<p>"We'll continue rest tomorrow at ...around three o'clock in the coffee shop in front of the station."</p>

<p>"Yeah, ...no, it's not like I'm going to go on a date with someone I've just met..."</p>

<p>"No, our conversation hasn't finished yet."</p>

<p>Even though I said that, it seemed weird to end the conversation up like that, and also, I won't be able to sleep because of my curiosity.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>At Shiraishi's comment, I felt a dull pain in both of my arms that had been grabbed, and I turned my gaze to look up, but the two of them walked with straight faces as if nothing had happened.</p>

<p>"A-And should I wear plain clothes!?"</p>

<p>"Rely on your notepad."</p>

<p>Isn't that the reason she's making the 'conversation notebook'?</p>
<p>I'd like to see it in action.</p>

<p>The last thing I saw was a clumsy kouhai hitting her head while trying to get up from under the table.</p>
