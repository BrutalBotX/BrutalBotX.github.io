<h1 style="text-align: center">#307</h1>
<hr />
<p> </p>
<p>“Nii-san, are you feeling tired?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As I took a breather in the living room, sweating, my sister Kaede spoke up.</p>
<p>I didn’t think I was doing anything out of the ordinary, but perhaps I had let out a sigh without realizing it.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As Kaede peered at my face from the side, I lightly pushed her head away and shifted slightly to the side.</p>
<p>Sitting in the space I cleared, Kaede’s feet tapped lightly as she waited for a response.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“It’s not that I’m tired, just thinking about something.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Is it something I can help with?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I replied, my cheeks softening as I say my sister tilting her head in a cute way asking if she could help.</p>
<p>I smiled as I pondered. While her high school was a girls’ school, she might know the common characters among those who had experience with video production.</p>
<p>As a reference, I showed her a video taken directly but edited with recorded footage.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Just as a reference, but can simple editing like putting clips together and cutting be done easily?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Video editing…?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Kaede leaned forward in front of my face to peer at the screen of my phone.</p>
<p>It wouldn’t be as exclusive an environment as it used to be, I supposed.</p>
<p>While I couldn’t do it, it wouldn’t be surprising if some students could, even around here.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>After thoroughly checking the video, Kaede turned her face towards me.</p>
<p>With glossy lips, maybe coated with lipstick, her big eyes probably reflected mostly my face in this moment.</p>
<p>Hino-kun would probably find this proximity startling…</p>
<p>I wouldn’t let him get this close as long as my eyes are black, though. &lt;BBX: As long as he’s alive&gt;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Simply putting clips together and cutting them, I can do, but if you want elaborate effects, it might need someone with experience, or else it might come off as uninspiring.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Kaede’s straightforward comment, considering that the shown video was something meant to bid farewell to the seniors, was evident.</p>
<p>In fact, even if I were to attempt video editing hastily, would it truly move people’s hearts?</p>
<p>I’m not confident about videos received from current students.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Perhaps, it’s ideal to have someone who has experience and is familiar with it, not just once or twice but regularly…</p>
<p>Finding that person would be the most challenging part.</p>
<p>After hearing Kaede’s response, I crossed my arms and looked up at the ceiling, lost in thought. Kaede seemed to sense my intentions and let out a thoughtful <em>Hmm</em> before speaking.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Doesn’t your school have something like a drama club or a film study group?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“We have a drama club, of course, but there’s no film-related group… It’s strictly drama, not video production.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I wondered if drama clubs nowadays also post videos. Negative opinions dominated my mind, likely stemming from preconceptions.</p>
<p>However, I’ll take it positively that my sister and I arrived at similar conclusions. It doesn’t mean I’m making the wrong choice. It would be foolish to seek answers and then give up thinking altogether.</p>
<p>I may not be the perfect older brother, but I want to be reliable. That’s why I shouldn’t rely entirely on my sister for all the answers.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Thanks, that was helpful.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I said, stroking Kaede’s hair as I rose from the sofa.</p>
<p>It was about time for Mom to finish preparing dinner. I picked up my jacket and headed to the front door.</p>
<p>Sitting and stewing won’t help me think clearly. I need to change my surroundings with a short walk.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Nii-san?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I’m going for a short walk… I’ll be back before dinner.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I reassured Kaede, who looked concerned, before leaving the house alone.</p>
<p>It was already dark outside, and the sounds of conversation drifted from neighbouring homes.</p>
<p>Lights were on in Shizuku’s house across the street, and her room on the second floor was already illuminated.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>What could she be doing now?</p>
<p>Perhaps she’s finishing her assignments and taking a breather. I briefly halted my steps, then continued in the opposite direction of the school.</p>
<p>How to find people to participate in the farewell party activities… Gathering people by grade or class… No, that wouldn’t be efficient.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Two days have already passed.</p>
<p>With only 12 days left, gathering videos during homeroom, lunch break, and after school, along with gathering people, would still not be enough time.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I’ll have to narrow down the options and contact specific individuals.</p>
<p>That seems to be the only way forward.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As I walked through the residential area and onto the busy street lined with eateries and shops, the light spilled out onto the pavement from the shops on the side. On the opposite sidewalk, students in different uniforms were chatting happily while holding McDonald’s bags.</p>
<p>Sometimes, I feel like indulging in fast food. But since I left my wallet at home today, I’ll have to resist the temptation. Besides, Mom is preparing dinner at home. If I say I’m not hungry because I snacked, she’ll sulk and won’t cook for two days.</p>
<p>Probably, tomorrow’s breakfast will be cornflakes.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Enduring the urge to eat out, I aimlessly wandered from crowded places to side streets and along the riverside.</p>
<p>Today, I should make sure to register on the SNS platforms popular among students. Then, I’ll search using the names of students who rank high in the school hierarchy.</p>
<p>However, those students are usually not inclined toward creative activities.</p>
<p>They live for the moment, enjoying the present to the fullest. Therefore, they’re often distant from activities without clear goals, like creating their own thoughts and paths.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Hence, students who spend their time a step or two away from the top may be more likely to fit the bill. The problem is, I don’t know the names of such students. Prominent students’ faces and names naturally come to mind, but the ones around them often remains unknown.</p>
<p>If I had acquaintances nearby, I could connect through them. Unfortunately, I only know the top or bottom tiers, including myself.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Just then, I remembered. Recently, I’ve started befriending Miyashita, who used to be in the group of a student named Nakajima or Nakayama,</p>
<p>I think. She seemed to be bossed around by her. Since she became honest about her feelings during the school trip, I haven’t seen them talking in class, so I forgot about it.</p>
<p>Miyashita might surprisingly know people with similar interests.</p>
<p>Being second or third rank often means having a wider network than the top. With tomorrow’s main goal set, I need to think of an excuse if I do find someone.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>People tend to prefer negative thoughts, even when they know they should do good. Whether it’s self-sacrifice or hypocrisy, I need to present reasons, benefits, and significance for participating, even if it’s under the guise of a noble cause.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Deep in thought, I lost track of time and found myself sitting alone by the riverside. The sound of the flowing river didn’t reach my ears, and my gaze was fixed on a point on the water’s surface. I didn’t feel the biting cold anymore, so deeply engrossed in my thoughts that I couldn’t even remember the time to head home.</p>
<p>However, my consciousness was abruptly brought back to reality by the sound of footsteps crunching on gravel from my left.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“At this time of year, if you sit down like this wearing just a hoodie, you’ll catch a cold, Minato-kun.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Shizuku…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The moonlight illuminated Shizuku’s black hair as it swayed, and she smiled and approached me. From her bag, she took out a scarf and wrapped it around my neck.</p>
<p>The faintly different scent told me she hadn’t been out of the bath for long.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Why are you here?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“That should be my line. I happened to see Minato-kun leaving the house and decided to follow. Not only your friend’s house, convenience store, and even the school were in the opposite direction.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Are you indirectly saying I don’t have many friends?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>If so, she must be preparing herself to jab at me sharply, right?</p>
<p>Considering I’m troubled by my lack of connections, I need her words to be said with a resolve.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>However, upon hearing my response, Shizuku burst into laughter joyfully.</p>
<p>Placing her hand elegantly in front of her mouth, I couldn’t help but sigh at her refined demeanour.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“If you can say such sarcastic things, it seems like there’s nothing for you to be down about.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>With a hint of relief in her voice, she took a seat next to me on the left. Without minding if her clothes got dirty from the sand, she settled in the space where our arms almost touched, then turned to face me.</p>
<p>In the moonlight, her face looked more mature than usual, and from an outsider’s perspective, the distance between us felt like that of lovers about to kiss, making me instinctively look away.</p>
<p>I wonder why she can face me with such composure at this distance.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Will you talk to me? About what’s troubling you, Minato-kun.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>While still facing me, she asked. Her expression showed she believed I would talk, and since she had come all the way here to follow up, it wouldn’t be acceptable for me not to say anything.</p>
<p>In that moment, I looked up at the night sky, contemplating how to express my feelings to her.</p>
<p> </p>
