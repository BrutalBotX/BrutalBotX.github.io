<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#120</h1>
<p> </p>
<hr />
<p>President was wearing a white swimsuit.</p>
<p>The curves of her body were clearly visible, and her figure was expressed in all its glory.</p>

<p>That was the reason I had trouble to look directly at her.</p>
<p>Then, president answered my question with an amused laugh.</p>

<p>"Well, since there are so many beautiful people here, I thought I should show some sort of uniqueness, or I'll be buried."</p>

<p>She's not buried ...on the contrary, she's floating.</p>
<p>Just like a floater.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Koizumi's face turned red from being so conspicuous, and Yuuto looked as if trying to find where to look.</p>
<p>And as for Hino-kun, he was shedding tears of joy as he stared not at the president but at Kaede, who was standing behind her.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Hino-kun, he was left alone for the time being, and as for the president, she was busy sticking out her chest cared about nothing else.</p>

<p>And she did it with such majesty that it seemed almost gallant.</p>

<p>"The first thing I thought of was a swimsuit! Well, it could have been a normal school swimsuit or something."</p>

<p>Why didn't she choose it then?</p>
<p>President basically gets the right answer to everything, but I think she also has some strange tendencies here and there.</p>

<p>For example, she allowed people like me and Hino-kun to join the student council.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Did the president notice that she stood out? She put on a white hoodie and turned around quickly.</p>

<p>There were still four girls standing there.</p>

<p>Miura chose the swimsuit prescribed by the school, Kaede chose a one-piece, and Shizuku and Kirasaka chose bikini swimsuits.</p>

<p>The colors of each four were different, Kaede was orange, Shizuku was light blue, and Kirasaka was black.</p>
<p>...Well as for Miura's it was dark blue since it was ...school specified.</p>

<p>I'm sure there would be better ways to describe them, but it was difficult for me hadn't attended these kinds of events before.</p>
<p>Of course, I've never been to a swimming pool with a girl in my life.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The only time I've been to the swimming pool was when I was in elementary school with my parents and Kaede.</p>
<p>In this situation where my knowledge and experiences were not applicable, my incompetence was revealed.</p>

<p>The only thing that I could understand is that there are many different kinds of swimsuits available these days...</p>
<p>After comparing the four swimsuits, I returned my eyes to president.</p>
<p>The nine of us, including the girls, started the cleaning that the boys had been working on earlier.</p>

<p> </p>
<p>Miura used the hose to wash off the detergent that had been sprayed on the floor.</p>
<p>At the same time, we started scrubbing off the dirt with the scrubbers.</p>

<p>"What do you think, Shinra-kun?"</p>

<p>As I was mindlessly scrubbing away the pool, Kirasaka came beside me and asked.</p>
<p>Kirasaka brushed away her short black hair and showed me her swimsuit like a model.</p>

<p>"...It's kind of flashy."</p>

<p>It's not about the color, it's the area of the fabric!</p>
<p>Black is not a flashy color, but her pure white bare skin is bad for the eyes in general.</p>

<p>When I make eye contact with her more than usual, Kirasaka raises the corner of her mouth with a grin.</p>
<p>It looks like she's having fun ...that's a relief.</p>

<p>I had to change my location to get away from Kirasaka, who was getting closer and closer, as if she had found something fun to play with.</p>
<p>However, I don't think I have anywhere to escape.</p>

<p>When I was pushed to the edge, another female student offered a helping hand.</p>


<p>"Kirasaka-san! You're getting too close to Minato-kun! Please don't skip your work."</p>

<p>"I'm not skipping work, I'm just asking for Shinra-kun's opinion."</p>

<p>It was Shizuku who stepped in between me and Kirasaka.</p>
<p>She turned her back towards me and held out her hands as if she was protecting me from something bad.</p>

<p>"I'll be the first to get his impressions, so you'll have to wait.</p>

<p>"I'll be the first, you wait."</p>

<p>Is my first opinion such a thing that they would have to fight over?</p>
<p>However, Shizuku looked that she wouldn't move aside, as if there was something she could not compromise.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Kirasaka was also stubborn, she also looked like she would not give in no matter what, so it naturally developed into a fierce scuffle.</p>


<p>I tiptoed away behind them and returned to where I had been and resumed my cleaning.</p>
<p>Koizumi and Yuuto are both working on cleaning nearby.</p>

<p>Miura ...is it just sprinkling water?</p>
<p>President gives instructions from the watchtower.</p>

<p>And my sister Kaede was busy moving around and supporting everyone.</p>
<p>Yeah, her brother likes that about her.</p>

<p>However, I would like to say a word to the man who is still shedding tears behind me.</p>
<p>...do your job</p>

<p>The reason Hino-kun joined the student council is because there are not enough male students to do the heavy work.</p>
<p>And yet, here he is.</p>
<p> </p>

<p>Maybe president noticed the low workforce and decided to get off her pedestal, then called in Hino-kun and Kaede.</p>

<p>"Hino, and Kaede-chan, can you come here?"</p>

<p>"Yes, I'll be there?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"I-I'm sorry! I'll also help."</p>

<p>After adding a word to follow, president started talking to them.</p>
<p>I didn't stop moving my hands, I just listened.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"I think I look better."</p>

<p>"Minato-kun prefers my swimsuit!"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Somewhere two people were making a lot of noise, and a lot of unnecessary information was coming out, but I should probably just ignore it.</p>
