<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#177</h1>

<hr>
<p>It was nostalgic.</p>
<p>In the morning, I left the house to part ways with Kaede on the way and walked to school alone.</p>
<p>After arriving in the classroom, I spent some time listening to music before heading continuing with the classes.</p>
<p>During lunch break, I ate Kaede's special bento in a secluded place, and then went to my afternoon class.</p>
<p>There wasn't anything to do in the student council, and when I heard the bell that marks the end of classes, I headed back home.</p>

<p>This should have been my normal routine just a short while ago, but I feel uncomfortable. It is evident that I had begun to get used to the noisy daily routine.</p>



<p>Most of the places I walk, scenes from my past come to mind as memories.</p>
<p>I began to take different paths to avoid them.</p>

<p>Human memories are not something that can be erased at will.<br>Even though I knew it was a useless action, but unnecessary thoughts were only stressful for me now.</p>


<p>In a park by the river, elementary school children were playing baseball.<br>Their faces were bright.</p>

<p>It is for the best.<br>One by one, as they approach adulthood, they will lose their innocence.<span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p>Their minds become more mature, they look around them, and they get used to blending in with the crowd.</p>
<p>When I look at the kids in front of me, I even wonder if we really are growing up.</p>

<p>Abandoning something, abandoning our innocence, is that what it means to grow up?</p>
<p>Isn't it just dyeing a blank canvas black, but not really growing up in any way?</p>

<p>Well, such mentality is useless.</p>
<p>As I resumed my halted steps, a pair of boys and a girl ran in front of me and then passed by.</p>
<p>They did not look alike in appearance, so I wondered if they were childhood friends.</p>

<p>As I turned and looked away from them, I caught sight of a girl in the same uniform as me.</p>

<p>"It brings back memories... we used to play like that too."</p>

<p>"...I think I was just being dragged around."</p>

<p>Looking in the same direction as me, Shizuku told me nostalgically.</p>

<p>For a moment, I wondered why she was walking this way, but I didn't need to think about the answer to that obvious question.</p>
<p>My house is right across the street to hers, and when I'm not walking through my normal path, my route home is constricted.</p>
<p>She must have guessed that I was going home from this path, even though it was a bit of a detour.</p>

<p>I stood there until the boys were completely out of sight, and then I looked forward.</p>
<p>Shizuku walked next to me, shoulder to shoulder, at the same pace.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>We looked at each other and at the scenery around us several times, but never spoke a word.</p>
<p>I wonder how long it's been since I've walked with her this way.</p>

<p>Shizuku just smiled at me.<br>As if to say, no words are needed.</p>

<p>We continued along the river, and just before entering the residential area, we stopped in front of an old house.</p>
<p>The house is rusted in places and there is no sign of it now, but it used to be a candy store.</p>

<p>That store, run by an elderly couple, was not exactly prosperous.<br>However, they said they enjoyed seeing the kids, so they stayed in business for decades until we were in junior high school.</p>

<p>But as the town's population dwindled and the number of children began to decrease, the store, which had a bit of history, closed its doors a few years ago.</p>
<p>On the last day of business, Shizuku, Kaede, and I visited the store, which remains strongly in my memory.</p>

<p>I remember that I bought a lot of sweets when I came to this store, even though I usually don't waste money.</p>

<p>I spent money less often in the past than I do now so my savings revolved around this shop.</p>

<p>Rather, I was even running the whole damn finances of this candy store.<br>...I'm kidding, of course.</p>

<p>The allowance of a child isn't much.<br>Shizuku let out a chuckle at my words, and her eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"But the things you bought were always things that Kaede and I liked. Were you thinking of giving them if we asked for them?"</p>

<p>"...Don't remember."</p>

<p>I don't want you to revisit my petty young mind.<br>Well, maybe I wanted to show a brother's kindness and a childhood friend how cool I was, even though I was a little boy back then, too.</p>
<p>
</p><p>Now it's a long time ago, a forgotten past.</p>


<p>Did she come here to reminisce?<br>No, of course not.</p>

<p>"You're mad, right...?"</p>

<p>"What..."</p>

<p>"The sports festival ...I invited you, but I couldn't say anything."</p>

<p>You're concerned about that?<br>I have told you before that I don't get angry about such things.</p>
<p>However, it might have been stuck deep in her chest like a fish bone.</p>

<p>"You can't blame yourself for not being able to say in that situation ...didn't I say I didn't care the last time I was there...?"</p>

<p>"But, ...in the end, I wasn't any different from before."</p>

<p>"I don't care what people think anymore" is what she said sometime ago.</p>

<p>This was the trigger for her own decision to change, and it was an issue that she had always held in her heart.</p>

<p>However, if it were that easy to change, the world would be a better place and there would be less trouble.</p>
<p>It takes time for people to change.</p>

<p>It is challenging to take the first step, and even then there's is a difficult path ahead.<br>A path that I hate and want to go back to.</p>

<p>She and I were just approaching the first of these.<br>She and I turned back.</p>

<p>I can't say that it was a bad choice for me.<br>However, it was a bad choice for Shizuku, and she herself seemed to regret it.</p>

<p>"You know how they say you should cherish your high school friends... so it's fine for you to think it's better that your classmates don't have a bad image of you."</p>

<p>"...Lies are told in such a way that the other person can't understand. ...I told you, didn't I? Minato-kun is a person who can't lie."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Shizuku shook her head while saying those words.</p>
<p>It may seem like she's repeating herself, but the time is a scary thing.<br>The more time you have, the more likely you are to come up with an answer that is close to what the other person is thinking.</p>

<p>I turned my head to the side to look away from her self-harming smile.<br>We continued on our way away from the old building and entered a familiar neighborhood.</p>

<p>We go through the main street and pass by a park that we often see.<br>We stopped in front of the road that separated the Shinra and Kanazaki families and stood facing each other.</p>

<p>"The sports festival is getting that way because of me, but I'm still going to give you my answer."</p>

<p>"...I don't think you need to be concerned about that."</p>

<p>Her eyes seemed to have an answer already.<br>I let out a slight breath of relief at those words and stepped onto the Shinra household.</p>
<p>
</p><p>The moment I unlocked the front door and pulled open the door, Shizuku spoke.</p>

<p>"Minato-kun! ...I'm not going to be in an event with anyone other than you."</p>

<p>I wonder what the girl who turned around and smiled at me was thinking when she spoke those words.</p>
<p>It will be a little while before we know the answer to that question.</p>
