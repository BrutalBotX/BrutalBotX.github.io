<h1 style="text-align: center">#259</h1>
<hr />
<p>Yuuto and the male students moved to the next floor, and Nakayama followed after finishing the conversation with Miyashita.</p>
<p>While talking to Miyashita, who was changing places with Shizuku, I looked up at Kirasaka, who was still looking up at the huge water tank at the end.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>She raised her white, slender hand toward the water tank and looked up with meaningful eyes.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"What's wrong..."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I stopped walking and asked her as I turned around.</p>
<p>Kirasaka lowered her hand in response to my voice and smiled while putting her hands behind her back.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"No, I just thought it was similar."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Similar, where?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Is it a riddle or something?</p>
<p>If you say that humans and fish are similar, it’ll endanger my life.</p>
<p>I feel like there is no common ground, but Kirasaka talked as if she was inflicting self-harm.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"I wonder if it’s being kept inside a cage called an aquarium …just like an ornament."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Then, what she wants to say is that she is also treated carefully for people to watch like fish in an aquarium.</p>
<p>Inside the cage of the classroom, she was being treated like something that people couldn’t come close to.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"If it's a self-deprecating joke, keep it moderation..."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Is that an advice? Or are you worried?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>At that, Kirasaka retorted.</p>
<p>She had her usual evil smile on, but it looked like she was searching for something.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Should I say that she is pretending to be usual?</p>
<p>Kirasaka quietly laughed at me who choked on words.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"I’m just kidding." With that, she walked up next to me and changed the direction of the conversation.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"To tell you the truth, I don't like when Shinra-kun thinks about other people while traveling."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"You really mean it..."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I also smiled wryly at Kirasaka, who said so easily without even trying to sugarcoat it even once.</p>
<p>Certainly, I am aware that I am doing meaningless things on my trip because of my meddling.</p>
<p>So, there is no room to say anything when she says it directly in words.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Even if it's a group action at school, how fun would it be to be by your side and talk to the usual relaxed you.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The eyes that stood next to me and looked up were so clear that they couldn't be seen as jokes.</p>
<p>I felt that she was just saying what she thought in her heart.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I’m sure, she thinks the same…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>She spoke as she looked ahead, where Shizuku stood.</p>
<p>Looking at Miyashita’s back, who is talking to someone, a small sense of guilt swirls in my chest.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Feeling guilty is the sign that I also feel inferior to her.</p>
<p>I prioritize the request of a female student who I don’t know over the school trip that she were looking forward to.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>From the perspective of someone who has been looking forward to doing things together for a long time, it is inevitable to feel angry.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“But―――”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Even so… there is a part of me that wants you to finish what you want to do now.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>But, I have something I want to do, something I want to settle.</p>
<p>What Kirasaka spoke overwrote what I was about to speak.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As if she knew what words to answer with.</p>
<p>Unlike the sharp gaze she usually gives male students, her eyes are gentle and warm.</p>
<p>The same eyes as Kaede and Shizuku, who understood me.</p>
<p>However, those eyes soon disappeared as she lowered her eyelids.</p>
<p>The next words that came flying were her usual mischievous voice.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“But be prepared after the school trip is over.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Watching her back as she grabs me and pulls me along with an impudent smile, I think she’s still someone who I don’t know what she’s thinking.</p>
<p>While having such a conversation, Miyashita and Shizuku were having a dialogue face to face.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Kanazaki-san isn’t dating Ogiwara-kun, is she?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“No, no, it’s a completely unfounded rumour.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shizuku waved her hand and asserted that it’s different, causing Miyashita to breathe out.</p>
<p>She seemed relieved, bit she still had doubts.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Then, is it okay if Ogiwara-kun ends up dating another girl?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“You don’t need my permission… and sometimes extra consideration only gets in the way.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>With these few words, Shizuku cut off the conversation and smiled broadly.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It was a statement that was so strong that it was hard to imagine coming from her usual gentle and ladylike nature.</p>
<p>It was also a moment when classmates realized that Shizuku Kanazaki, whom she had seen, was really only a very small part of who she was.</p>
