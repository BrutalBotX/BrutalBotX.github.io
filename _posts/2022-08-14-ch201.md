<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#201</h1>
<p></p>
<hr>
<p>The Cherry Blossom season has arrived.</p>
<h6 class="zw-paragraph heading0">&lt;TL: Sakura = Cherry blossom&gt;</h6>
<p>The cherry blossoms no longer flutter, and the leaves on the trees have begun to turn red as if to signal the end of summer season.</p>
<p>The heat that envelops my body is replaced by a pleasant cooler breeze.</p>

<p>The hottest event awaits the students at the Sakura Academy before the end of the summer.</p>
<p>An athletic festival, free from daily studies, and a school-wide celebration that overflows with the individuality of the students.</p>

<p>The students' awareness of the Student Council elections is now in the past, and their focus has shifted to the biggest event that is just around the corner.</p>
<p>The student council, likewise, is about to enter its busiest time of the year.</p>
<p>
</p>
<p>A season that has the potential to alter the various expectations and anxieties greatly in their studies, club activities, love, youth, and future of the student is just right ahead...</p>
<p>The School of Cherry Blossoms has to undergo a brief change into the School of Festivals.</p>

<p>It is the students who will create this change.</p>
<p>They think independently, make suggestions, and take action.</p>
<p>In a school where students are left to manage their own initiative, it is more difficult than it sounds.</p>

<p>If we fail to prepare for this, the greatest celebration will turn into the greatest disgrace.</p>
<p>The question is the leadership of those who stand at the top of the organization, the trust of the students, and flexible thinking.</p>

<p>The challenges that will come are many.<br>Rise of unexpected people and the troubles that accompany them.</p>
<p>We will have to prepare our own ways to solve the problems as much as we can expect.</p>
<p>If we are juniors who only rely on others, that person will not be able to leave with peace of mind.</p>

<p>What I recall between my steps toward the student council office are the words that were said to me when I joined the student council.</p>
<p>I'm not confident that I've ever been able to answer that expectation so I have to judge calmly and objectively...</p>

<p>This is the first and last time.</p>
<p>I only have a short time period, this Sakura Festival, in which I can act for that very purpose at the same place.</p>
<p>To be recognized by that person.</p>
<p>I want to be recognized by someone who has no admiration, no love interest, just someone who genuinely represents the ideals of the people around her.</p>

<p>My feelings might be similar to when I confronted my friend and competed with him.</p>
<p>Perhaps I just want to satisfy myself.</p>

<p>Student council was not the place where I was called because of her personal feelings, but it was the place where she recognized me, Shinra Minato.</p>
<p>Maybe I just want to get the final word on a question that I have been asking myself.</p>


<p>The executive committee is supposed to be led by a junior who is seemingly dependable but is also extremely prone to unexpected developments.</p>
<p>Although she has not expressed it in words, she has a dependable friend who will support her.</p>

<p>Worrying might turn out to be a groundless fear.<br>She should concentrate on her own position.</p>
<p></p>
<p>As assistant to the student body president, it is my job to support her in her task.</p>
<p>I let out a deep breath as if to re-motivate myself in front of a room in a corner of the school, and then put my hand on the heavy iron door.</p>

<p>I looked at the figure of the senior who welcomed me with a smile.<br>I wonder what the person with that smile is thinking right now.</p>

<p>Is it anxiety or anticipation for the big event that is just around the corner...?</p>
<p>With these questions in my mind, my day as a student council member begins again today.</p>
<p>
</p>


<p>Before we choose the next generation of students and pass the baton to them, we will learn something.</p>
<p>We will learn about our predecessor, who has been called the best of her generation.</p>

<p>We will see the scene created by her existence, which until now we have turned our eyes away from as someone who lives in a different world from our own.</p>

<p>Let the Sakura Festival begin!</p>
