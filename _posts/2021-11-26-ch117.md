<h1 style="text-align:center;">CH#117</h1>
<p></p>
<hr>
<p>Summer has begun.</p>
<p>&nbsp;It's July, and the campus of Sakurano-oka Academy is somewhat in a state of excitement.</p>

<p>　It wasn't because of the new school term or the fresh atmosphere.</p>
<p>　Needless to say, I already knew the answer.</p>

<p>　It was because the summer vacation and the summer festival, the biggest event in the school, were just around the corner.</p>
<p>　The biggest event of the year is approaching, and that's why the students are so excited.</p>

<p>　There were no exceptions around me, and no matter where I went, the topic of conversation was the same.</p>
<p>　Plans for summer vacation trips, promises to go to summer festivals with their girlfriends.</p>

<p>　Students who had not yet found a girlfriend were clenching their fists and rubbing shoulders with similar friends, eager to make one by this time.</p>

<p>　I'm sorry, but even if you are determined to do so, you can't do something impossible....</p>
<p>　I'm sure that after summer vacation, winter vacation, school trips, Valentine's Day, cultural festivals, and other big events, people will say similar things.</p>

<p>　They say things and don't act on them.</p>
<p>　I feel like this is a typical pattern of modern students.</p>
<p>　Be it at class, at home, and in the student council.</p>
<p></p>
<p>　One particular day, when I was starting to get tired of the topics, I was assigned a task for the after-school student council activities.</p>

<p>"We're going to have a pool cleanup this weekend."</p>

<p>　As soon as the meeting started, the president told the board members.</p>
<p>　I opened my mouth wide and almost let out a sigh.</p>

<p>　The moment I heard the president's words, I had a hard time understanding what she was talking about because all I could think of was a question mark.</p>
<p>　Why would the student council be cleaning the pool on a holiday?</p>

<p>　Why would the student council be cleaning the pool on a holiday when there was already an indoor pool used by the swimming club?</p>
<p>　In addition, the boys have nothing to do with it... This may be a bit of a misrepresentation, but the boys don't have swimming classes.</p>

<p>　Only female students have swimming as an elective in their physical education classes.</p>
<p>　That's why I thought it had nothing to do with me, so I didn't understand the meaning of President's words.</p>

<p>It is natural that there is a pool cleaning as a preparation for class at this time of the summer.</p>
<p>　I thought that the cleaning would be done by a contractor, so I ignored it.</p>

<p>　I had no idea that it was a job for the student council...</p>
<p>　It was only when the president gave me a cold stare that I realized that all the other board members knew about it.</p>

<p>"I'm sure you're not going to say that you didn't hear what I said last week... are you, Shinra?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>"..."</p>

<p>　The silence meant exactly what President said.</p>
<p>
</p><p>　That's strange.</p>

<p>　In my memory, I thought that last week's student council activity was only about the summer events.</p>
<p>　...Or, perhaps it was because it was the day after I was forcibly taken to the hot springs by Kirasaka and I was in a daze.</p>

<p>　In other words, it was my lack of concentration and my fault.</p>

<p>"It cannot be helped, I'll explain it again."</p>

<p>"Please do."</p>

<p>　I honestly said a few words of apology and listened to the explanation.</p>
<p>　The other board members knew what was going on, but they were listening attentively.</p>



<p>"Every year, our school holds a swimming class for female students. If it was a normal year, there wouldn't have been any problems, but this year the pool is undergoing renovation, as you know."</p>

<p>"The contractor was in the school this afternoon, wasn't he?"</p>

<p>　Koizumi said, probably checking the record of the contractor's entry into the school.</p>
<p>　It's true that we've been passing people in work clothes for the past few days.</p>



<p>So we decided to use the outdoor pool this year, and the student council will take over the cleaning.</p>

<p>"...what a troublesome role."</p>

<p>　Why on earth would this job be given to us?</p>
<p>　President will not refuse to do so if asked, but she already has a lot of work to do on a regular basis.</p>

<p>　This job may have been part of it, but if it was a sudden request, I would like to complain to the teacher who asked her to do it.</p>



<p>"The more people you have, the better. It would be great if you could ask your friends to help out."</p>

<p>That's true... The outdoor pool is big, so we'll need more people.</p>

<p>　When Miura takes out a diagram of the outdoor pool, she paints the order in which the pool needs to be cleaned in detail in different colors.</p>
<p>Even if you make a rough estimate, five or six people other than the student organization are unlikely to end it in a day without help.</p>

<p>　Each member of the board would probably have to call out to their friends to get people to help.</p>
<p>　Unfortunately, don't expect me to do it.</p>

<p>　After all, the number of people I have to talk to is exceptionally small.</p>
<p>　And since some of them would definitely refuse, it would be worse to get their hopes up.</p>

<p>　President turned to me and told me what she was thinking.</p>

<p>"Shinra should also invite Kanzaki and Rei."</p>

<p>"...Who's going to control those rampaging horses?"</p>

<p>　President laughed at the names of two of the few people I could talk to.</p>
<p>　It is true that I can invite both Shizuku and Kirasaka.</p>

<p>　I'm sure that Shizuku will be able to help us.</p>
<p>　However, the story will be completely different when it comes to including Kirasaka.</p>

<p>　I can't imagine that it will end up without any problems.</p>
<p>　If someone doesn't act as a mediator, the whole place will be surrounded in a tense atmosphere.</p>

<p>　President answered my question as if it were obvious.</p>

<p>"It is, of course, within the scope of Shinra's work."</p>

<p>　...It is... isn't it?</p>
<p>　The moment President mentioned the two names, I had a somewhat troublesome feeling.</p>

<p>　But whom President spoke of next was a person I didn't think of, at least not yet.</p>

<p>"And if it's okay, isn't it an option to invite your sister as well?"</p>

<p>"Kaede?"</p>

<p>　I think Kaede had said she would be free this Saturday.</p>
<p>　Normally, I would ask Kaede here, and if she agrees, she can join us as one of the helpers...</p>

<p>　I think that's too much of a problem.</p>
<p>　There is a junior in front of me who worships Kaede as a goddess.</p>

<p>　As you look at him, his eyes are shining and he is nodding his head in approval, and he looks at me expectantly.</p>

<p>　To be honest, I'm against letting Kaede meet this guy.</p>
<p>　Sure, it was only a photo, but you never know what he's up to.</p>

<p>　There is a possibility that he will say or do something even more troublesome than Kirasaka.</p>
<p>　When I couldn't agree with President's proposal, she proceeded with the meeting.</p>

<p>"If there are any students who are willing to help, please let me know by the Friday before. We'll dismiss early today."</p>

<p>　There was no meeting at all.</p>
<p>　There was no meeting, only a brief reminder, but today's student council meeting was over.</p>

<p>　In the end, the president of the student council left the room after saying, "Please try talking to your sister," leaving only me and Hino-kun in the room.</p>

<p>"Please, big brother!"</p>

<p>"Who's your brother?"</p>

<p>　I'm not going to marry off my sister.</p>
<p>　I've had a selfish plan since elementary school to have her take care of me at least until I can stand on my own.</p>
