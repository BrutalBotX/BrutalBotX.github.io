<p>&lt;TL&gt; : A bit late, nonetheless its here. I may not be able to do the usual number of chapters while being here, I'll try to aim for 2 chapters a week or so.</p>
<hr>
<p>This chapter marks the end of the chapter on student council issues.<br>The second term arc will follow.</p>

<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span></p>
<p>Dear Father.</p>
<p>How are you spending this damn hot summer?</p>

<p>In Japan, the sun is still shining as usual, the hot asphalt in the distant horizon is flickering like that a mirage.</p>
<p>Why are Japanese summers so hot?</p>

<p>I have no answer to this question.</p>
<p>Thus, I am fine.</p>

<p>Kaede is fine too.</p>
<p></p>
<p>In this digital age, I don't know why he ordered me to contact him using with letters.</p>
<p>Did he want to make it a topic of conversation at the company that he and his son are exchanging letters?</p>
<p>I don't think I have anything special to write, so I hope this letter is sufficient.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Last but not least, please raise the amount of my monthly allowance not only of your daughter's, but also of your beloved son's.</p>

<p>P.S.</p>
<p>Recently, I could see that the defensive power of the back of your head has been declining, so I recommend an upgrade in your equipment i.e. a change of shampoo.</p>

<p>Shinra Minato.</p>


<p>"Is this how it's supposed to be...?</p>
<p>I mumbled as I stopped writing.<br>Appropriate phrases were listed on the thinly patterned paper prepared by Kaede.</p>

<p>But it's not because it's a hassle to begin it from scratch.</p>
<p>Definitely not...</p>

<p>A week has passed since the President's bold declaration.<br>In the meantime, I have finished digesting my summer vacation assignments and am finally ready to enjoy my summer vacation to the fullest, but my personal problems don't seem to decrease.</p>

<p>It is becoming unclear whether I will be able to finish my summer vacation easily and enjoyably, or not.</p>

<p>In the end, two things have changed.<br>The first is that the president has started to contact me on a regular basis.</p>

<p>The line of communication between me and president, which until now had only served as the basis of administrative reports, has evolved a few notches.<br>Well, the contents are very similar to ordinary daily conversations...</p>

<p>It was the best way to get used to keeping in touch with someone of the opposite sex.</p>
<p>For the first time, there was nothing for me to talk, so I only answered "That's right", "Wow", and "Understood".</p>
<p>I never thought that my lack of communication skills would be exposed in a situation like this. ...you failed me, Minato-kun.</p>


<p>And the second change is the change in Shinra family's circumstances.<br>It is the same two siblings as before, and although the parents are still absent, the liveliness has increased ...a little too much.</p>

<p>"...Checkmate."</p>

<p>While I was writing a letter to my father, Kirasaka, who was the only one with a higher and colder gaze than the other visitors, declared this as she placed the piece she had picked up from the board.</p>
<p>On the tabletop were spread out a shogi board and a chess board, wherever she had taken them out from.</p>

<p>Yuuto was playing Shogi and Shizuku was playing Chess.<br>Against the two, Kirasaka declared her victory with ease, as if it were a matter of course.</p>

<p>"...One more game."</p>

<p>"Damn, that's Kirasaka-san for you."</p>

<p>I will not question these three being in my house anymore.<br>But there's a person who is out of the ordinary.</p>

<p>The first-year student from Sakuragaoka Academy, Momiji Shiraishi, was present here.<br>She was not playing Shogi or Chess, but was standing still with both her hands and knees on the floor, like a horse.</p>

<p>Her arms were shaking and her legs felt unstable.<br>It was no wonder.</p>

<p>She has Kirasaka Rei sitting on her ...back.</p>

<p>"She's no match for her ...and the board there is all black, too."</p>

<p>"...Why am I the only one being punished...?"</p>

<p>"We can't allow the losers to complain ...this world is a place where the weak have no say."</p>

<p>Kirasaka looked down at Go (*Othello, whatever you call it), which is placed in front of Shiraishi, as if looking down at her.<br>There, the black colors are neatly lined up.</p>

<p>In this type of board game, if the ignorant people are going at each other seriously, the element of luck does not intervene.</p>
<p>Winning or losing depends entirely on individual skill.</p>

<p>Who was Kirasaka who was able to play against the top students of her grade by herself and win a crushing victory against them all?<br>Riding on the back of Shiraishi, she was indeed a queen.</p>


<p>What has happened in the week since then?</p>
<p>And why are we in the situation we are in now?</p>



<p>After returning home from the incident with the president, I was told by Kaede in a dissatisfied tone as soon as I returned home.</p>

<p>"Nii-san, a female kouhai came to visit you."</p>

<p>"Kouhai...?"</p>

<p>The only person who comes to my mind, who fitted this description, is Shiraishi.</p>

<p>"Ah, ...do what did she say?'</p>

<p>She pulls out a bottle of cold mineral water from the refrigerator and pours it down her throat in one gulp.<br>Kaede, with a frustrated expression that never seemed to clear up, just shook her head and said nothing.</p>

<p>Unusual...<br>Kaede is one to inform me of the visitor's requirements, even if they are a little vague.</p>

<p>The fact that she doesn't, means that either it was really only for a moment, or she has a reason for not talking.<br>Considering Shiraishi's personality and words and deeds, the latter is more likely.</p>

<p>It can't be helped that we all have good and bad chemistry.</p>
<p>If it had not been Shiraishi who visited me, I would have asked one or two more questions, but with her, there was no need.</p>

<p>In all probability, it would have been about the student council.<br>Just to be sure, I checked my phone to see if Yuuto had contacted me, but there was no notification.</p>
<p>She must have come to my house without consulting Yuuto.</p>


<p>I sat down on the sofa and let out a deep sigh containing various emotions.<br>For now, just for today, I would like to refrain from thinking further.</p>

<p>I am already over capacity.<br>I catch out of the corner of my eye Kaede sitting next to me, looking hesitant and in agony.</p>
<p>I'm sure my sister would like to ask me about today's events, not about the visitors, but she seems to be hesitating whenever she sees me.</p>

<p>"I know you want to ask me about today's ...matters, and I'll tell you as much as I can answer."</p>

<p>"O-Oh, I see. ...then---"</p>


<p>The other party was the student body president, Akane Hiiragi, and the president is not in love with me.</p>

<p>To put it simply, the word "match of conditions" is appropriate.<br>Kaede breathed a sigh of relief when she heard these words.</p>

<p>"Then, what about today's juniors?"</p>

<p>"That's the matter of the student council election ...and since I left the negotiations to Yuuto, she must have come to me for a response to that."</p>
<p>
</p><p>I'll call Yuuto later.<br>After answering Kaede's question shortly, I left the living room with a heavy heart.</p>

<p>In my quiet room, I lay on my bed and let my thoughts run wild.</p>

<p>"...I can't concentrate on my personal problems unless I solve the ones from the Student Council first."</p>

<p>I didn't say these words to anyone.<br>It was just for myself.</p>
<p>The room was supposed to be empty and silent.</p>

<p>"Well, ...I also think that the student council's problem is the first problem to be solved, since the deadline is fast approaching."</p>

<p>Yes, a similarly melancholy voice echoed in the corner of the room.<br>I turned my head, probably the fastest turn of my entire life.</p>

<p>I turned it so hard that my neck joints even made a cracking sound.</p>
<p>The force of the motion caused a dull pain on my nape, but I was so startled that I didn't care.</p>

<p>"Ah, sorry for interrupting you, Minato-kun."</p>

<p>"...Why are you in the room before me?"</p>

<p>It was Shizuku who was there.<br>The same outfit as before, Shizuku, who should have returned from the hotel earlier, was sitting on a chair in my room, not at her home.</p>
<p>I was caught completely off guard and when I asked her about the situation, she announced with her ample bosom.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"It's a strategy meeting!"</p>
<p></p>
<p>"I hate it."</p>
<p></p>

<p>The first strategy meeting at Shinra Minato's house was held half-heartedly that didn't even take the room owner's thoughts into account.</p>
