<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;"><span class="EOP"> #121</span></h1>
<p> </p>
<hr />
<p>President called Kaede and Hino-kun over to her and asked them a favor.</p>
<p><span class="EOP"> </span>"I forgot the equipment box in the student council room, I'd like you both to pick it up for me if you don't mind.</p>
<p><span class="EOP"> </span><span class="EOP"> </span></p>
<p>"Okay."</p>

<p>"Understood!"</p>

<p>After the two agreed, president handed Kaede the hoodie which she was wearing.</p>
<p>Although today was a holiday, walking in a swimsuit would be a problem.</p>

<p>Even without words, I could tell that she had taken this into consideration.</p>

<p>Kaede accepted it, gave a small bow, and then followed Hino-kun.</p>
<p>But from my point of view, I'm worried too.</p>

<p>Although we have only known each other for a short time, I understand that Hino-kun is not a bad junior.</p>
<p>However, my worries didn't completely disappear.</p>

<p>As I looked at the president, she looked back at me and our eyes met.</p>
<p>She smiled as if to reassure me, "I know," and gave instructions to a person.</p>

<p>"Miura, you follow them, and I'll take over your part."</p>

<p>She got down from the watchtower and took over her part. As Miura was instructed by the president, she started following the two of them without refusing.</p>

<p>Well, at least this way, Hino-kun won't be able to do something strange.</p>

<p>Now that I had nothing to worry about, I resumed my part when I was called out from behind.</p>
<p>As far as I know, the number of people who talk to me is limited, but this time it was an unfamiliar voice.</p>

<p>"You have a good sister."</p>

<p>"...yeah."</p>

<p>The person who spoke to me was Koizumi.</p>
<p>He muttered it while looking in the direction in which the three went.</p>

<p>It is true that Kaede is excellent by everyone's standards.</p>
<p>Not just in academics, but in socializing as well.</p>

<p>She knows exactly where she stands and doesn't do anything out of line.</p>
<p>At the same time, she takes the initiative in her work.</p>

<p>She smiles at everyone and doesn't make anyone feel uncomfortable, which makes her an excellent sister.</p>
<p>...But her brother at it.</p>

<p>"I've heard that she's beautiful and has the top grades in her grade ...in addition, she understands how to get along with people well."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"..."</p>

<p>Koizumi's words sounded as if he was comparing her to someone else.</p>
<p>It's sounded not so much like a comparison but more like an overlap.</p>

<p>"Don't you find it difficult as an older brother with such a younger sister?"</p>

<p>Koizumi said, as he asked a simple question.</p>
<p>Strangely enough, I didn't even have to think about it, the answer came to me naturally.</p>

<p>"I've never felt that it's hard because my sister is talented."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>There are many things that can be difficult for siblings, just because of the difference in gender.</p>

<p>However, if you ask me if it is difficult for me because my sister is superior, I would say no.</p>

<p>It is true that the superiority or inferiority of siblings can be seen clearly because they are close to each other.</p>
<p>My father often told me.</p>
<p>"Since your little sister is capable, why can't you, as an older brother, work a little harder?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I'm sure this is a common conversation not only in my family, but in any other family with siblings.</p>
<p>It's only natural to be seen as an object of comparison just by being close to them.</p>

<p>In fact, if they don't even compare us, we might feel neglected.</p>

<p>However, that does not mean that it is natural to have a hard time.</p>
<p>From here on, it varies from person to person, but as far as I'm concerned,</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I'm <strong>used to</strong> such situations.</p>

<p>The girl I met before my sister was just as good, if not better.</p>
<p>The first male friend I ever made, could do anything.</p>

<p>More recently, the girl sitting beside me had the abilities which rivaled both of them.</p>

<p>In addition to that, there is a younger sister in the house who is so capable that it's hard to believe we are actual siblings.</p>
<p>Wherever I went, I was surrounded by similar people.</p>

<p>Maybe that's why I got used to them.</p>
<p>At the same time, I understood that my abilities were not that high.</p>
<p> </p>

<p>The reason why we feel uncomfortable when we are compared to others is because we still have some expectations of ourselves.</p>
<p>We are sensitive to comparisons because we cannot give up the idea that we are better than them.</p>

<p>I gave up such faint expectations a long time ago.</p>
<p>I've decided that it's better to do what's right for my abilities from the beginning than to hope for the best and be disappointed.</p>

<p>Well, when you're my age, you've been compared so much that you don't even feel anything anymore.</p>

<p>Besides, my sister had always been fond of me, her brother.</p>
<p>She used to follow me around, and now she follows me side by side.</p>

<p>Now that my parents are working abroad, we are the only family members in the house.</p>

<p>I can't say that it's hard for me to have such a sister.</p>
<p>No matter how far I go, she will always be my cute little sister.</p>

<p>The only thing is that I feel bad that I can't make her proud as an older brother, but I don't think that's an issue here.</p>


<p>That's why my answer came out the moment I was asked.</p>
<p>Koizumi looked at me with a look of envy as I answered immediately.</p>

<p>"You're amazing, Shinra-kun... I'll never be able to give the same answer."</p>

<p>"Do you have a younger sister too?"</p>

<p>For the first time, I talked to Koizumi about something other than school related.</p>
<p>It was the first time I had talked to Koizumi about something other than something official.</p>

<p>It was clear that even though we worked at the same place, we lacked communication.</p>
<p>Koizumi shook his head at my question.</p>

<p>"I'm an only child ...But maybe I'm in a similar position to Shinra-kun."</p>

<p>Koizumi's gaze was not directed towards me, but towards a girl.</p>
