<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#197</h1>
<p> </p>
<hr />
<p>A quiet time between two siblings came after a long and hectic day.</p>
<p>The two of us walked side by side through the residential area, and then visited the shopping district.</p>

<p>"Nii-san, let's go shopping for dinner!"</p>

<p>"Wait!"</p>

<p>Starting from the nearest store, we bought vegetables, meat, and fish, not only for today's dinner, but also for tomorrow's.</p>
<p>Today's dinner would probably be curry or hamburger steak.</p>
<p>With this thought in mind, I received the carrying bag.</p>

<p>"What's your guess for today's dinner?"</p>

<p>She asked me with a potato in her hand.<br />By now, I, as a Nii-chan's point of view would expect me to say it to be curry...</p>
<p>However, it is Minato-kun's nature to not do what is expected.</p>

<p>"Hamburger steak."</p>

<p>"Bubu, it's curry today."</p>

<p>...No, I thought about it, didn't I?<br />But, today's menu was already set in Kaede's mind, isn't it?</p>
<p>As an older brother, I've heard it, I wonder if I'm the only one who thinks that my request would be taken into consideration.</p>
<p>I turned such a gaze to my sister.</p>

<p>"Just kidding, let us have hamburgers."</p>

<p>Kaede said with a wry smile, as if my innocent childlike eyes had an effect on her.</p>
<p>I don't know why, I feel like I've been spoiled and it's rather complicated.</p>

<p>"No, ...curry is fine."</p>

<p>I handed the vegetable bag to the owner and took another bag from Kaede's hand after she paid the bill.</p>
<p>Kaede turned around upon hearing my words.<br />I silently walk alongside her as she walks away from the store.</p>

<p>"It sounds like a lot of work ...to be competing with Yuuto-san."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>When Shizuku had contacted her, I knew that she would have told her my circumstances.<br />There way no way that Kaede would bother to go all the way to the opposite Sakuranaoka Academy.</p>
<p>She said it would be fine if we spoke at home, because we are siblings and we would have the time to do so.</p>
<p>However, the fact that Kaede made time outside our home like this means that she must have guessed that I wouldn't talk to her at home.</p>

<p>"You could have talked to me about it..."</p>

<p>The words she muttered pierced deep into my heart.<br />I am sure that Kaede thought that I didn't rely on her.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>However, I had my own reasons.</p>
<p>With our parents overseas, she and her brother are left to do most of the household chores and also attend school.</p>

<p>My sister is already supporting me adequately from my point of view.</p>
<p>I didn't want to cause her any more trouble.</p>

<p>Kaede is a brilliant girl, and as her brother, I know that better than anyone else.</p>

<p>If I talk to her, she may think about it, explore ways, and even discover some possibilities.</p>
<p>But I didn't want her brother ...to be thought of as a helpless man any more.</p>

<p>I am aware that people around me don't think of us as siblings due to our different looks, and also the fact that I, a talent-less brother, has a sister full of good qualities doesn't help it very much.</p>
<p>Still, I always hoped to be a cool older brother in front of my sister.</p>

<p>"I'm sorry, ...but I don't want you to think that I don't consider Kaede to be unreliable."</p>

<p>"I know that Nii-san won't think so...but if you could have told me about it, even a little bit, I could have helped."</p>

<p>Sad eyes looked towards to me.<br />I met her eyes up front and lightly patted her head with my free right hand.</p>
<p>It's always been kind of a habit of mine when I've had nothing left to say.<br />Kaede, who let me stroke her hair without refusing, chuckled and said.</p>

<p>"This is how Nii-san has always fooled me..."</p>

<p>Kaede said and grabbed my luggage with her small hands as if to take half of it from me.</p>
<p>I hand over the lighter one out of the two, and then we walk together through the crowd in the shopping district.</p>

<p>It's starting to get dark and the shopping district is probably at its peak around this time.</p>
<p>Even so, I felt slight loneliness, which had become smaller than it was in the past.</p>

<p>"Are you going to win the election?"</p>

<p>Kaede, muttered a few words in a voice loud enough not to be drowned out by the sounds from the surrounding stores, and gave me a serious look.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"It depends on Yuuto, ...if that guy keeps going like this, there's a good chance we'll win."</p>

<p>"I see..."</p>

<p>She nodded and let out a slightly relieved breath.</p>
<p>I'm sure she had heard most of the story from Shizuku, but she seemed relieved to hear it from my mouth.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>So ...if Yuuto remains the same, we may win.</p>
<p>However, the moment I said it to myself, emotions swirled through my chest that were difficult to combine into words.</p>
<p> </p>

<p>"However, Nii-san doesn't look happy."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"Did you do something to make you feel guilty? Or is it something else?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Kaede, who has returned her gaze to the front, asks a question that strikes at the essence of the matter.<span class="EOP"> </span></p>
<p> </p>
<p>I don't remember doing anything that I should feel guilty about.</p>
<p>The use of the video for publicity, the use of the broadcast room for school-wide announcements, and the verbal exchange with Yuuto directly were all done within the scope of rules.</p>

<p>"I also heard from Shizuku-san that you had a run-in with Yuuto-san and his supporters."</p>

<p>"Did she tell you that much...?"</p>

<p>They were free to talk as much as they wanted, but even I didn't think it was necessary to tell her that much who had been friends with Yuuto for a long time.</p>
<p>For Kaede, Yuuto is also a senior, a friend of her brother, and a close friend of hers.</p>
<p>It is not pleasant to hear such a person is in direct conflict with his brother.</p>

<p>"When I heard the story, I honestly thought, 'Of course' but not towards Nii-san, but to Yuuto-san."</p>

<p>I listened to Kaede's words quietly.</p>

<p>"I could not go to the same high school as Nii-san, but there is Shizuku-san. Besides, I found out that Kirasaka-san also supports Nii-san, ...but then there is no one to support Yuuto-san."</p>

<p>"But he's got enough people around him to help him..."</p>

<p>"No, it's different, ...it's about emotional support."</p>

<p>Kaede paused as she said so.<br />Then she turns her body to face me and puts her free hand on my chest.</p>

<p>"No matter how many people are around, they won't reach here."</p>

<p>The small palm on my chest felt strong and different from that of the little sister I knew.</p>
<p>I realized that she was growing up as well, and that she would not be a little sister forever.</p>

<p>"Maybe for Yuuto-san, the only person who can reach out to here is my Nii-san, ...not Shizuku-san or Kirasaka-san, but only Nii-san."</p>

<p>Kaede's words made me think of the environment that had surrounded Yuuto up to this point.</p>
<p>They push their aspirations and ideals on him, turn troublesome roles and tasks over to him, and approach him with a smile when it is convenient for them.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>While everything was an equal friendship for Yuuto, who never said no and would never do so, I was certainly placed in a different category.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"The two of you are just clumsy people..."</p>

<p>Hearing Kaede's sigh, a wry smile appeared on my face for some reason.</p>
<p>No, really, as and elder brother and a senior, we are probably just two troublesome and clumsy people to Kaede.</p>

<p>"Nii-san gets irritated because he thinks that Yuuto-san is a great person, doesn't he? That is why your heart is stirring at the sight of his unworthiness."</p>

<p>"Are you ...Buddha?</p>

<p>"Please listen to me properly."</p>

<p>"Yes..."</p>

<p>There is nothing dignified about being an older brother in a situation where your sister says you're not.</p>
<p>I said that thinking that it would change the mood a bit, but she just looked at me with completely cold eyes.</p>

<p>...It's better not to anger my sister, it seems.</p>
<p>I shushed myself and focused on my ears to make sure I didn't miss a single word of what was to follow.</p>

<p>"All you can do now, brother, is to win Yuuto's favour by doing things your own way. After that, everything will be fine."</p>

<p>I gently put my hand over her's, which was resting on my chest.<br />Warmth engulfed my cold hands.</p>
<p>The actual reason for the irritation was just as Kaede said.</p>
<p>The only way to resolve this irritation is to win the election campaign against Yuuto first, and then change it with the help of others.　</p>
<p>I need to abandon the desire to change the environment now itself and then compete with him in his natural state.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Can you review my speech when we get home, or could you just listen to it...?"</p>

<p>"Sure, we can go over it together after dinner!"</p>

<p>Naturally, the scenario is different from the scene I or Yuuto had in mind.</p>
<p>However, that's the so-called reality, and it shows that our thoughts were still a childish imagination.</p>

<p>But, if I were to so frustrated that I could not even concentrate on the competition at hand, it would be impossible for an ordinary student like me to survive the election.</p>
<p>It is impossible from the start to solve problems without harming anything, including the relationships and impressions of those around me.</p>

<p>So all I have to do is think about how to get beyond the person in front of me.</p>
<p>Then I can think about the problems after that.</p>

<p>If I can't do it alone, I should rely on my sister.</p>
<p>If I still can't do it, then I should turn to my childhood friends and classmates.</p>

<p>I am used to being hated by people and being left out of the group.</p>
<p>Otherwise, it would be impossible for me to survive in an environment where there are so many extraordinary people around.</p>

<p>Well, for now, I'm going to try to find the best way to make Yuuto regret this as much as possible.</p>

<p> </p>

<p> </p>





<p>Then, I went home and spent a long, long time with my sister.</p>
<p>We reviewed the content of the speech, reconfirmed my expected scenario, and worked out a plan to deal with the risks.</p>

<p>In the meantime, time passed and we both fell asleep in the living room.</p>
<p>It is a secret between us that even Kaede, who never overslept, fell asleep  until Shizuku came to pick us up as promised.</p>
