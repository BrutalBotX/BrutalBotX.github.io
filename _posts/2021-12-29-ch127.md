<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#127</h1>

<hr>

<p>I was walking alone without a destination in the school, which was getting excited about the fireworks festival.</p>
<p></p>
<p>No matter where I walked, just the people changed, but the topic remained the same.</p>

<p>Who to invite, where to meet, do you have a girlfriend to take to the festival?</p>
<p>It's the typical conversation of a high school student, but also very innocent.</p>

<p>I've heard that as we grow older, we don't have as much fun looking forward to these kinds of events.</p>
<p>It's because we are in an environment where we can enjoy the present and without any worries that we are able to enjoy this kind of event.</p>

<p>But you guys, I'm sorry that I'm getting carried away with this kind of talk about the future, but there is a possibility that you too will have to face reality.</p>
<p>Whatever it is whether it's hidden or not will be revealed when the time is right.</p>

<p>Yes, the results of the final exam.</p>
<p>In fact, the first semester's final exam which had been held over the span of last week.</p>

<p>Since we hadn't studied for the test beforehand, we didn't have much to talk about, so we didn't bother, but the results would be handed in today.</p>
<p>Towards the end of the recess, some of the students started to look pale.</p>
<p>
</p><p>In the midst of all this, one person walks leisurely down the corridor to the vending machine, and that person ...is me.</p>
<p>I'm not going to hide it because I don't need to.</p>

<p>If you're wondering why I'm so carefree about my surroundings, the answer is simple.</p>
<p>I'm sure I'm an average student.</p>

<p>Mr. Average.</p>
<p>You can call me that if you want to.</p>

<p></p>
<p>...It's too embarrassing, and it's also lame, so let' s just pretend I never said that.</p>

<p>As for the final exam this time, I know that my score is about average.</p>
<p>I'm sure you'll be happy to know that I'm not the only one who's had a few bad experiences with this.</p>

<p>Damn you talented people...</p>
<p>Shizuku smiled and said, "Let's review properly next time," but as for Kirasaka, she said, "Oh, even if we reverse your score, Shinra-kun, mine's still better than yours," My pride, which was already as small as an ant's, got hurt.</p>

<p>I'll never forget it.</p>
<p>I'm sitting here in front of the vending machine wondering what to buy, trying to forget about the previous conversations I tried remembering the conversation we had in the classroom earlier.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Festival..."</p>

<p>To be honest, I wasn't too enthusiastic.</p>
<p>Of course, I would have to talk to Kaede, and depending on the outcome, we would have to gather at my house.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Even so, I don't like the atmosphere of the festival itself, in other words, I don't like the act of people gathering together and making a lot of noise, so naturally I can't help but be uncomfortable.</p>

<p>Anyway, it's been years since I've seen a fireworks display with someone other than Kaede.</p>
<p>It's probably because Shizuku used to come to my house to watch it, so I shouldn't have much problems.</p>

<p>But as far as my memory goes, it's a very old memory.</p>
<p>It's a memory from my childhood now, and I've forgotten how I even enjoyed the festival back then.</p>

<p>I bought a drink from a vending machine and walked back to the classroom with it in my hand.</p>
<p>Midday, I was thinking about the day of the festival.</p>

<p>The fireworks display starts at seven, and until then, the street in front of the station will be filled with stalls.</p>
<p>No cars are allowed to enter, and the stores are lined up on both sides of the street.</p>

<p>If possible, I'd like to finish my shopping and get home before the street is filled with pedestrians.</p>
<p>It would be a good idea to ask the old man on the shopping street about the opening hours of the stalls on the way home today.</p>

<p>Returning meant returning to the same hustle and bustle until I reached the classroom.</p>

<p>I could see people getting into the mood at the end of my sight.</p>
<p>It was a student with slightly dyed brown hair, who seemed to be enjoying high school to the fullest.</p>
<p>They all seemed to be talking about their plans for this weekend, with him in the center.</p>


<p>As I watched them, I felt the difference between myself and them.</p>
<p>I'll never be able to enjoy the festival as much as they do.</p>


<p>I don't know why, but it's a theory I have and it has to do with the way I've been living my life.</p>
<p>I don't want to make noise with others, I don't want to go to a festival with everyone.</p>

<p>When we act in a group, not everyone is happy to act in the direction we choose.</p>
<p>Someone has to compromise, someone has to care, and someone has to look out for others.</p>

<p>It may be that they are doing it without realizing it.</p>
<p>But I can't help but see it as obnoxious.</p>

<p>If it's fun to follow the choices made by a core group of people, then I don't need to participate in fun games with my friends.</p>
<p>No, it's better not to participate.</p>

<p>People often say that I am alone and lonely, but who gets to decide that?</p>
<p>I'm a true individual, and if you ask me, being alone is the best.</p>

<p>You don't have to decide where you want to go, what you want to eat, or even how much you want to care if you're in a group.</p>
<p>That's why I even think that acting alone is the most ideal.</p>

<p>This is just a theory.</p>
<p>Just a theory, not the correct answer.</p>
<p>I guess there is no right answer to a question that does not even have a questioner.</p>

<p>The only thing I can say is that reality rarely works out the way I want it to, and that despite what I think, the human relationships around me are starting to get more complicated.</p>

<p>I miss the days when I was alone, gazing out the window in a daze.</p>
<p>I walked back to the classroom through the corridors crowded with students, remembering the nostalgia of the memories which was only a few months old.</p>
