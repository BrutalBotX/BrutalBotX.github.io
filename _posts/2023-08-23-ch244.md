<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align: center">#244</h1>
<hr />
<p>Later on, when Shizuku and Kirasaka eventually returned to the classroom after that incident, the boys in their group were still chatting and giggling.</p>
<p>And then, they apparently proposed the idea of merging with Yuuto's group as a common opinion.</p>
<p>The result goes without saying.<br />They readily agreed.</p>
</p>
<p>To be honest, enjoying the school trip was the goal. It must have been an unexpected proposal to be able to share the same group with Yuuto.</p>
</p>
<p>After obtaining the boys' approval, the two of them headed to the staff room.</p>
<p>They brought up the idea of merging with the homeroom teacher and conveyed that all members were in agreement.</p>
</p>
<p>Upon receiving the updated list of members from the teacher, they returned to the student council room with smiles on their faces.</p>
<p>The way they walked to the student council room was so light that not only I but all the members must have guessed it from just one glance at them.</p>
</p>
<p>Gradually, the atmosphere within the classroom became more peaceful. As days passed, the second-year building underwent a transformation, generating anticipation.</p>
</p>
<p>And on the day before the school trip, I was calling my mother on the international phone from my living room.</p>
<p>The reason was to ask what souvenirs she would like.</p>
<p>After a few rings, a familiar voice came through the receiver.</p>

<p>"Yes, yes my dear son?"</p>

<p>"How did you know it's not Kaede..."</p>

<p>As soon as we started talking, I couldn't help but wonder if this person might be an esper.</p>
<p>Maybe it's mother's intuition.</p>
<p>Hearing my mother's voice after a long time brought a subtle sense of calm, and a smile appeared on my lips.</p>

<p>"Since it's the school trip tomorrow... is there something you'd like me to bring back?"</p>

<p>"Mom would be happy with anything Minato buys for me."</p>

<p>She has the same youthful voice as always. This time, I couldn't help but smile wryly.</p>
<p>The contrast in a call with Dad is remarkable...</p>
<p>However, if there's nothing specific she wants, I'll just choose something from Minato-kun's list of choices, so something like sweets would be fine.</p>
<p>As I was thinking of appropriate parting words, mom said to me first.</p>

<p>"Take care and have a great trip, Minato."</p>

<p>"It's not like I'm traveling alone. You don't need to worry so much."</p>

<p>"A mother always worries about her child."</p>

<p>I could imagine my mother's gentle face as she said that.</p>
<p>She's probably smiling and squinting her eyes.</p>
<p>Thinking that, I suddenly felt embarrassed, which is typical for a teenage boy.</p>

<p>"Well then, I'd better go."</p>

<p>"Ah, wait, Minato."</p>

<p>As I was about to say goodbye and hang up, she interrupted me.</p>
<p>Then there was silence for a few seconds.</p>
<p>"...Did you have something you wanted me to bring back after all?"</p>

<p>"Umm... no, it's nothing. Good night, Minato."</p>

<p>When she said that, she gently placed the receiver down as if nothing happened.</p>
<p>The sound of the call ending echoed, leaving a slight sense of unease.</p>
</p>
<p>"She usually calls me 'Minato-chan'..."</p>

<p>There's something she still can't say.</p>
<p>Both Mom and Kaede are terrible at keeping secrets, which is easy to guess from the way they speak.</p>
<p>A slight unease churned in my chest, but there's no point in endlessly pondering about what I don't know.</p>
<p>I proceeded with the packing that I had stopped halfway through.</p>
</p>
</p>
<p>The day of the school trip arrived.</p>
<p>In front of the Shinra house, a luxury car was parked.</p>
<p>You could tell it was a high-end car at first glance.</p>
</p>
<p>At 6 in the morning, I, Shizuku, and Yuuto were going to the airport in the Kirasaka family car. We handed our luggage to the driver, Kuroi-san.</p>
<p>Next to Kaede, who came out to see us off, there stood President.</p>
<p>At President's suggestion, she would be looking after Kaede during the three days I was away.</p>

<p>It's reassuring to have President by her side in the absence of our parents.</p>
<p>It was surprising that they had interacted and bonded during the cultural festival, but Kaede accepted President's offer without any resistance.</p>

<p>"Take care and enjoy your trip, Nii-san."</p>

<p>"I'll do my best... I'll send photos."</p>

<p>After Kuroi-san loaded the last piece of luggage into the trunk, I gave my thanks and then got into the car. I waved lightly to my sister before getting in.</p>
<p>They watched us until the slowly moving car was out of sight.</p>
</p>
<p>Inside the car, Shizuku and Yuuto were chatting, while Kirasaka was quietly opened a bookmarked page in her book.</p>
<p>The most significant event of our high school life had finally arrived. I wondered what kind of experience it would be.</p>
</p>
<p>Enough of that, back to present.</p>
<p>Waking up early in the morning, the journey to the airport in the car, my consciousness gradually became hazy.</p>
<p>It's not that I'm sleep-deprived, but it's just that early mornings and I don't get along, so I'm tired.</p>
<p>I lean back and close my eyes, partly because the seat I'm sitting on is unnecessarily comfortable.</p>

<p>Whenever the car sways and Shizuku's and Kirasaka's shoulders touch mine, I open my eyes with a jolt and close them again.</p>
<p>But gradually, I began to feel that the occasional touch on my shoulders wasn't just due to the car's movement.</p>
<p>It's a slow, subtle feeling, like being pulled slightly left and right, that travels through my arms.</p>

<p>It's a slow but unnatural sensation that keeps me awake.</p>
<p>However, just before I lose complete consciousness, voices from both sides reach my ears.</p>

<p>"Kirasaka-san, please let go of Minato's hand."</p>

<p>"And maybe you should let go of the arm you're casually holding onto too?"</p>

<p>Yeah... it's my fault for closing my eyes and trying to sleep.</p>
<p>But I whispered in my heart before letting go of my consciousness that I was embarrassed to ask them to quit arguing with each other in the car with Yuuto and Kuroi-san present.</p>

<p>Time flies when you are asleep.</p>
<p>Yuuto's voice woke me up to a large building outside the window.</p>
<p>'Haneda Airport Parking'</p>

<p>I noticed something when I put strength into my legs to loosen my stiff body.</p>
<p>Both of my shoulders are unusually heavy.</p>
<p>And then, a gentle floral scent tickled my nostrils.</p>
<p>Moving my gaze to the side, Kirasaka was sleeping with her head on my right shoulder and Shizuku onto my left shoulder.</p>

<p>"You've got a flower in both the hands"</p>

<p>"Or, more accurately, a head on both the shoulders"'</p>

<p>When I jokingly muttered in a low, sleepy voice, Yuuto, who had already taken down his luggage, gave me a sharp shove.</p>

<p>How can I wake them up in a situation where even my arms are pinned down?</p>
<p>Can someone please tell me?</p>
</p>
<p>A gentle wake-up call in the ear is the protagonist's way.</p>
<p>And since I am not one ....I woke them up with a light head butt for now.</p>

<p>I'll call this the 'Morninghead'.</p>
<p>As a new way of waking up, I even have to summarize it in my graduation thesis and present it.</p>
<p>After grabbing my luggage and thanking Kuroi-san for the ride, the four of us stepped inside the airport.</p>
</p>
