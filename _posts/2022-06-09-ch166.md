<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#166</h1>

<hr>
<p>She declared the meeting to be a strategy meeting, but after that, both of us remained silent.</p>
<p>It was no wonder.</p>

<p>We both understand what the problem is and what we need to do in order to solve it.</p>
<p>However, the problem is that it has already developed to a level that cannot be solved by an individual alone.</p>

<p>No, let me reframe that sentence&nbsp;...the situation has developed to a level that cannot be solved by Shinra Minato's ability.</p>

<p>Hence the silence.<br>I know that I cannot do it by myself anymore.</p>

<p>Even if I solve Shiraishi's case and the student council election problem, the next problem will be about the executive (Sakura) committee.<br>If I could even solve that, next I would have to turn my attention to my recent relationship issues.</p>

<p>But no matter how many questions I ask myself, the answer never seems to come to my mind.<br>A temporary way is to throw it to my future self, but that is not a fundamental solution.</p>

<p>I am reminded that I must broaden my perspective from the self-centered, self-focused way of thinking to a view that would consider others around me.</p>
<p>However, even though my brain knows this, my deep-rooted self-centered ideology ...refuses to allow me to do so, and as a result, I am unable to come up with an answer.</p>
<p>What's the benefit of me getting this deep into a kouhai's issue who I don't even know much about?</p>

<p>Even if I could solve the problem, there's still this issue of relationships.<br>What exactly is a childhood friend, a friend, or a classmate...?</p>

<p>I think it would be a mistake to try to push Shizuku and the others into the framework of those categories.<br>Sometimes I wonder if she and them have already been established with a separate relationship in my mind.</p>

<p>Friends... they are all different.<br>I don't even know how to describe it properly, I hate my vocabulary.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Were the choices I've made up until now, correct. The actions I've taken, the words I've said and done, are they really right?</p>
<p>...No, this is my problem now that I can think back to the past.</p>

<p>I continued to do the best I could under those circumstances.<br>I meant to do so.</p>

<p>But recently, I began to wonder.<br>What is the 'best'?</p>

<p>I think that I am only justifying based on my own principles, but from the viewpoint of others, the exact same choice could be a bad one and makes me look like a ridiculous person.</p>
<p>I was beginning to doubt my own justification because those around me were right, superior, and close.</p>
<p>
</p><p>During the moment of silence with Shizuku, only a recurring and endless chain of thoughts circulated in my brain, as if I was stepping into a swamp that was as deep as I could think.</p>

<p>"Minato-kun is a very strong person..."</p>
<p></p>
<p>"What is it that so suddenly..."</p>

<p>What finally came out of her open mouth was that one sentence.<br>What makes a person strong and what is the standard for strong depends on the individual's sense of values.</p>
<p></p>

<p>I don't know what she meant by strong.</p>

<p>"The only person who hasn't changed since the past is ...you, Minato-kun ...The people around you and I have changed, too. We've spent our lives abandoning ourselves and overwriting our new selves, building upon the excuse that 'to change is to grow up', and that we're growing up."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>I suppose that is ...natural.<br>It would've been abnormal if it wasn't.</p>

<p>It's not that I didn't change, it's that I couldn't.</p>
<p>I did not want to coexist healthily with my environment if it meant that I have to change myself.</p>
<p>I just didn't like the peaceful, ordinary days of how the society worked.<br>The superficial relationships, the daily routine of flattering those who are superior to us and seeking their favor.</p>

<p>If that is what real human relationships are, I don't want them.</p>
<p></p>
<p>'A space where I can feel at ease', as the president said.<br>That is exactly what I wanted.</p>
<p>That is probably the reason why president chose me.</p>

<p>"I can imagine that Minato-kun will resolve this series of problems by following the decisions of the "Shinra Minato" as you've done in the past."</p>

<p>"...I can only do so much."</p>

<p>I know best what I can do.<br>All I can do is repeatedly try to choose the best solution within my limited abilities and options.</p>

<p>Even if I end up in a situation where people hate me in the process, it's still not in my nature to abandon myself and worry about the way people look at me.</p>

<p>"That's why Minato-kun is so strong ...but I don't want Minato-kun to be alone any longer."</p>

<p>The raised gaze, combined with the light from the window, made her look radiant.</p>

<p>
</p><p>"I can't stand the thought of Minato-kun becoming someone else's ...but I also hate to see Minato-kun alone with no one interested in him."</p>

<p>Shizuku muttered with a wry smile, "It's a contradiction, isn't it?"<br>But I couldn't affirm the word contradiction.</p>

<p>I never imagined that anyone would have any bad feelings about me being alone.</p>
<p>To me, it was a normal, unremarkable day in my life.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"To tell you the truth, I was a little glad to see Kirasaka-san talking to Minato-kun during the new term."</p>

<p>"Glad?"</p>

<p>Shizuku nodded, with her cheeks flushed red.<br>Do dogs and monkeys have such feelings towards each other...? &lt;TL&gt;: xD</p>

<p>I was about to open my mouth to ask why, but Shizuku continued.</p>

<p>"I was happy to know that someone other than me and Yuuto had finally come to know the good qualities of Minato-kun ...although at the same time I was angry at the fact that she was a woman."</p>

<p>When she said the words she added to the end of the sentence, Shizuku had a completely blank expression on her face and I could imagine that she was really angry.</p>
<p>Shizuku, s-she's really scary.</p>

<p>"That's why I don't want Minato-kun, who is supposed to be working the hardest for this issue, to not be given due recognition, and for others to be praised for their efforts."</p>

<p>Shizuku said as if declaring.<br>Her serious eyes are directed toward me with a strong glint in them.</p>

<p>This is speculation, but she may have had that experience before.<br>It could have happened that she, who did nothing but worked hard behind the scenes, was the last one to be praised ...without any recognition.</p>

<p>No, I think it did happen.</p>

<p>"It's always been that way and for me ...it's normal."</p>

<p>I don't have any interest in people around me, and I don't want to be admired.<br>So, again, I don't go around telling people how hard I worked.</p>
<p>Even if I did, few people would believe me.</p>

<p>When I told Shizuku, she just smiled sadly.<br>It was like ...she had surrendered, and as if she knew in advance what she was going to do.</p>

<p>"I know Minato-kun... you don't want that, do you?"</p>

<p>"Ah..."</p>

<p>The volume of the voice of Shizuku gradually became lower and lower until it was almost inaudible at the end, and I felt a slight sense of apology as I replied in return.<br>Normally, this would have been the end of the story.</p>
<p>Shizuku's shoulders would've dropped and she would've left the room, but that was not the case today.<br>Instead of dropping her shoulders, she immediately turned her gaze upward, something akin to a strong will in her eyes.</p>

<p>"So ...I'll be the one standing beside Minato-kun when he's working hard! Even if no one praises Minato-kun, I will say that Minato-kun was doing his best... and that person, and Yuuto-kun will do the same."</p>
<p>
</p><p>That person that Shizuku is referring to, I somehow know who that is without having to put it into words.</p>
<p>And Yuuto is the same.</p>

<p>"I will help Minato-kun."</p>

<p>I felt as if Shizuku had reverted back to the old Shizuku a little bit as she said that without listening to my opinion.</p>
<p>I was back to the days when she would just smile and innocently pull my hand without listening to me.</p>

<p>The hand she held out to me was small and white.<br>But it was a very reassuring one.</p>
<p>As if hesitating to take my hand back, Shizuku immediately grabbed my right hand, which was moving slowly.</p>
<p>I smiled at her and turned towards her.</p>

<p>"Sorry... but please look after me."</p>

<p>"Yes! Leave it to me!"</p>

<p>Is there any childhood friend who is so dependable?</p>
<p>Today's Kanazaki Shizuku was strong and kind enough to make me feel this way.</p>
