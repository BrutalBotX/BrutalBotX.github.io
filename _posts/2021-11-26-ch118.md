<p>
</p><h1 style="text-align:center;">Ch#118</h1>
<p></p>
<hr>
<p>After school, I asked Shizuku, Kirasaka, and Yuuto to go to a certain place to gather enough people to clean the pool.</p>
<p>　It was another high school in the city, located on the opposite side of Sakuranuoa Academy.</p>

<p>　We were headed to Kaede's girls' high school.</p>
<p>　Of course, I haven't told the three of them about the cleanup yet.</p>

<p>　It was a bother to talk to Kaede again, so I thought it would be better to get the people together first before asking them.</p>

<p>　The three of them, who were following me without saying anything specific, were looking up at the building as if it was a rare place to visit.</p>

<p>The girls' school that Kaede goes to was a little further on, right?</p>

<p>"...Yeah, it's just up the hill ahead."</p>

<p>　There is an uphill before Sakuranooka Academy, but the high school Kaede goes to has an even steeper uphill.</p>
<p>　It's the so-called heart-breaking hill, and just the thought of running up this hill when you're running late is sufficient to make you feel depressed.</p>

<p>　The moment Kirasaka and Yuuto saw this hill, they looked at it with disgust, but Shizuku seemed to be used to it.</p>
<p>　She had been with Kaede several times when she picked her up, so she was not surprised.</p>



<p>　As we slowly but surely walked up the hill, we saw a beautiful white building standing in front of us.</p>
<p>　I don't know if there is such a thing as an Eastern or Western style school building, but the white building that resembles a castle gives off a sense of being out of place.</p>

<p>　This is the school building of the girl's high school that Kaede goes to.</p>

<p>　It's not a school for young ladies, but it's a high school for girls where children from well-to-do families attend, and there were many cars stopping to pick them up.</p>
<p>　We stood on the other side of the road and waited for Kaede to come out.</p>

<p>　At first, it was easy to overlook the task of finding my sister in a crowd of only female students, but now I can recognize her from a distance.</p>

<p>　The moment Kaede herself sees me, she suddenly accelerates and runs right in front of me, as if she is equipped with an accelerator.</p>
<p>　It was so obvious that I probably didn't even need to get used to it.</p>

<p>　Today, Kaede, who was walking near the school gate, stiffened her body as if she had been struck by lightning.</p>
<p>　She looked around and turned her gaze to the other side of the road where we were standing. Her face lit up as she walked lightly up to us.</p>

<p>"Nii-san! Thank you for picking me up today!"</p>

<p>"Don't worry about it... I had something to talk to Kaede today."</p>

<p>"To me...?"</p>

<p>Kaede asked, tilting her head slightly.</p>
<p>Yeah, her brother thinks it is an adorable gesture.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Well, it's too noisy to talk here, let's move to another place."</p>

<p>　I believe there was a coffee shop nearby.</p>
<p>　Many students don't stop there, and it's far from our high school, so there shouldn't be a problem.</p>

<p>　The five of us, including Kaede, decided to hasten to the coffee shop.</p>


<p>　It was past three o'clock, a relatively empty time of day.</p>
<p>　As soon as we entered the store, we were shown our seats, and after making sure that all of us were given cold drinks, we started talking.</p>

<p>"This weekend, if you have time... I'd appreciate it if you all could help me clean the pool."</p>

<p>"Sure."</p>

<p>"I don't mind."</p>

<p>"No."</p>

<p>"I'll also help!"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>　Now, do you know who said what?</p>
<p>　I'm not Prince Shotoku, you know?.</p>
<h6 class="zw-paragraph heading0">(TL/N - A politician from Asuka period, compiled the chronicles of the government, after the Chinese model, to make up the first book of Japanese history)</h6>

<p>And I am not confident that I can hear what was said correctly when someone replies to me at once, and at the same time.</p>
<p>
</p><p>　However, for some reason, I was able to perfectly hear one person...</p>

<p>　I'm sure I heard it clearly.</p>

<p>　I also think I completely know who said it.</p>
<p>　When I turned my gaze to her, as expected, Kirasaka was looking at me with a look of disinterest.</p>

<p>"No. It's an outdoor pool, right? It's dirty."</p>

<p>"I haven't said anything yet..."</p>

<p>　Kirasaka tells me the answer on the premise that I will ask why she dislikes the idea.</p>
<p>However, for one reason, I could not refute since I had the same impression when President asked me about it.</p>

<p>　Cleaning the pool... I certainly don't like that idea.</p>
<p>　Goose bumps appear just by thinking of entering the water that is dirty and green.</p>

<p>　As I was considering asking the three of them to remove her from the situation with a reasonable solution, Kaede joined the conversation.</p>

<p>"It would be nice to have you with us, Kirasaka-san."</p>

<p>"...but I'm not very good at cleaning work."</p>

<p>　Oh, I don't think she can do it herself.</p>

<p>It seems she has a housekeeper at home for that kind of work.</p>
<p>But Kaede didn't back down.</p>

<p>"Perhaps the pool will open early."</p>

<p>"Even if we get to swim..."</p>

<p>"Wouldn't it be a good unveiling if you bought yourself a new swimsuit?"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>She's wavering.</p>
<p>She is completely shaken by Kaede's words.</p>

<p>　To make matters worse, the person behind her, Shizuku, nodded several times in agreement.</p>

<p>　When I turned my attention to Kaede for a moment, she was also looking at me.</p>
<p>　It looked as if her eyes told that she will convince her.</p>

<p>　I wonder if my imouto is good at persuading the onee-samas.</p>
<p>　Since they both acted as an elder sister on routine basis, wouldn't it be more effective to persuade them using my imouto's sisterly skills...?</p>

<p>　I and Yuuto, who were completely turned into air, devoted ourselves to watching the situation alongside each other.</p>
<p>　It was all we could do.</p>

<p>"A bentou..."</p>

<p>"There would no one around but the student council and us..."</p>

<p>　One by one, Kaede gradually persuaded Kirasaka by using her psychology against her.</p>
<p>　And then, perhaps having fallen completely at the words of a situation with few people, she nodded one.</p>

<p>"I understand... I'll help."</p>

<p>"Thank you!"</p>

<p>The smile on Kaede's face was radiant.</p>
<p>　It was the moment when I understood that this girl was capable of thinking and acting far more than what I had imagined.</p>
