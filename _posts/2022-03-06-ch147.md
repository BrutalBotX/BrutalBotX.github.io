<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#147</h1>


<hr>
<p>The summer sun irritates my skin.</p>
<p>The usually unhealthy white skin should look like that of a high school student who has spent some outside in this summer vacation.</p>

<p>I am supposed to be spending a long vacation, confined to my room spending alone, but instead, my brain is packed with a schedule.</p>

<p>- Days from the beginning to the end of summer vacation.</p>
<p>- The time and number of days in the Student Council till the&nbsp; &nbsp;elections.</p>
<p>- Need to talk to the required people along with it.</p>
<p>...was what occupied my mind.</p>

<p>As for time to finish assignments, I am glad that most of them are done this year.</p>

<p>Most people would think that was what I have had planned for my nearly month-long summer vacation.<br>However, let me tell you.</p>
<p>My original plan was to get up in the morning, eat, read, take a bath, and go to bed.</p>
<p>It was supposed to be a completely <strong>indoor person</strong>, which is far from what the case is.</p>

<p>Some time have passed since when I returned home after my talk with the president. I threw myself on the sofa as soon as I came, in the air-conditioned living room without even turning on the TV or the lights.</p>
<p>The impact on my face was stronger than I had imagined, and I mistook that my nose was bleeding.</p>
<p>In fact, I even mistake my runny nose for a nosebleed sometimes.</p>

<p>"...-I wonder?"</p>

<p>It was just a monologue, not something uttered in search of an answer.<br>But unexpectedly, a reply came back.</p>

<p>"What happened?"</p>

<p>"...I didn't know Kaede was home too."</p>

<p>Kaede, with a shopping bag in her hand, stood at the entrance of the living room, tilted her head and asked a question.</p>
<p>Kaede looked at me with a little sweat pouring down her forehead, moving in the direction of the air conditioner breeze, as if to relieve the heat built up in her body.</p>

<p>It must be a strange situation from Kaede's point of view.</p>
<p>Her brother, who was supposed to be at a study camp to finish his assignments, came back only to find him talking to himself in the living room.</p>

<p>"I just went shopping in the shopping district. ...So, is there something troubling you nii-san?"</p>

<p>"No, nothing's troubling me, though, ...It's just a little something related to the student council."</p>

<p>Kaede sat down next to me and took out an iced coffee from a bag and listened to me as I sipped it.</p>
<p>We were as close as ever, and I could feel the heat on my arm from Kaede's body, who had just returned.</p>

<p>I know the root of the problem and the answer.<br>But I can't connect the path to get there.</p>
<p>I know the quickest way to solve the problem.<br>I can just bow down to Shizuku and the others and ask them to cooperate with us.</p>
<p></p>
<p>I'd rather throw away my petty pride than to report that I couldn't do anything with my big mouth.</p>

<p>But while it sounds good to rely on them that way, it also sounds somewhat forced.</p>
<p>As a council member, I would have to be active in the student council, so I won't be able to help much.</p>
<p></p>
<p>'I can't do it, but you guys do your best!' ...might make me question my humanity.</p>
<p>I feel guilty that because I am friends with someone I could easily ask to do something.</p>
<p>
</p><p>I'm not sure if Kaede could tell from my voice and facial expression that I was talking about something serious, but instead, she said in a cheerful tone.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"Nii-san has been always been an over-thinker, so why don't you just talk to Shizuku-san for the time being?"</p>

<p>She then pointed to a room in the house across the street.<br>It was a window with a single light-source, where Shizuku's room was located.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"I can't say too much when it comes to Nii-san's school, and I'm sure Shizuku-san won't deny your request."</p>

<p>No, imouto (*Younger sister), Shizuku-san is the main issue as well.<br>Rather, Shizuku-san is the main issue.</p>

<p>My legs are heavy, partly because she was somewhat grumpy when we parted at school.</p>
<p>But in the end, the fact remains that we are going to talk.</p>
<p>It's just a matter of whether it's sooner or later.</p>

<p>If it were true, I would have liked to have talked to her later when I made a firm decision, but now it's clear that it's better to move quickly.</p>

<p>"...Can I ask you to contact Yuuto and Kirasaka from my phone? I'll go get Shizuku."</p>

<p>"Understood!"</p>

<p>Pointing to the phone on the table and requesting her, I nodded once to Kaede, who made a saluting pose, and then decided to go to Kanazaki's household across the street.</p>
