<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align: center">#233<span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span></h1>
<p></p>
<hr>
<p>I looked around while taking a momentary rest under the shade of the trees with the noise and clamor as my background music.</p>
<p>Although it was unnecessarily noisy, it became valuable when I considered the fact it was a part of this event.</p>

<p>As I exhaled with an empty cup in my hand, I heard footsteps treading on the grass from behind me.</p>
<p>I turn around to see the person and found that Kirasaka was replaced by Shizuku, who was looking at me with a smile on her face.</p>

<p>"Is it okay to take a rest?"</p>

<p>"...Then is it okay to not take it?"</p>

<p>Shizuku walks up to me with a drink in her hand, which she must have purchased before arriving here, and gently reaches out to me.</p>
<p>
</p><p>I grabbed her hand without refusal and was about to ask her something as I stood up.</p>
<p>But she smiled, as if to say that it was unnecessary.</p>

<p>"I'm looking forward to the best break of my life from here on out."</p>

<p>"Is that how you see it...?"</p>

<p>Excessive expectations will put pressure on me, so please don't do that.</p>
<p>Well, it's not like Shizuku is expecting a gentlemanly response from me.</p>
<p>Around us... it's a long-standing rule of thumb.</p>

<p>I lightly brush the dust off my uniform next to Shizuku, then turn my attention to her.</p>
<p>When our gazes met, she pulled back her arm as if to say she had been waiting for me.</p>

<p>"Let's go! I want to go to the shopping district!"</p>

<p>"Ehhh... but it's far."</p>

<p>"Don't we have our own buses?"</p>

<p>When I was silenced by the legitimate argument, Shizuku smiled smugly and proceeded to walk towards the main gate.</p>
<p>I had no choice but to follow her, so I followed her behind like a ghost.</p>
<p>Let's forget about that...</p>

<p>We boarded the bus that was waiting at the main gate until the next departure time, and sat down side by side.</p>
<p>We had nothing much to say to each other, so we both watched the people visiting the festival from inside the bus.</p>

<p>A moment of silence followed, and then the bus engine started.</p>
<p>The driver announced that the bus was bound for the shopping district, and as it started to drive away, Shizuku murmured quietly.</p>

<p>"I'm looking forward to it..."</p>

<p>"It's my first time to go to the shopping district for a cultural festival."</p>

<p>"Yes..."</p>

<p>A little, it feels like the distance between us has shrunk ever since she said those words.</p>
<p>I looked next to her with only a glance, not turning my face to the side, and she was smiling and clasping her hands in her lap.</p>

<p>I watched the townscape flow by through the bus window, and in less than ten minutes, the entrance to the shopping district came into view.</p>
<p>The bus stops at the designated spot, and the people sitting in the front get off one by one.</p>

<p>We got off the bus as well, and found that the entrance to the shopping district was decorated in a different way than usual.</p>
<p>The word "Sakura Matsuri" &lt;Cherry Blossom Festival&gt; was written in cherry blossom colors on a signboard.</p>

<p>We entered the shopping district, feeling somewhat pleased that they had chosen Sakura instead of Kikyo.</p>

<p>"It's a great success!"</p>

<p>"...Looks like there are more people here than in our high school."</p>

<p>People, people, just people!</p>
<p>The mayor must be chuckling somewhere at this right now.</p>

<p>The shopping district, which is in full swing, looks different from usual.</p>
<p>Both sides of the street were lined with stalls, just like at a summer festival, and even in front of a supermarket, people were selling skewers and other items at a discount.</p>

<p>Since the shopping street is mainly for eating and drinking, all the stores were lined with many attractive products at this time of the day.</p>
<p>Shizuku must have felt the same way, and her eyes lit up as she scanned the surroundings and moved her head around.</p>

<p>"Where shall we go then?"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>I answer after a short pause to Shizuku, who looks up at me with such a shining gaze.</p>

<p>"Let's go to Uncle's first, shall we?"</p>

<p>"Yes, we should go there first!"</p>

<p>Shizuku nodded and started to walk ahead.　</p>
<p>A wry smile unintentionally spills out at the figure of her grabbing my uniform around the elbow as she proceeded.</p>

<p>I wonder why they grab uniforms, both Kirasaka and Shizuku?</p>
<p>It's not like I'll run away.</p>

<p>The shopping district is comparatively older, but there are many junior high school students among them.</p>
<p>The boys' eyes were on Shizuku, but she didn't seem to mind and continued on her way.</p>

<p>About halfway down the shopping street, the familiar old fish shop came into view.</p>
<p>With its fragrant aroma wafting in the air, they probably were selling seafood skewers.</p>
<p>As the two of us stood in line at the shop, the old man noticed us.</p>

<p>“Oh, isn’t it Minato and Shizuku-chan!”</p>

<p>"Yo…"</p>

<p>"Hello."</p>

<p>I raised my hand and Shizuku responded with a small bow.</p>
<p>With a smile that didn't seem to fit his strong face, the old man handed the item to the customer in front of us and spoke to us.</p>

<p>"What, just the two of you on a date? How nice to be young."</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>…I just came to the store during my break.</p>
<p>You don't have to thank him while smiling either Shizuku...</p>

<p>You don't even get uncle's jokes, do you?</p>
<p>He'll take it seriously, and I'll have to deal with the hassle of being teased later.</p>
<p>While the two of them were having a pleasant conversation, I decided to ask for a proper order.</p>

<p>"Two scallop skewers"</p>

<p>"Aiyo, I'll cook delicious food for the cute boy and the young lady."</p>

<p>Thinking about it, it had been a long time since I had a casual conversation with Uncle like this.</p>
<p>Even at the summer festival, he threw some serious words at me.</p>
<p>I wonder if he was happy that I came together with my childhood friend to the store, as good friends.</p>
<p>I felt a little sorry if I had him worried.</p>

<p>While waiting for our order to be ready, Shizuku looked around happily.</p>
<p>I also looked around with curiosity at the unusual shopping street.</p>

<p>After all, this street with many meat shops is strictly prohibited to enter when hungry.</p>
<p>There are plenty of things that can make you fat.</p>
<p>Shizuku is a girl, so she must be extra cautious in this area.</p>
<p>She seems to calculate her calories or something.</p>

<p>Girls are amazing, aren't they?</p>
<p>They've got a spirit of steel, suppressing their greed and taking care of their diet.</p>

<p>Then again, I feel that the moment a trendy food is discovered, that suppression is reduced to nothing... I wonder if that is a kind of reward for perseverance.</p>
<p>It's hard to say something...</p>

<p>"Look, it's Uncle's special grilled scallops."</p>

<p>"Thanks, here's your money..."</p>

<p>"I can't take any money from someone like my grandchildren, just eat it!"</p>

<p>He pushed back the money I had offered and said so.　</p>
<p>I checked behind, but since no one was lined up, I decided to accept the courtesy.</p>
<p>I handed one of them to Shizuku, who smiled and thanked me as well.</p>

<p>"Thank you very much!"</p>

<p>"Thanks ...then, good luck."</p>

<p>I turned back and thanked him, and the old man smiled with a puckered expression on his face.</p>
<p>He let out a hearty laugh in a voice that must have come from his depths and said a few extra words as he looked over at us.</p>

<p>"Enjoy your date!"</p>

<p>…Let’s stop because there are eyes around us.</p>
<p>Not only me, but Shizuku's face was red too.</p>

<p>But it's not a bad feeling to still be treated kindly by someone who has taken care of you since you were a child.</p>
<p>I waved my hand appropriately and left with a wry smile on my face, and Shizuku chuckled as well.</p>

<p>Sitting on a bench on a street in the shopping district, chewing on a freshly grilled scallop, Shizuku reveals her feelings in a gentle tone of voice.</p>

<p>"They are good people, all of them in this shopping district."</p>

<p>Before reaching Uncle's store, I was approached by many other stores.</p>
<p>A meat shop owner, a kimono vendor, a fruit and vegetable vendor, and a candy store owner also approached me.</p>

<p>They have been using this place for a long time, and since they have known us since we were young, they are kind to us as if we were their own children.</p>
<p>That's why we can't help but feel indebted to the people who work in this shopping district.</p>

<p>Many people say they don't like the countryside because it is inconvenient, but I, on the contrary, don't dislike the close relationships that exist here in the countryside.</p>
<p>I think this is because they are older than my parents.</p>

<p>"It's even more now when I come here, ...the only one's I seem to remember are the ones who I meet alongside Minato-kun."</p>

<p>"...It's because we've known each other since childhood."</p>

<p>The order of magnitude of the time we shared is different from other students.</p>
<p>The amount we know is also different, so it's not surprising that the ones that remain in your memory are the ones with me.</p>

<p>But I knew that was not what she meant.</p>
<p>She knew, and the words spilled out of her mouth unconsciously, as if to go unnoticed.</p>

<p>"I have lots of friends. There have been many fun and exciting things... but the things that remain in my memory are only the casual things I did with Minato-kun."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"I am sure that if other people heard about me like this, they would think I am a terrible person."</p>

<p>She looked up at the sky, her expression was clear but her voice sounded very sad.</p>
<p>There were no other students from our school nearby, and since I was next to her, her innermost worries were spilling out.</p>

<p>So I'm responsible for making sure I hear it.</p>
<p>I'm the one who is still not giving answers and creating ambiguity in the relationship.</p>

<p>"What should I do for ...everyone, for Minato-kun?"</p>

<p>The gaze directed at me was very fragile, tinged with a pale light.</p>
<p>Her arms were held tightly, pleading for an answer.</p>

<p>Comforting words would not solve her problems.</p>
<p>That is why I thought about it in silence.</p>

<p>And when I slowly opened my eyes and met hers, I quietly decided to tell her my thoughts without a single lie or pretense.</p>
