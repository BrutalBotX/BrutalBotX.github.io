<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#152</h1>

<hr />
<p>There were no further developments.</p>

<p>A week has passed since then.<br />The first part of the summer vacation was hectic, but things are now calm and quiet as planned.</p>

<p>The issue with Shiraishi is not something that can be resolved quickly.</p>
<p>There is nothing I can do for her now but to wait and see what kind of choice she will make after she is satisfied with her decision.</p>

<p>Will she follow through with her original goal of becoming student body president, or will she choose to be a member of the executive committee, or will she choose a third option?</p>
<p>One needs time to think.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>At first I wondered if I should just wait and see, but Shiraishi said she would get in touch with me, so it's not a bad idea for me my life for now.</p>
<p>The holiday that had arrived after a long time, a day off to relax and not worry about anything  ........did not last very long.</p>



<p>Late in the afternoon, I was reading alongside my sister on the sofa in the living room.　</p>
<p>Kaede was writing the family budget next to me, knitting in her spare time, and peeking at the paperback I was reading, so it was supposed to be a normal day.</p>

<p>While I was thinking about what to have for dinner tonight, the phone in the living room rang with the sound of an incoming call.</p>

<p>"I'll get it."</p>

<p>"Nnn... okay."</p>

<p>Kaede stood up next to me and rushed to the phone, picking it up while placing the receiver to her ear.</p>
<p>After looking around with a sideways glance, she returned her gaze to the text in front of her.</p>

<p>I have read and re-read this book several times, so I sat up and decided it was time to get a different book, and Kaede raised her voice in an unusually surprised manner.</p>

<p>"O-Otou-san! What's the matter, you called so suddenly..."</p>

<p>"Oyaji.....?"</p>

<p>When I realized that the caller was my father, I sat back down again from my half-standing position.</p>
<p>It is rare for that man to make a phone call.</p>

<p>He never calls unless he really has something to do, which is why I immediately guessed it was something urgent.</p>

<p>"Yes, though..."</p>

<p>Kaede's eyes took a few glances at me several times and while I walked over to her side.<br />Then, I took the receiver silently, put it to my ear, and spoke.</p>

<p>"Long time no see, Oyaji."</p>

<p>[Minato, it's been a while.]</p>

<p>Just like that, the long-awaited father-son conversation occurred over a phone call.</p>

<p>"It's rare to see you call."</p>

<p>[Oh ...originally I would have gone home to talk to you about it, but I have a few days left until summer vacation, so I thought I'd just leave a message first."</p>

<p>As usual, I realized his not too dominating tone indicated that we are father and son duo indeed.<br />I suppressed my disgust at the similarity between our speeches and waited for his next words.</p>

<p>I don't need to use unnecessary words when talking with this person.<br />We are people who don't like to talk to each other as parent and child, because that is the only thing we are alike in.</p>

<p>[The arranged marriage arrangements have been done.]</p>

<p>"Wha-?"</p>

<p>[As soon as I get back to Japan, I'm going to ask you to meet them.]</p>

<p>---Just as I was about to protest at my father's one-sided, nonchalant statement, Kaede took the phone away from me and yelled at him.</p>

<p>"I thought your Okaa-san said no to Nii-san's talk! It is a story that neither Nii-san nor I had agreed to in the first place!"</p>

<p>Uh, yeah, I guess so.<br />I certainly wasn't convinced, and I even almost forgot about it before he mentioned it.</p>


<p>"Anyways, I'm not convinced on this topic over the phone! Anyways, let's talk about this topic when Otou-san gets home!"</p>

<p>With that, the receiver was put down with a loud thump, and Kaede, who hung up the phone, started breathing heavily...<br />Then she turned her eyes towards me while giggling and approached silently.</p>

<p>I didn't do anything wrong, but when I involuntarily backed away, but Kaede unexpectedly walked next to me and picked up the phone from the table.</p>
<p>When I thought she was operating the screen like a demon, she immediately put it to her ear.</p>

<p>As I watched quietly to see who she was calling, Kaede opened her mouth and shouted loudly.</p>

<p>"Shizuku-san! It's an emergency!"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Aaa....."</p>

<p>Kaede's one sentence made it all clear.<br />My circumstances after this, my situation, and the fact that I could not escape.</p>

<p>I sat down on the sofa as if I had lost my strength, and within seconds the intercom rang, which made my whole body tense.</p>
