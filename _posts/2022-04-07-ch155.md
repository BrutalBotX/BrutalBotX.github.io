<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#155</h1>

<hr>

<p>Parents are the people closest to us.<br>For better or for worse, they are the first people we imitate in our life.</p>

<p>People say that children grow up watching their parents' backs, and I am no exception.</p>
<p>I grew up watching my father and mother.</p>

<p>I am sure that they had no small influence in shaping the Shinra Minato that I am today.<br>Every time I see them, I am reminded that they we are indeed blood related.</p>


<p>
</p>


<p>Surprisingly, that day came early.<br>It happened suddenly, without any prior notice.</p>

<p>Four days had passed since my conversation with Yuuto.<br>The days of completing assignments while waiting to hear from Yuuto, who had been in contact with Shiraishi, came to an end.</p>

<p>While I was having breakfast to start the day, that person came back.</p>

<p>"Long time no see, you two."</p>

<p>With a carry bag in one hand, the man in the suit looked first at Kaede and then at me.<br>His gaze, less compassionate than when he looked at my sister, as if he were merely checking on the changes, quickly shifted to the house.</p>

<p>With a single breath of relief that the living room looked much the same as it did before he left home, our father ...Shinra Yuichi stepped forward.<br>He kept his luggage near the door and hung his suit on the back of a chair.</p>

<p>"I'm home."</p>

<p>The encounter with my father began in a rather non-dramatic, mundane way.</p>


<p>"O-Otou-san! If you had called me, I would have come to pick you up."</p>

<p>Startled Kaede quickly stood up and rushed towards him...<br>She took the souvenirs from her father's hand, and when he told her so, she hurriedly took out a cold drink from the refrigerator and offered it to him.</p>

<p>He smiled, accepted it, and opened his mouth so as to speak.</p>

<p>"I just wanted to see the looks on the kids' faces when they were surprised."</p>

<p>"...You're a dad with bad taste."</p>

<p>You are old enough to be acting like a child.<br>When I grumbled some cold words, he turned his gaze towards me once more.</p>

<p>"Minato ...you're not bothering Kaede, are you?"</p>

<p>"Rest assured ...Kaede is having an easier time than when dad is at home."</p>

<p>It must be annoying for a daughter to have an overprotective parent nearby.</p>
<p>After exchanging words that seem hardly like a conversation for someone who met after a long time, the subject changed when Kaede entered the room.</p>

<p>"Isn't mother with you?"</p>

<p>Indeed, Mom isn't behind him.<br>At first I thought she would come in later with luggage or for some other reason, but apparently not.</p>

<p>Kaede wondered about the sight of dad coming home alone, and asked.</p>

<p>"Mom's went to shopping and will come home later. She was looking forward to cooking dinner with Kaede tonight."</p>

<p>"Jeez, you should have told me earlier..."</p>

<p>Kaede muttered in frustration, puffing out her cheeks even more.<br>Alright, she's cute. Yeah it's fine since she's cute.</p>

<p>I don't know what's fine, but it's fine.</p>

<p>With my sister by my side, I slowly pulled an empty seat next to me.</p>

<p>" ...Why don't you sit down instead of standing there?"</p>

<p>Of course, I sat some distance away.<br>I hate just thinking about it ...a son and father bumping shoulders and having friendly conversation.</p>

<p>For father and son to be in a good relationship, this is the standard scene of the Shinra family.</p>
<p>Without saying thank you, he too sat down on a chair as a matter of course and spoke softly to Kaede, who sat down across from him.</p>

<p>"Kaede ...is taking a bath with me today---"</p>

<p>"Don't want to."</p>

<p>"Then we can sleep together..."</p>

<p>"Don't want to."</p>

<p>An abrupt expression of affection for his daughter.<br>However, it was rejected with a cold tone.</p>

<p>It was also accompanied by a detached look and an expression of total rejection from her.</p>
<p>In response, dad calmly smiled and said...</p>


<p>"Minato ...it's raining today."</p>

<p>"Wipe your tears and snot, it's disgusting."</p>

<p>There was not the slightest hint of a father's dignity.</p>

<p>Shinra Yuichi</p>
<p>Age thirty... thir-...late thirties.</p>
<p>Occupation- businessman, position being a section manager.</p>

<p>He was born into a very ordinary family, and there are no major aspects in his appearance that stood out.</p>

<p>He is good at talking with people, and I think I have mentioned before that he got his current position just because he is a good talker.</p>
<p>He plays the cool father to his son and acts calmly in the house, perhaps wanting to show his daughter what a cool father he is.</p>

<p>However, his abilities are mediocre, and his true nature is easily exposed.<br>The chances increase even more so if he is pretending to have a personality that is contrary to his own.</p>

<p>He tries to express his affection for Kaede, whom he loves dearly, in ways that are completely out of his character, but has never succeeded.<br>He is not the type to flatter mom, Kotone, but somehow they managed to be married and have two children.</p>

<p>I don't want to hear about my father and mother's love affairs, so I didn't ask, but this is the reality of our siblings' father.</p>

<p>His selfish comments and attitude often make me mad, but I guess the reason I don't dislike him is because of this helpless stupidity.</p>

<p>"...But, I asked you so much to call me Papa though...."</p>

<p>"I won't ever call you that, I swear."</p>

<p>I'm scared, Kaede, she's scary.<br>Your brother's coffee cup is vibrating at the level of a phone's incoming call.</p>

<p>I cut to the chase with a bit of fear towards Kaede, who is consistently not changing her cold treatment of her dad.</p>
<p>Rather, there is only so much to talk about with this person.</p>

<p>"Oyaji... I assume you have an explanation for my arranged marriage story?"</p>

<p>The father, who had been discouraged by the cold treatment from Kaede, changed his expression to a serious one at that one question.<br>The words that followed didn't show any hints of a joke.</p>

<p>"I apologize for deciding on this matter without consulting you ...but there is someone I really want you to meet, and I'm not joking."</p>

<p>The tone of his voice, the expression on his face was one I had seen several times before.<br>When I decided on high school, when I turned down my dad's offer to move overseas, and when I told him that I would be living with Kaede alone.</p>

<p>There was this serious father figure.</p>

<p>"I don't know, but it's not for your own position or any such trivial reasons, is it?"</p>

<p>"How could I not love my son enough to give him up for my position ...it's because of the other party's strong desire."</p>

<p>With a sigh, he gives a small chuckle.<br>He does not love his son as much as his sister, but he seems to have other ways to express his love for him as well.</p>

<p>That rare expression on his face forced me to keep silence.</p>
