<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#212</h1>

<hr>
<p>The student council members began to take action when the proposed limitations on whether or not the festival will be a joint event were presented.</p>
<p>When the board members, led by President, were about to make their moves after being assigned to their respective responsibilities, but Shizuku said something that made them all wait.</p>

<p>"What I'm saying is... if it's within my capabilities, of course I'll do my best..."</p>
<p>
</p><p>President stopped her steps as she heard Shizuku's words.<br>The two of them faced each other and their gazes crossed.</p>
<p>There is no one between the two of them.</p>
<p>I, Kirasaka, and the other board members were all standing there watching the situation unfold.</p>
<p></p>
<p>What did Shizuku really want from the president?　</p>
<p>Is there a job that Shizuku, who is supposed to be in charge of contacting and consulting with the students, would like to do?</p>
<p>The student council must be thinking so.</p>

<p>But, for some reason, my thoughts were going in a different direction.</p>
<p>I knew what Shizuku was trying to tell the president.</p>
<p>What Shizuku is trying to say is the only thing that clearly separates her and me.</p>

<p>It's like a boundary.</p>
<p>If I stepped over it, there would be no going back and I would be deprived of a reasonable amount of time and freedom.</p>
<p></p>
<p>However, she stepped in here because she wanted to.</p>
<p>She knew it was what she had to do, that there had to be something she could do.</p>

<p>She, is different from me.</p>
<p>She is a person who originally had no reason to enter this place.</p>
<p>Shizuku, who must have spent all this time suppressing her own will, deserves more time for herself.</p>
<p>I know this, but I can't stop her from speaking out, even though I know in my heart that she is about to do so.</p>
<p></p>

<p>"Please put me in the student council."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>At Shizuku's words, even President gulped.</p>
<p>The clear separation between me and Shizuku here is whether or not she is a member of the student council.</p>

<p>She herself had once refused to join the student council, and had declared that there was no need for her to be a member.</p>
<p>However, her words that overturned that idea showed her determination.</p>

<p>"The reason for this ...I guess, it's obvious isn't it?"</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>President's voice sounded almost like resignation, and Shizuku replied without a pause.</p>
<p>
</p><p>Shizuku's gaze, which should have been fixed, turned for a moment to me standing behind her.</p>
<p>And then, she quickly returned to her former position and expressed her feelings.</p>

<p>"I can't find any other way for me to enter the place where Kirasaka-san and President already are."</p>

<p>As she tells her, Shizuku stares at President, as if looking for a reaction.</p>
<p>President is talking with Shizuku with her back to me, so I can't see her expression.</p>
<p>
</p><p>I can tell you that President is also conflicted.</p>
<p>The fact that she doesn't answer immediately, as she usually did, is the best proof of this.</p>
<p>
</p><p>Naturally, the student council members don't know about the relationship between the president and me.</p>
<p>Koizumi, Miura and the others must think that Shizuku wants to be involved in the negotiations with Kikyo Jogakuin.</p>

<p>Also, they know that Shizuku and Kirasaka have feelings towards Shinra Minato that go beyond the concept of a friend.</p>
<p>However, they do not know for sure whether it is as a childhood friend or as an object of observation.</p>

<p>They don't know, the relationship between me and Shizuku.</p>
<p>They don't know, the moment of encounter between me and Kirasaka.</p>
<p>
</p><p>They don't know that a rift has been building between Yuuto and Shizuku since before the summer break.</p>
<p>
</p><p>I was the one who kept them from knowing, and even if they knew, I was the one who kept them from getting in.</p>
<p>I definitely did not tell them this unnecessary information to keep the student council members engaged.</p>
<p>I assumed that if a problem arose, I would solve it myself.<br>I knew I couldn't do that.</p>

<p>Somewhere in the back of my head, I already knew.<br>I knew that my actions would have some influence on the people around me.</p>

<p>In fact, Shizuku wanted to join the student council.</p>
<p>The reason, as she stated, was to get herself a title that would be appropriate for this joint meeting.</p>
<p>The underlying reason is to be close to Shinra Minato.<br>Also, the fact that I am still unable to respond to her feelings.</p>
<p>Understanding all of this, she made her wish.<br>She knew that this action would not change my feelings.</p>

<p>“Certainly, if Kanazaki joins us, things might go smoothly. …Also, it’s not for me to worry about the future of the student council…”</p>

<p>Breaking off her words, President looks back.<br>From her eyes, she was asking Koizumi and me.</p>

<p>"Of course! I welcome Kanazaki-san to our group!"</p>

<p>Koizumi said happily.<br>Miura and Hino-kun nodded with smiles on their faces without any objections.</p>
<p>Shiraishi, who was not present at the meeting, would surely welcome her with open arms.</p>

<p>"Shinra... how about you?"</p>

<p>President turned her attention to me at the end and muttered quietly.<br>In reality, this is the last question she will ever ask me here.</p>
<p>According to the rules for the student council members, a student can be nominated for an open slot with the recommendation of the enrolled members and the approval of the teacher in charge.</p>
<p>I have no doubt that Sudo-sensei would agree.</p>
<p></p>
<p>She would be the right person in terms of ability, popularity, and everything else.</p>
<p>So, if I stop Shizuku from joining, I would be denying them all.</p>

<p>"I..."</p>

<p>I grunted and squeezed a little bit out of my throat, and my eyes met Shizuku's at the edge of my sight.</p>
<p>She was gently smiling at me.</p>
<p>
</p><p>Her eyes were filled with a strong will, as if she had made the decision on her own.</p>
<p>To be honest, I'm scared.</p>
<p>I was afraid that she might be tied down by me.</p>
<p>I have only one life, and I can never be sure that I will be able to live up to her expectations of the future.</p>

<p>My personality and beliefs negate the relationship I'm trying to build up in a vague way.</p>
<p>I will not be able to respond to her words until the day I can truly understand what it feels like to love someone on my own.</p>

<p>Until then, I am sure that she will look back on all the time she has spent with me as a waste.</p>
<p>Such useless thoughts were the only thing that passed through my mind.</p>

<p>"Minato-kun... you bad habits are coming up again, aren't they?"</p>

<p>In the silent atmosphere, Shizuku said in a gentle tone with a hint of laughter.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"You've been thinking negatively again, and you think it's your fault, don't you?</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>I couldn't say anything back.<br>She was right.</p>
<p>She has been by my side for many years and seems to know what I'm thinking at times.</p>
<p>When she pointed out my bad habit, she also shook her head.</p>

<p>"I've been thinking about this for a while. ...If Minato is in the student council, I'd like to help out. Like Minato-kun, who wants to do something as an assistant to the president, I want to do something for Minato-kun too."</p>

<p>...How's it like to be a student council member?<br>I can't help but think about it.</p>
<p>Why am I still coming up with cryptic questions in situations like this?<br>Minato-kun, you have a bad habit.</p>
<p>Having been told that much by Shizuku, I could not deny President's question.</p>
<p>I nodded slightly and conveyed my intention of approval.</p>

<p>"Then, I will ask Kanazaki to act as secretary of the student council, albeit temporarily. I will officially notify you later, after receiving approval from the faculty."</p>

<p>"Thank you!"</p>

<p>At President's words, Shizuku lowers her head dramatically.<br>Koizumi and the others applauded and Shizuku smiled and showed a little embarrassment.</p>
<p>I'm not sure if this was really the right thing to do...<br>The answer is still not clear.</p>
<p>However, if there is anything I can do now, it would be to try to avoid any regrets, regardless of the future relationship.</p>

<p>"Once again, I ask you all to work hard for the success of the Sakura Festival."</p>

<p>President asked everyone, smiling and nodding.<br>Koizumi, Miura, and Hino-kun replied and started their activities.</p>
<p>With documents in their hands, they talked about what they needed to do and started to move.</p>
<p>I watched the situation and turned my attention to the girl next to me.</p>
<p>
</p><p>She, too, seemed to be thinking about what she should do.</p>
<p>Then, President turned to other girl standing next to me, Kirasaka.</p>
<p>She asked the question openly without hiding it from her.</p>

<p>“Was it okay for Rei not to join the student council?”</p>


<p>"Me?"</p>

<p>If Kirasaka had wished to join at that moment, I wonder what would have happened.</p>
<p>There was a great possibility of that, and in fact, she might say that she would have joined too.</p>
<p>I was also interested in President's question too and turned my attention to Kirasaka.</p>

<p>"Right..."</p>

<p>She puts her hand on her chin and falls silent for a while with a thoughtful look on her face.</p>
<p>Then, as if she had come up with a bad prank, she says it with that usual wicked smile on her face.</p>

<p>"As long as I'm involved in the situation, I don't care where I am... Besides, it doesn't matter if it's mine in the end."</p>

<p>At these words, Shizuku's cheeks puffed out her cheeks, and President let out a sigh.</p>
<p>Kirasaka Rei, it seems, will not waver under any circumstances.</p>

<p>"You are totally a scary woman, you know."</p>

<p>President's words sent an involuntary shiver down my spine as well.</p>
<p></p>
