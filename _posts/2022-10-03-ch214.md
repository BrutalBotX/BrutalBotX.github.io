<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#214</h1>

<hr>
<p>October. The sun was setting sooner than usual, and the residential area was lit by pale lights when I returned home after the after-school student council activities were over.</p>
<p>I parted ways with Shizuku at the front door and stepped into my safe haven.</p>

<p>A place I return to and leave every day.</p>
<p>When I opened the door to the living room, warm light and an appetizing aroma greeted me first.</p>
<p>Soon, I hear footsteps rushing toward me after adjusting the stove in the kitchen.</p>

<p>"Welcome home, Nii-san. Thank you for your hard work today."</p>

<p>Kaede, my healing angel, runs up to me, smiles, and extends her hand in front of me.</p>
<p>I don't need to ask that if it is a gesture of receiving luggage, and I can't help but fantasize in my brain that we are like a newly married couple.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"I'm home. ...I'm going to go change first."</p>

<p>"Okay!"</p>

<p>I handed her my handbag containing all the work related things I wanted to get done today, and quickly returned to the hallway.</p>
<p>I went into my room and changed into some casual clothes, then went back to the living room and sat down on the sofa.</p>
<p>
</p><p>There is a bag by the table in front of me, from which I take out some documents.</p>
<p>My thoughts immediately switch to the document that lists last year's expenses and this year's budget.</p>

<p>I compare the budget before the first joint proposal was put forward with the revised budget and sigh at the situation, which is still very close to the margin.</p>
<p>I know that expenses are minimal for a student festival, but it is difficult to make ends meet when you are presented with a figure and told to make do with just this much.</p>

<p>It's as cold as my pocket money, which is limited every month.</p>
<p>
</p><p>As for the baseball team's cosplay game, I asked Shizuku and Yuuto to persuade them to get together a group of volunteers and we came to a consensus that they would do it.</p>
<p>The problem is the cost of the vague joint proposal.</p>

<p>There was no way to come up with a budget for this.</p>
<p>In addition to the fact that there had never been a joint event in the past, there was a vague obsession with this joint event thing.</p>

<p>Even I felt uncomfortable talking with the other party.</p>
<p>The scene that came to mind was an exchange with President and Sera.</p>
<p></p>
<p>I can only think of one person who would know the answer as close as I know it.</p>

<p>"Kaede ...what is the president of your school like?"</p>

<p>I turned to my left and ask my sister standing in the kitchen.</p>
<p>Kaede only pokes her head out and turns her gaze upwards.</p>

<p>"President Sera, is it? ...She's a good senior, but..."</p>

<p>I thought she was acquainted with Kaede from the time her name came out of Sera's mouth, but it seems that they are not that deeply acquainted.</p>
<p>She put her fingers to her temples with a difficult and groaning sound.</p>
<p></p>
<p>However, Kaede showed a surprised expression before she reached to the question.</p>

<p>
</p><p>"Why is it a woman again...?"</p>

<p>"What do you mean again?"</p>

<p>The cold gaze that was directed at me pierced.</p>
<p></p>
<p>…Again, I can't understand it.<br>Shizuku is a childhood friend and Kirasaka is a classmate.</p>
<p>The two were brought together by chance, and President was also an arranged marriage partner for the convenience of my parents.</p>
<p>This time, it isn't a relationship that Kaede should be concerned about at all since it's just a matter of information.</p>


<p>"Look, we're having a lot of discussions with your president about the school, and I would like to gather as much information as possible."</p>
<p>
</p><p>I reply to Kaede in a way that is convincing and avoids any specifics.</p>
<p>I had expected a similar exchange to take place a couple more times, but surprisingly Kaede's reaction seemed to be agreeable.</p>

<p>"I also heard through rumor that we are considering a joint event with Sakuranaoka Gakuen. So my brother in the student council is interested about that huh."</p>

<p>Kaede nodded her head in agreement.</p>
<p>It's easy to avoid the need for unnecessary explanations, but I have a feeling that something is being interpreted a little differently, but at this point I don't really care.</p>

<p>"So what kind of person is she?"</p>

<p>I asked my sister again.</p>
<p>This time, without letting her thoughts go off on a tangent, she mentioned her reputation at school.</p>

<p>"Academically, she is an impeccable person. I heard that her name was ranked quite high in the mock exams she participated in every time. Oh, just..."</p>

<p></p>
<p>As the information was steadily being disclosed, I was at a loss for words.</p>
<p>She gives a look that is hard to say and is surely not very good.</p>

<p>I turned my body toward my sister as well as my gaze to see what was going on.　</p>
<p>
</p><p>She groans while thinking, as if wondering if she should talk or not.</p>
<p>After watching her for a while, her eyes open and her expression turns serious as if she has made up her mind.</p>

<p>"I've heard rumors that Sera-senpai failed the high school exams at Sakuranaoka Gakuen and that she and Hiiragi-san don't get along."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Kaede, who spoke with difficulty, hid her head, which was sticking out from the kitchen, and resumed making dinner.</p>
<p>Kaede is probably not comfortable talking about rumors without her consent.</p>

<p>She told me because she trusted me as a family member.</p>
<p>I for one think that information should not be wasted.</p>

<p>It is hard to imagine that a student who regularly ranks high in mock exams would fail our high school.</p>
<p>I can't say that there is no possibility of a success story ...where a student hated studying in middle school, but something triggered his or her academic talent in high school. But the chance of it happening is very small.</p>

<p>In fact, it is hard to believe that someone who has risen to the position of president would not have studied hard in middle school.</p>
<p>And the fact that she doesn't get along with Hiiragi Akane is also a rumor, which I think is far from the truth.</p>
<p>
</p><p>Not everyone can get along with everyone.<br>However, there are probably very few people who dislike Hiiragi Akane.</p>
<p>That is how much she is trusted and has the talent to be well-liked.<br>It would be impossible for someone who does not know her to dislike her.</p>
<p>I remember when I was about to think that, since they have nothing in common except that they are the same age, it is just a rumor...</p>
<p>President said ...that she came in first in the last mock exam, I believe.</p>
<p></p>
<p>I heard that President was also ranked first in the national mock examinations held after she moved up to the third grade.</p>
<p>I had heard this several times during casual conversation.</p>

<p>Not only the last time, but also in the mock examinations in which President participated, she had dominated the first place as if it was a matter of course.</p>
<p>I was astonished, but I remember letting out a sigh when I realized that she was the Akane Hiiragi, that made it possible.</p>

<p>When I met Sera as a member of the student council, the eyes that were facing President were so dark that I could not read her emotions.</p>
<p>It was not that she was looking down on her, but if he had something else in mind.</p>

<p>This is speculation, a prediction, a theory that only I am convinced of.</p>
<p>But it is worth investigating, isn't it?</p>

<p>My arm moves to my pocket on its own as I think of this.</p>
<p>From the phone-book section of the phone I opened, I tapped a familiar name among a small number of people and made a call.</p>
<p>A few rings later, the person on the other end of the line picked up right away.</p>
<p>
</p>
<p>[Shinra-kun, it's nice to get a call from you.]</p>

<p>"Sorry for calling so abruptly ...Kirasaka, are you okay with the time right now?"</p>

<p>I had a question, or rather a request, for the person I called, Kirasaka Rei.</p>
<p>
</p><p>[I'm okay 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, as long as it's a call from Shinra-kun.]</p>

<p>"Is it OK all year round..."</p>

<p>Although I was taken aback by the unexpected answer, I moved on to the main topic.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"I have a favor to ask, can you look up the past few mock exams that President has participated in regarding the top performers?"</p>

<p>[I don't understand the need for it though...]</p>

<p>"It doesn't have to be all of the top students, I just need you to find out if someone named Sera participated."</p>

<p>Before I could respond to Kirasaka's words, I told her who I needed the information on.</p>
<p>Kirasaka on the phone pauses there and nothing can be heard over the phone's speaker.</p>
<p>The phone is unnaturally quiet, and I look at the screen to see if it's dead, but the screen of the call is still being displayed.</p>

<p>[You're not going to tell me you're interested in a woman called Sera, are you...?]</p>

<p>The voice I suddenly heard was a low and cold that seemed to come from the pit of her depths.</p>
<p>It is as if someone is whispering in my ear, and my whole body was covered with cold sweat.</p>
<p>I was so nervous that I felt as if a blade was being held to my throat, so even though I was on the phone, I vigorously shook my head and denied it.</p>

<p>"I'm not interested, really... I just wanted you to look up some information that we might need for the Sakura Festival, that's all."</p>

<p>[Well, that's fine. I will instruct them to collect as accurate information as possible, but that said it's difficult to gather perfect information, so do forgive me for that.]</p>
<p></p>
<p>"That's fine, please then."</p>

<p>I can hear her giggling over the phone when I requested her.</p>
<p>Kirasaka's standard of fun is something that ordinary people can't understand.</p>

<p>I was listening silently, wondering if there was something interesting about it this time as well, and just before the caller hung up, she left a short comment.</p>

<p>[You owe me one.]</p>

<p>A beeping sound repeats to let me know that the call has been disconnected.</p>
<p>Since I was the one who asked for the favor, it was only natural that I should owe her one, but when I thought about what she was going to ask me to do, I must have made a horrible deal.</p>
<p>A deal with the devil, yep that's what it was.</p>

<p>However, I spent the day with a sense of hope that the information Kirasaka would bring to me might help break the deadlock in our discussions.</p>






<p>The next day, when I entered the classroom of the third class of the second year, Kirasaka was sitting in her seat as usual.</p>
<p>She had a paperback book in her hand, and the sunlight shining through the window created a picturesque scene.</p>
<p>With me sitting next to her, the scene turns into a drawing.</p>

<p>"Here's the list of mock exams I was asked..."</p>

<p>"Sorry and thank you."</p>
<p></p>
<p>I accepted the bundle of papers offered to me and thank her.</p>
<p>Kirasaka smiles and quickly returns her gaze to her book.</p>


<p>Shizuku, who came to school with me, put her bags on her seat and then sat down on the seat in front of me.</p>
<p>By today, I have already started to lose track of whose seat is in front of me.</p>

<p>"What is this for?"</p>

<p>Shizuku asked me as I was flipping through and roughly checking the contents.</p>
<p>I took a breath after confirming the contents without giving an immediate answer.</p>
<p>Then I began to explain, on the assumption that it was just a prediction.</p>

<p>"This is a list of the results of the mock examinations President took and the results of Kikyo's Sera. I asked ...Kirasaka to look them up for me."</p>

<p>"I'm curious as to how she looked it up, but since it's ...Kirasaka-san, I dare not ask."</p>

<p>Yes, let it be like that.<br>I'm curious too, but there are some things in this world that are better not to ask.</p>
<p>Well, I guess she mentioned beforehand that the contents were not perfect over the phone call because she looked into the matter just enough not to touch the taboo.</p>
<p>So I could confirm the contents with peace of mind.</p>

<p>"The joint proposal for the festival was brought up by the other side... so I had my doubts about Sera not proposing any specific ideas."</p>

<p>"Certainly, she said the same thing over and over again, as if you she was a broken tape-recorder."</p>

<p>Nodding to Kirasaka's words, I relaxed my body.</p>
<p>Leaning my weight against the back of my chair, I look up at the ceiling.</p>
<p>I had thought that tradition was just a word and that the fact that they hadn't suggested anything else simply meant that they just wanted to throw the tedious task of creating a plan at us.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Another consultation is scheduled for after school today.</p>
<p>I had assumed that there would be no progress or regress in the talks, but the outcome could be different.</p>

<p>"It's a possibility, but around today the other side should suddenly be proposing a suggestion to move forward."</p>

<p>"...That seems hard to imagine from the way things have been going previously, is there a reason for that?"</p>

<p>Shizuku asks a perfectly valid question.</p>
<p>Everyone should have similar questions.</p>

<p>However, if Kaede's theory that the presidents of the two schools do not get along with each other is correct in a sense, then the flow of events so far should have been the flow that the other party had anticipated in advance.</p>
<p>In this pointless exchange of conversation, we were unable to come up with a concrete plan.</p>

<p>This could be seen as a lack of imagination and ability to manage the project.</p>
<p>Under such circumstances, it is easy to imagine that if the other party suddenly came up with a concrete proposal, we would be convinced and agree to it.</p>

<p>In the process of planning a joint project, it is wrong to compare the superiority or inferiority of student councils.</p>
<p>However, if deciding the superiority or inferiority is more important to the other party than anything else, it makes sense to me.</p>

<p>"Perhaps you want them to recognize that you are better than them."</p>

<p>It is difficult to understand this as a response to Shizuku's question.<br>But it is the best answer I could come up with.</p>
<p>The two girls tilted their heads curiously and looked at me.<br>I am sure this a statement they did not understand.</p>

<p>There are some things that only people who have been compared for a long time can understand.</p>
<p>Just as siblings have been compared to each other, they must have been compared to each other as the presidents of the same organization.</p>

<p>"It's childish self-satisfaction..."</p>

<p>The words I muttered out the window were lost in the noise of the classroom.</p>
<p>The two girls, who could barely hear the words, spent the rest of the day until after school without knowing the true meaning of those words.</p>
