<h1 style="text-align: center">#251</h1>
</p>
<hr />
<p style="text-align: left">Back when I had just entered middle and high school, I had been asked for similar favors.<br />Sometimes, I was approached as a mediator, while at other times, they wanted to know about us so that they could appeal to them in advance.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">The requests this time, however, were far more complicated than I had expected.<br />Simply asking someone to help with their confession could be quite a challenging endeavor.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">For someone to attract someone who can't even promote themselves, you need more than just a request.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">If you genuinely want to be seen favorably by someone you're interested in and be involved with them, you have to take action yourself.<br />But she still asked.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Confessions are significant life events, and to increase the chances of success, you need to narrow down your options and simplify your methods. But it made me wonder whether I should have even listened to her.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">I couldn't help but think that maybe I was repeating the same pattern with other students as I did with Miyashita Aya.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"Why are you asking me…?"</p>
<p style="text-align: left">In situations like this, I often throw this question out there.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">After all, it's crucial to understand why they're making this request, especially when they don't stand a chance.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">The response varied time to time, but the answer which I received this time was completely unexpected.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"Because I thought you were the only friend of Ogiwara-kun."</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"…"</p>
<p style="text-align: left">I couldn't help but wonder what she was talking about.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Yuuto's friend list was already well beyond its limits, and it was common knowledge.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">My gaze seemed to be filled with suspicion.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Then she explained further to supplement her words further.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"No, not in a bad way. I just thought you two were really close."</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"No, no, no..."</p>
<p style="text-align: left">When I thought of people looking at me like this it felt uncomfortable, it made me feel sick, like a pair of people who are close.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">When I shook my head and denied it, she burst out laughing, and for a moment, the atmosphere lightened.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">However, it quickly became tense again.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">This was no laughing matter.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"Even if I help, it won't necessarily increase your chances."</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"I know, but I just want to create a situation where we can talk alone."</p>
<p style="text-align: left">If that was the case, then there was no need for me to get involved. I was about to retract my offer before even voicing it.</p>
</p>
<p style="text-align: left">The image that came to mind was Nakayama's figure at Shuri Castle, where she had ignored Miyashita's suggestion.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Even if she came up with some reason to create a situation for them to be alone together, she probably wouldn't agree.</p>
</p>
<p style="text-align: left">However, I have to confirm the reason for not relying on the female students.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">“Is there an option of relying on Nakayama by any chance?”</p>
<p style="text-align: left">“That’s not possible, If it goes to that I might not even be able to join the group.”</p>
<p style="text-align: left">The weight of those words revealed that the complexity of the female students' friendships was even greater than I had imagined.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Perhaps they all shared this desire because they all idolized the prince-like figure?</p>
<p style="text-align: left">There is no reason nor any obligation to help.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Seeing how modestly and cautiously Miyashita approached me for this request, it seemed clear that she expected the possibility of rejection when making this request.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Although I didn’t have any intentions of getting involved with human relationships in this school trip but…</p>
<p style="text-align: left">There’s one thing that stuck me when I thought about it.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">I realized something amidst all this.<br />Maybe this was a chance to straighten things out between Shizuku and Yuuto.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Whether the reasons were sound or not, this problem concerned me as well.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Shizuku and Yuuto were still dancing around each other in a vague relationship.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Perhaps it was time for an outside intervention.<br />However, during the school trip, we were bound by group activities. Even with my influence, managing eight people for a single purpose was a bit overwhelming.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"I'm not enough by myself. If we can get someone who understands the situation a little better, we might be able to find a way to make time for a conversation during our group activities..."</p>
<p style="text-align: left">When I made this suggestion, Miyashita became silent with a thoughtful look on her face.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Since I only have limited connections, there’s only a few people I can rely on.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">I suggested, and after considering it for a moment, she agreed.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"You can talk to Kirasaka-san if you want. But what worries me are Kanazaki-san and Ogiwara-kun..."</p>
<p style="text-align: left">"You don't need to worry about them. I don't think there's anything to worry about... Besides, if you don't have solid evidence, you shouldn't have come to me in the first place."</p>
</p>
<p style="text-align: left">When Miyashita said that, the corners of her mouth turned up slightly, and she smiled.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">At that moment, I realized that I had let slip more than I intended.</p>
</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Even the real reason for this conversation seemed like a reason to confirm whether the rumours of Shizuku and Yuuto dating were true or not to someone who knew about it.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">It seems like she's a more cunning than I thought.</p>
</p>
<p style="text-align: left">But maybe you have to be a little sly to date Yuuto.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">He's such a good-natured wandering prince.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Creating a reason to get involved has been accomplished.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Now, the question is whether I really need to get involved.<br />Once again, I laughed at myself for creating reasons first and then facing my own feelings.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Miyashita looked at me strangely. After saying ‘it’s nothing’ I resumed my thoughts.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Helping someone with their love life might help me understand something I haven't understood about myself.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">So, even if it goes against my nature, taking action might not be a mistake.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">“Alright, I understand. I'll help.”</p>
<p style="text-align: left">“That's great... Thank you.”</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Perhaps relieved, Miyashita let out a sigh and relaxed her tense body.<br />Having a conversation with someone she had hardly talked to, discussing her crush, and asking for advice must have been quite nerve-wracking.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Although I felt sorry for her in this situation, I blurted out one more thing.</p>
<p style="text-align: left">“Now, there's one proposal I have right away.”</p>
<p style="text-align: left">“Eh, already?”</p>
<p style="text-align: left">Amidst her confusion and panic, we began discussing the first move.</p>
</p>
