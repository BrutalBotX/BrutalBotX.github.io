<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#154</h1>


<hr>
<p><span style="font-size:revert;background-color:var(--global--color-background);color:var(--global--color-foreground);font-family:var(--global--font-secondary);">In the living room of the Shinra household, Kaede was frowning after Shizuku's departure, looking dissatisfied.</span></p>

<p>"It's wrong to suddenly decide on an arranged marriage without even explaining it to Nii-san."</p>

<p>"..It's not wrong if it's that guy."</p>

<p>What came to mind was the face of my father, whom I had not seen in nearly half a year.<br>I was born with my father's image, and he had no expectations of me, nor any interest.</p>

<p>It was because he himself was aware, as I once was, that there are things that cannot be changed even by effort.</p>
<p>Therefore, he never expected too much from me, but only the bare minimum.</p>

<p>It is not that I dislike such a father.<br>It would have been harder for me if he had excessive expectations when&nbsp; comparing to my talented imouto.</p>
<p>So, I don't think anything of it.</p>

<p>I owe him a debt of gratitude for raising me as his son, and I love him as a family member, but I don't respect him.</p>
<p>I don't think he would want to be respected by his son either.</p>


<p>Still, it is true that I am confused by the news that came so suddenly.<br>Neither Kaede nor I have quite accepted the reality that we have been confronted with.</p>

<p>"I thought arranged-marriages were an event that used to happen a long time ago."</p>

<p>"Nii-san, shall we turn him down?"</p>

<p>Kaede, sitting next to me, asked as she took my hand.<br>I could feel a slight tremor from her firm grip.</p>

<p>"...I'm going to decline first, but I don't know how far the story has already progressed."</p>

<p>Frankly, I'm not interested.<br>I think there are probably fewer people in this day and age who would accept such a sudden offer and say, "Yes, I understand."</p>
<p>I am one of them.</p>

<p>However, I need to hear how this story came about and the disagreements between mother and father before I proceed any further.<br>The only thing I can do right now is to contact mom and ask her to take care of the remaining work.</p>

<p>"Kaede ...I'll be back in a bit."</p>

<p>"Y, yes. ...I understand."</p>

<p>"Don't worry, I'll be right back."</p>

<p>After patting Kaede, whose expression was darkened with anxiety, I got ready to go out.<br>It's just a simple change of clothes, phone, and wallet.</p>

<p>I took a back street in the neighborhood, different from the road I usually take to school, and headed for someone's house.<br>After walking about ten minutes, I pressed the intercom at the house and heard the other party's voice through the window.</p>

<p>"Yes, who am I speaking to?"</p>

<p>"It's me, Yuuto."</p>

<p>Your friendly neighborhood, Ogiwara-kun in times of trouble, Yuuto-kun the smile manufacturing machine, Yuuto-kun the woman killer.<br>Heading to the house of Yuuto Ogiwara, who had many aliases, I just nonchalantly stated his name as my own&nbsp;in front of his house.</p>

<p>"What's the matter, it's unusual for Minato to come to my house."</p>

<p>"Things have gotten a little messy."</p>

<p>I cut him off just like that, and told him the gist of the story.</p>
<p>A sudden phone call from my father, that he had been talking about an arranged marriage, and that it would be taking place in the near future.</p>
<p>When I explained this, Yuuto's expression changed as if he was surprised.</p>

<p>"You have someone ...who's willing to be your arranged marriage partner?"</p>

<p>"Oi... don't be rude ...I'm still worried about this, maybe someone quite a bit older will show up."</p>

<p>Yuuto, who uttered those remarks, laughed and started thinking, saying that he was just joking.<br>I had previously asked Yuuto to help me with the Shiraishi case, and he seemed to have a pretty good idea why I was here.</p>

<p>"You came here to talk about the Shiraishi case because you might be stuck at home on this matter, right?</p>

<p>"Thank you for being so quick."</p>

<p>If Shiraishi contacts me while I'm involved in household matters, there's a good chance I won't be able to respond.<br>At that time, I would leave it to Yuuto to handle the situation temporarily.</p>

<p>Neither Shizuku nor Kirasaka should be able to do that.<br>Those two are not compatible with Shiraishi.</p>

<p>Shizuku has a tendency to be reserved, and Kirasaka is the opposite, unreserved.<br>For Shiraishi, she is someone who anticipates the flow of conversation. But Yuuto is someone who can be flexible in his interactions, and thus, the right person for the job.</p>

<p>"I'll skip Shiraishi's story since you already knows it, but there are three possible outcomes."</p>

<p>"First one being sticking with her idea of being the student body president, the other being the executive committee ...what's the third one?"</p>

<p>"Both."</p>

<p>Hearing the third option, Yuuto smiled bitterly.</p>

<p>"It's also a choice ...isn't it?"</p>

<p>"Well, ...it's only a possibility."</p>

<p>Even the smallest possibility, as long as it came to my head, I add it.<br>We should be able to settle on one of the two main ideas, but even so I should still be prepared in case she wants to fulfill both of them.</p>
<p>So, I told Yuuto that I wanted him to contact Shiraishi to let me know what her current thoughts were on the two main ideas.</p>

<p>"I don't mind contacting her, but wouldn't it be better if ...Minato contacted her instead?"</p>

<p>"Do you think ...I'd be good at texting with girls?"</p>

<p>"Impossible."</p>

<p>Immediate response.<br>He stated clearly.</p>
<p>Then, he smiled and nodded.</p>

<p>"Ok, I'll contact Shiraishi-san from my side ...anything else?"</p>

<p>"I'll call and let you know when the situation at my house will be taken care of on my end ...If I may, I'd like to hear Shiraishi's thoughts once before that."</p>

<p>My small brain doesn't have the capacity to handle such issues for such long periods of time.<br>Besides, it would be preferable for me to be able to go about my troublesome affairs without having to think about them.</p>
<p>Yuuto said he understood, and then anxiously questioned me.</p>

<p>"Does Kanazaki-san know about ...that, uh, arranged marriage?"</p>

<p>"Oh ...I talked to her earlier. She just left saying he had something to do."</p>

<p>"I see. ...then that's fine."</p>

<p>Yuuto sighed, perhaps worried that I was keeping it a secret from Shizuku.<br>We exchanged a final goodbye and I left, asking him to contact me later.</p>



<p>"Well ...did Kanazaki-san go to Kirasaka-san's house or something?"</p>

<p>Yuuto muttered to himself as he looked at his friend's back till it was completely out of sight.</p>
<p>Leaving his words to be drowned in the sounds of residential area.&nbsp; <br>"Yosha!" he said and started contacting his kouhai to fulfill a request from his best friend.</p>
<p></p>
