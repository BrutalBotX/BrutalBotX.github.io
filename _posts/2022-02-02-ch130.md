<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#130</h1>

<hr>

<p>The station is not too far from home.<br>The three of us were walking side by side, and many people around us&nbsp; were also moving in the same direction.</p>

<p>Some of them were couples dressed in yukata, and others were little kids dressed in the same manner, rushing along happily.<br>I remembered that Kaede also used to do the same and then fall down.</p>

<p>Perhaps it was a coincidence, but Kaede seemed to remember the same thing, and muttered.</p>

<p>"It brings back memories... I used to fall down because I used to be too excited."</p>

<p>"You know, you just used to fall down when there wasn't anything to fall down on."</p>

<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>If I had to guess, I'd say she's about to fall down even now.<br>Then Yuuto spoke to me as if he envied the conversation between his siblings.</p>

<p>"Maybe if I had a sibling, I would have had more fun at home."</p>

<p>"...I won't deny it, but there are also good things about being an only child."</p>

<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>For example, they can buy you whatever you want.<br>...And then there's the fact that you can basically have it all to yourself, I guess.</p>

<p>I guess it can be both good and bad, depending on the person.</p>



<p>As we approached the station, the number of people around us increased.<br>The sidewalks, where I had no problem walking side-by-side earlier, were now lined up with people walking in lines.</p>

<p>Every once in a while, I almost bumped shoulders with someone passing by, and then at least I managed to catch sight of the clock tower, the meeting place.</p>

<p>"Perhaps the two of them would come together."</p>

<p>"Don't get your hopes up too high..."</p>

<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>It's hard to imagine them standing next to each other.<br>The two of them have hard-core incompatibility.</p>



<p>The three of us turned around, checking the time on the screen of our phones.<br>I hadn't heard what they were going to wear, but I knew that it would be hard to find them out since everyone was wearing similar clothes.</p>

<p>I wondered why young people were walking around in such similar outfits.<br>It's so hard to distinguish.</p>

<p>As I was inwardly complaining about the mass-produced clothes these days, my gaze stopped at a certain place.</p>

<p>The overwhelming difference from the people around which I have experienced many times before.<br>Those girls were so distinguished from the rest, it could never be imitated even with clothes or makeup.</p>

<p>Their appearance fascinated and attracted the gazes of the people around.<br>Perhaps because they were too well-groomed, there was no one brave enough to approach them.</p>

<p>If it had been just one person, there might have been someone who tried.<br>But with two such figures standing side by side, it would certainly not be easy to talk to them.</p>

<p>Slowly, I changed my direction and walked towards the girls.<br>They must have noticed us, and their bored expressions brightened.</p>

<p>Well, as for Kirasaka, there wasn't much change.</p>

<p>"Hello Minato-kun! And Kaede-chan and Ogiwara-kun too."</p>

<p>"Oh, ....you're wearing a yukata."</p>

<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"Yes!"</p>

<p>I glanced once again to see the Shizuku who cheerfuly replied.<br>She was wearing a white yukata with a white base and polka-dot patterns, and carrying a small purse.</p>

<p>Combined with Shizuku's white skin and bright expression, it looked great on her.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"...You've made me wait so long, you're cheeky, Shinra-kun."</p>

<p>And next to her, Kirasaka was similarly dressed in a yukata.<br>In contrast to Shizuku, the yukata with black fabric and blue flower patterns made Kirasaka look even more mature.</p>

<p>"Sorry, ...but I'm still on time with this one."</p>

<p>She continued to greet Kaede and Yuuto as she avoided my verbal attack with an apology that showed no remorse.<br>Shizuku and Kaede praised each other's appearance, and a pleasant atmosphere was formed.</p>


<p></p>
<p>When the greetings were over, Shizuku and Kirasaka looked at me in silence.<br><span style="font-size:revert;background-color:var(--global--color-background);color:var(--global--color-foreground);font-family:var(--global--font-secondary);">I immediately knew what they meant, but I felt embarrassed to put it into words.</span></p>

<p>I'm not sure what to say, but the fact that I have to say it in front of my sister and friends made it even harder.</p>

<p>"Isn't it ...good?"</p>



<p>Why did I reply with a question?</p>
<p>Why did I use a question in my response? I didn't realize that as I exposed my lack of communication skills, or my ability to use the incorrect wordings, in such a situation.</p>

<p>To hide my embarrassment, I turned my back and walked away.<br>It must have been my imagination, because I heard a voice behind me giggling.</p>








<p>"It's as lively as ever!"</p>

<p>Kaede said honestly as she looked at the people gathered at the festival.<br>It's certainly crowded.</p>

<p>In fact, it's so crowded that I'm starting to feel sick.</p>

<p>It's surprisingly nerve-wracking to walk around without bumping into people and looking obvious.<br>And since I was walking with three people who stood out even more this year, I was even more concerned about the people looking at me.</p>

<p>"The number of fireworks and the stalls increases every year, I guess."</p>

<p>"But it's a shame that they look the same every year, isn't it?"</p>

<p>Shizuku and Yuuto were having such a conversation behind me.<br>No matter how famous the festival or fireworks are, if you see them every year, you certainly won't feel the freshness.</p>

<p>Whether you think it's a waste of time or not, for me it's more difficult to find out what's different from previous years.<br>It's more difficult than looking out for mistakes.</p>

<p>"There are too many people ...maybe we should reduce..."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>What are you, a God?<br>The idea was so scary that I couldn't stop trembling...</p>


<p>But what kind of reduction were you going to do?</p>

<p>There is no doubt that something will be shot down from the sky.<br>They do that all the time on Friday Roadshow and stuff!</p>
<p>I often hear that the SNS server went down every year.</p>
<pre class="zw-paragraph heading0"><span class="EOP">(TL/N - I seriously don't know what the above 3-4 lines are on about.)&nbsp;</span></pre>


<p>While each of us were commenting on the crowds and stalls, words from a passing group reached my ears.<br>They were just some sentences, and I'm not sure what kind of person said it, but I could definitely hear it clearly.</p>

<p>"Wow, she's seriously beautiful."</p>

<p>"The two behind her are cute, and the guy next to her is hot too."</p>

<p>There is no other word that is as accurate and reliable as an opinion from a complete stranger.<br>Because there is no human relationship, personality, or other information, so they judge you just based on your appearance.</p>

<p>The fact that I was not part of their conversation is nothing to worry about now.<br>They probably don't even think that I'm a part of this group in the first place.</p>

<p>They are a group of people who are just ...extraordinary, so naturally I hear the same kind of things everywhere.</p>

<p>Normally, a sense of inferiority and disgust might swirl in one's heart.<br>One may lament the fact that they are not seen in the same way.</p>

<p>But this is what reality is, and it has always been the same.<br>It's not the first time I've been told this, it's just the first time in a long time.</p>

<p>I've often heard that people's character changes when someone has something they don't have, and I've almost forgotten that.<br>Lately, I've been living in an environment where I'm either blessed or cursed by the people around me, but either way, I've been surrounded by people.</p>

<p>Exposure is a terrible thing.<br>I had the illusion that I was in the same position as them, that I was categorized in the same way.</p>

<p>No matter what the environment, I was still the same.<br>The difference between me and them is so clear.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Perhaps it was because I was able to reconfirm this, but for some reason, only a feeling of relief remained in my heart.</p>
