<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align: center">#217</h1>
<p></p>
<hr>
<p>Outside the window, girls dressed in sailor uniforms different from those of Sakuranaoka Academy were boarding their buses.<br>After watching the last one of them get on, Hiiragi Akane turned around and opened her mouth.</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Shinra, it's not like you to compete in a stall sales, is it?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>The expression on her face seemed to be mixed with doubt.<br>It was obvious that this would happen if someone made a reckless agreement in front of me without any prior explanation.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The only thing that seems to be swirling among the student council members, including the president, is pure doubt, not blame.</p>

<p>"If I had explained it to President beforehand, she would have settled the case with ...some other compromise."</p>

<p>President will make the right choice so that the students can enjoy the festival in a just, fair and non-discriminatory manner.</p>

<p>Competitions and betting will never come out of her mouth.<br>But President's usual right choice is not the fundamental solution in this case.</p>

<p>That student called Sera will continue to be obsessed with winning against Hiiragi Akane after this.<br>I only had an unreasoned confidence in me.</p>
<p>She knows.<br>The heart of a person who desperately reaches for the back that cannot be caught up with effort</p>
<p>Even then, she kept on struggling and became distorted.<br>She is working to satisfy her personal greed at an event that is supposed to be enjoyed and built up by everyone in the same school.</p>

<p>From the perspective of the students around her, she may look different.</p>
<p>Does she appear to be a good student council president who is working energetically for a joint event between the two schools, which has never happened before?</p>

<p>However, that's all she looked like to me.<br>If I deny Sera's words, then I betray her efforts.</p>
<p>Mercilessly, without any warning, with just the results you are devastated by your lack of ability.</p>
<p>Talented people would hate this kind of thinking.<br>They are also striving. They say that it is the result of hard work.</p>
<p></p>
<p>There is probably no doubt about it.</p>
<p>The quantity and quality of effort is required for those with less talent to surpass the results that even talented people have achieved through hard work.</p>

<p>The world is not equal ...it is brutally unequal.</p>
<p>But that's the reality.</p>

<p>"Did the two of you know that Shinra was going to take some action...?"</p>

<p>President's gaze is directed at Shizuku and Kirasaka, who were standing next to me.</p>
<p>Shizuku replied apologetically to those eyes that were mixed with exasperation.</p>

<p>"I'm sorry, I just didn't stop to think that Minato-kun had his own ideas."</p>

<p>"...Rei too?"</p>

<p>"I just wanted to watch what was going to happen."</p>

<p>When asked, Kirasaka gives an answer that was typical of her.</p>
<p>Even President, who has known Kirasaka for a long time, chuckles when she hears Kirasaka's one-liner and makes no further mention of it.</p>

<p>In a room where everyone was quiet, I exchanged glances with President again.　</p>

<p>"Sera... Sera-Hikari... that's the other school's President's name..."</p>

<p>I said that while moving around the room, and taking out the materials that Kirasaka had collected from my bag.</p>
<p>The name Sera Hikari is listed under President's name on the ranking list in the documents.</p>

<p>Placing it on the desk, everyone looked down at the contents of the documents.</p>

<p>"I didn't know she was Hikari, since she only gave her surname..."</p>

<p>President nodded and said.</p>
<p>Looking back, I wonder if Sera had deliberately introduced herself using only her surname when she came to Sakuranaoka Academy, and was looking for the President's reaction...</p>
<p>It is meaningless to speculate about the past when it has passed.</p>
<p>
</p><p>“It seems that she admired and has continued to challenge you for a long time."</p>

<p>As far as Kirasaka's data is concerned, she has challenged the same mock exam at least ten times and lost.</p>
<p>I do not blame President, and only state the facts I know from the data and Sera's words.</p>
<p>President's expression changed to one of surprise for a moment after hearing my words, and then she laughed again.</p>

<p>"It's ...something to be held in high expectation."</p>

<p>From her point of view, she might be trying to say that she is not a person to be admired.</p>
<p>However, it is also true that there are people who actually admired this person and wished to surpass her.</p>
<p></p>
<p>President's mouth never uttered a word to deny Sera's feelings, and the point of contention turned to the words I had proposed to Sera.</p>


<p>"And so the next stage of the game is the cultural festival... which is not a very admirable thing..."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>This was the first time President had a strong gaze towards me.<br>Why is it so scary when some elder sister figure gets angry...?</p>
<p>Are you my Onee-san? ...I'm getting convinced of it</p>

<p>That said, I honestly haven't prepared an excuse that would be enough to make the senpai standing in front of me nod.</p>
<p>I was going to push through with momentum on the spot but now...</p>

<p>I thought about asking for some help from Hino-kun, but the tall red-haired boy in the corner of my sight started shrinking more and more.</p>
<p>I can see that he is not good with disputes.</p>
<p>Even with those looks he's like this. ...Is this what you call gap moe?</p>

<p>"What's the point of having a match and all?"</p>

<p>"If I may say so... it would motivate the students if the sales from all the stalls were to be announced and awards given."</p>

<p>"It's an event where money is involved ...and we don't want to create a sense of competition, which could inadvertently stimulate the students and cause sterile problems."</p>

<p>If this were a university, for example, it would be a different story.</p>
<p>University students have a stronger social consciousness than high school students.　</p>

<p>The only career path left for them is to become a member of society.</p>
<p>They are at an age where they have to become adults whether they want to or not.</p>

<p>But we are still high school students.</p>
<p>From an adult's point of view, we are still like children, and even President would not want to carelessly touch money-related matters.</p>
<p>The denial, which I could easily imagine, caused a moment of hesitation in me.</p>

<p>What I am trying to do is wrong and President is right.</p>
<p>Those words flashed through my mind.</p>

<p>"It's the first time we've held a joint event, and not many would behave strangely."</p>

<p>Still, I think with all my thought processes in full operation, uttering the words that came to my mind immediately.</p>
<p>No excuses will work with this person.</p>

<p>If I lie, she will see right through me.</p>
<p>Even if I throw some emotional words at her, I don't think the calm President would be swayed by those emotions.</p>

<p>"...Haa"</p>

<p>Exhale and shrug off unnecessary thoughts.<br>Don't think too deeply or too hard. ...After all, I'm just a mob-like student.</p>
<p>I don't have the energy or the driving force to motivate everyone.<br>That's why I must think this through.</p>

<p>I say what I have to say, even if it is difficult to say, calmly and without being carried away.</p>
<p>I will do what I think is right, even if it results in people not liking it.</p>


<p>"...The joint proposal may be for the convenience of the adults. But that troublesome student council president called Sera or something like that is showing a stubborn attitude because of her unintended connection with President."</p>

<p>I muttered this in a small, quiet voice.</p>
<p>President also shook her shoulders slightly and replied in a lower tone than usual.</p>

<p>"I am sorry, but there are limits to my contacts and abilities. I can't afford to deal with one-sided competitiveness."</p>

<p>...It's a fair point.</p>
<p>What President is saying is absolutely right.</p>

<p>But there is one thing that all the students here have in common.</p>
<p>The students are all feeling a sense of urgency to start preparing for the Sakura Festival without having to spend time on this kind of discussion.</p>

<p>At this stage, the work of the student council is significantly behind the original schedule.</p>
<p>Since the first day, the student council has only minimally participated in the Sakura Festival committee.</p>

<p>No matter how much effort Shiraishi and Yuuto put in at the heart of the committee, the absence of the student council in the minds of the students could lead to a sense of distrust.</p>

<p>"It's the fault of the other party who proposed the sudden collaboration, it's the fault of our faculty for being slow to make a decision, and it's a waste of time for us to be meeting in an ambiguous situation too."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Maybe they won the election against Yuuto and were trying to act like adults.</p>
<p>They probably assumed that if they acted from behind the scenes, the problem would be solved.</p>

<p>That was the result of a combination of coincidence and luck.</p>
<p>If there are so many talented students around, it is more like a high school student to push through simply with childish ideas.</p>

<p>Childishness is also a privilege that is only allowed now.<br>It's the 'it's great to be young' thing.</p>

<p>"We're going to cooperate, we're going to compete, and we're going to end the festival with a success. ...Even if they're that greedy, you can handle it, can't you?"</p>

<p>In the end, you end up leaving it to others.</p>
<p>I can't be the hero.</p>

<p>There are many people around who could be protagonists.</p>
<p>So I should be the one to support them.</p>

<p>With these thoughts in mind, without the slightest hint of a dignified remark, and even when I make a remark that I think is rather bad for humanity, I still stand tall.</p>

<p>"Haa..."</p>

<p>President puts her hand on her forehead and lets out a loud sigh.</p>
<p>I'm really sorry.</p>

<p>It's a waste of time, so I'm telling you to hurry up and end the meeting and make the cultural festival a success. That's a sigh, isn't it?</p>

<p>In the silence, with the jittery eyes directed at her from Miura and Hino-kun piercing her side, President raises her right arm and points at me.</p>

<p>"The condition of the game is not just money, go and make Sera agree to it."</p>

<p>"...Yesh."</p>

<p>After President's words, I turned on my heel.<br>I felt Shizuku and Kirasaka walking behind me as I silently left the room.<br>As I was leaving the student guidance office, President said.</p>

<p>"From tomorrow onwards, we will use the phone for communication and concentrate on our own preparations for a while. Tell them to contact me when they decide what they want to put up at the stall."</p>

<p>I turned to see President standing there, not with the tense expression she had on her face earlier, but her normal face.</p>
<p>She nods and replies with a gentle smile and a sly glance at her poorly made-up younger brother.</p>

<p>"Well, I'm off then."</p>

<p>The other party had already left the bus for the high school.</p>
<p>Making it a top priority to contact Kaede and have her wait for her President, I left Sakuranaoka Academy.</p>
