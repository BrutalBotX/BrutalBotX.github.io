<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#188</h1>

<hr>
<p><em>*Clap* *Clap*</em></p>
<p>The roars echoed as they raised their hands together in the air.<br>As a result, Koizumi was selected as the new student council president and Shiraishi was as the new vice president.</p>
<p>The two student council leaders had now been decided, and now was the time to decide on the people who would assist them.</p>
<p>
</p><p>This might be a relief for the President. She might also have jumped of joy when the decision came in favor of Koizumi.</p>
<p>That is how much she looked after Koizumi as her younger brother.</p>

<p>Currently, there are no reports of candidacy for the positions of Treasurer, which Miura is currently serving, and General Affairs, which Hino-kun is handling now.<br>If things continue as they are, and if the two of them are willing to continue, they will unconditionally continue and be on the next student council as well.</p>
<p>The problem is that Yuuto and I are running for the same position.<br>The position is assistant to the president, which I currently hold.</p>

<p>The position of secretary is vacant, and if there are no candidates, recommendations will be made with the approval of the other student council members, led by Koizumi.</p>
<p>The recommendation is only a proposal from the student council to the students to join, not the presidential quota that Akane Hiiragi had.</p>

<p>Students who receive a recommendation will have to go through a process of interviews with the student council members and other teachers in charge before joining.</p>
<p>The workforce problem can be filled in through recommendation, in the worst-case scenario.</p>
<p>
</p><p>So, I only need to concentrate solely on the election.<br>After parting with Shiraishi and learning from Shizuku and Kirasaka about the reactions to the president's decision within the school, I was on my way home.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"It looks like they're officially announcing Minato-kun's and Ogiwara-kun's candidacy tomorrow at noon."</p>
<p>
</p><p>Shizuku told me, holding a form with the recruitment guidelines for the council election in her hand from somewhere.</p>
<p>Arere~, that's funny~<br>The voice of a famous boy detective ran through my mind...</p>
<p>Why does she have it and I don't...?<br>Facing the biggest mystery of the day, I switched my thoughts and started thinking.</p>

<p>"We don't even have time for the sports festival. ...And since the president and vice president are elected, the general framework and direction of the organization is determined, and now it's only a matter of choosing people who fit."</p>

<p>"Is it only me or are the two applicants doesn't seem to fit in at all?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>From the other side from where Shizuku was, Kirasaka spoke calmly.</p>
<p>Really, I would appreciate it if you would stop poking at the exact areas that people are concerned about.</p>
<p>
</p><p>I would have talked to the president if it was possible, but that person seems to be busy today.</p>
<p>However, she is busy discussing the future of the incoming student body president and vice president with the teachers.</p>
<p>So here we are, the three of us, walking home together along a road, with little more than houses and fields, and the atmosphere not as awkward as it used to be.</p>
<p>Since everyone's focus was on the elections, we were able to talk about it without unnecessary additions.</p>

<p>"That's the problem, isn't it... because, aside from Ogiwara-kun, Minato-kun is completely out of the 'ideal' idea..."</p>

<p>"Don't look at me like you're sorry."</p>

<p>Both Koizumi and Shiraishi, although they had different approaches, thought of cooperation as the foundation.</p>
<p>That's why I am a completely different species from the 'ideal', as I would be far from cooperative.</p>
<p>Kirasaka gives me a similarly sorry look.<br>Stop! Don't ...look at me with those eyes!</p>

<p>If I turn to the left or to the right, my gaze will eventually cross with theirs, so I I just turned back in the opposite direction.</p>
<p>After walking a few steps ahead of me, when I heard them giggling and laughing, I then turned around with a sigh.</p>

<p>"The students would naturally think so, but it's too risky to let a student with more influence than the student body president into the organization in the first place..."</p>

<p>Because he's that soft-hearted, friendly, smile-making machine.<br>'Make the school better for everyone!' He's absolutely the guy who could say that...</p>

<p>I wonder if there is anyone in the student council who could stop him when he clashes with his own ideas head-on with the president and the organization along with him...</p>
<p>The truth is that it will be difficult for the next student council without Akane Hiiragi.</p>
<p>Koizumi, Shiraishi and Miura are all excellent.</p>
<p>Hino-kun is well... Hino-kun...</p>

<p>Regardless, Ogiwara Yuuto, with a person to serve as a suppressor for his actions, this possibility is close to a gamble.</p>
<p>I guess that risk is not something I could deny. But I do also have a chance of winning.<br>If I think about the faculty votes, it certainly is the most effective way go at it.</p>
<p>Besides, tomorrow is the big day, and today is just a strategy meeting.<br>If I start thinking in the dark now, there is no way I could successfully carry out the battle that follows.</p>

<p>"Well, well, ...what shall we do?"</p>

<p>"Are? I here thought that Shinra-kun already had a plan with a high winning possibility."</p>

<p>"It's not that it's a way to win, it's a way to be able to seize that low possibility which is already present. ...I can't exactly call it a plan if there's an extremely low chance of success, so I'm going to need you both to help me out."</p>

<p>I walked about with a thinking look on my face as I told them about my future plans, which I had not been able to explain properly in the student council room.</p>
<p>The two never spoke any words and just looked forward without making eye contact.</p>

<p>"...Yuuto will definitely take the straightforward route in his campaign, because even if he gathers information from his sources and devises a strategy, in the end, the best way for him to win is to go for it head-on."</p>

<p>The ability to gather information, connections, and capacity to win through a wide network of students through those of the lower ranks.<br>If he goes out with a similar strategy against me, the winner is obvious.</p>
<p>And, since I don't have that kind of network or recognition, this won't even be a competition anymore.</p>

<p>"You said you didn't want to put us in the forefront, but was it because you are still in opposition to Shiraishi-san's idea after all?"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Shizuku asked me.</p>
<p>From her point of view, they may want me to rely on them without worrying about what I said to Shiraishi.</p>
<p>Kirasaka must have wondered the same thing since she remained silent without interrupting.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"That's part of it.. also, because having the two of you on the top of being the one to get the faculty votes would raise a lot of questions."</p>

<p>"Likewise... does that mean that Shinra-kun, with the two of us behind him, might end up having a strong influence within the organization?"</p>

<p>"Well, something like that."</p>

<p>That's what president said.<br>The reason she made me join the student council was because I am a person who isn't influenced by the opinions of either the students or the council.</p>
<p>Since I was one of the few students who judged things from a third-party's perspective, I fulfilled president's requirements.</p>

<p>I don't know if she was right or wrong in pointing this out.<br>However, even assuming I end up joining the next student council, what I could do is similar.</p>

<p>The student council, which is supposed to be in the link between the school and the students, should always be a third party so that it does not lean toward one side or the other.<br>Koizumi is adored by the students, and Miura supports him.</p>
<p>Shiraishi is a calculating person who is liked by both students and faculty, and the current organization is just barely on the student side.</p>
<p>If Yuuto joins, it will definitely be transformed into an organization for students.</p>

<p>I am not saying that it is wrong.<br>However, I wonder if it is correct.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"I don't have any firm commitments or convictions to say. ...I just want to be a neutral figure in the organization and act as a counterweight to prevent any biased opinions."</p>

<p>So, I continued, addressing the two of them.</p>

<p>"So ...I want you two to support me, and if the faculty members think even a little that even you both are recommending me, Shinra Minato, my words might reach them as well."</p>

<p>The same goes for the president.</p>
<p>In order to make sure that my words don't end up with the students snickering at me, I'll make sure that my words are heard by showing that I have a connection with the president to both the students as well the faculties.</p>
<p>From there, my election to the student council will really take off.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"With that, I present my request to both of you."</p>
<p>
</p><p>I announced again, taking a few steps ahead of them and turning around.</p>
<p>Shizuku clasped her hands together behind her body and Kirasaka crossed her arms as if urging me to continue.</p>
<p>What should I do...? I know what I want to say, but the words will inevitably sound strange to them.</p>
<p>But I cannot not say it.</p>

<p>"That's why from now on I want you to stay by my side, not Yuuto's..."</p>
<p>
</p><p>"Is this a proposal?"</p>

<p>"Are you proposing?"</p>

<p>"No."</p>

<p>Why is it that only at times like these they are so in sync?</p>
<p>Nevertheless, I'm short on words.<br>I'll try to supplement it and explain my intentions clearly.</p>

<p>"During elections, we set up a simple base with desks behind the speaker, right? I'd like you to sit there..."</p>

<p>What we do at that desk is put up our manifestos.</p>
<p>Koizumi and Shiraishi's bases had a staff member who would distribute them to students who asked for support.</p>
<p>I want the two of them to be sitting there.</p>
<p>Although it would be a little far away, I wanted to create a silent perception among the students that the two people helping me are my supporters.</p>
<p>However, I would only ask the two of them to respond to questions from the students that they are assisting me.</p>
<p>It makes no sense to make it clear that they are fully supporting me, which would only bring in their popularity votes.<br>I would not be able to proudly say that I had won over Yuuto.</p>
<p></p>
<p>It is only to make sure that the students cannot ignore my words.<br>After that, I would have to make the second and third moves to make up the handicap little by little to win the game.</p>

<p>When I told the girls that I wanted them to stay by my side with this intention, they nodded in agreement.</p>

<p>"I understand... and I'll do anything I can to help you together by your side, Minato-kun!"</p>

<p>"Thanks to you ...I'm saved."</p>

<p>"I don't mind either, and would you like me to stand by your side 24/7 with our arms linked together?"</p>

<p>"Ah, no, that's fine."</p>

<p>Kirasaka said with a wicked smile on her face, and I immediately refused.<br>However, since that was the extent of her teasing, she smiled after a giggle.</p>
<p>It is crucial that I can officially entrust the two of them to help me in this way.</p>
<p>Now I can effectively direct the president as my second arrow and Shiraishi as my third.</p>

<p>"...Then, I'll buy you something at the shopping district."</p>

<p>"Really?"</p>

<p>"I've never had a skewer before. ...I'll get one at Oyasan's."</p>

<p>Both of their eyes lit up when I said I'd treat them.</p>
<p>...Gluttons</p>
<p>After giving a wry smile with a hint of amazement, I followed their backs as they walked enthusiastically to the shopping district, where the day came to an end.</p>
