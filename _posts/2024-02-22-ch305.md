<h1 style="text-align: center">#305</h1>
<p> </p>
<hr />
<p>“Shinra… hey, are you even listening, Shinra!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The stern voice of the most veteran history teacher in the grade echoed not only in the classroom but also out into the corridor.</p>
<p>Realizing for the first time that I was being called, I instinctively raised my eyes and shook my shoulders.</p>
<p>It felt like being startled suddenly by something nearby.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Ah… sorry.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As both my classmates and the teacher’s slightly displeased gaze were directed at me, it was a typical scene of a teacher scolding a student not paying attention in class.</p>
<p>However, it had been a long time since I found myself in the same position.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>While not entirely focused, I usually made an effort to appear less conspicuous than my other classmates.</p>
<p>I approached the class with the seriousness to answer whenever called upon.</p>
<p>But today, my textbook was open to a page unrelated to the content on the board, and my notebook remained blank, unaware of what was being asked or pointed out.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Glancing forward in the classroom, I unexpectedly caught sight of Shizuku, who puffed her cheeks as if to say to pay attention and Yuuto, whose mouth turned into a grin.</p>
<p>A sigh escaped Kirasaka next to me, and chuckles could be heard throughout the class.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…From now on, focus on the lesson. Open your book to page 76.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I’m sorry…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Opening the designated page and contemplating my own answer to the question asked, the teacher nodded in approval.</p>
<p>With the class returning to normal without further incident, no one continued to watch me.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shifting my gaze to my notebook amidst the returned class atmosphere, only unrelated numbers were scrawled across it.</p>
<p>When the bell signalling the end of class rang, the teacher left, leaving assignments behind.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Released simultaneously, students gathered in small groups with friends, engrossed in trivial conversations.</p>
<p>Amidst this, I stood up to buy a drink from the vending machine to ease my cramped and rigid thoughts.</p>
<p>As I left the classroom and walked down the corridor, I heard light footsteps approaching from behind.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“It’s rare to see Minato getting scolded by the teacher.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…And the guy who seemed to enjoy it is right next to me.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Well, if a guy who seems to be clueless most of the time gets scolded out of the blue, it’s hard not to laugh.”</p>
<p>Remembering and laughing, Yuuto poked my right side with his finger and teased.</p>
<p>I brushed his hand away, increased my pace, and distanced myself from him.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>During the short break, there are limited places to go, so whether I distance myself or not, I end up in the same place. I buy cold tea despite it being winter and gulp it down to soothe my throat.</p>
<p>…Will this help cool my head a bit? No, probably not. I understand that physically chilling won’t change much, but it’s just a way to comfort myself.</p>
<p>The situation is not favourable at all.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It’s been two days since I talked to Kirasaka, but there has been no progress. While Hino-kun and I confirmed details like the positions of students, the layout of the stage, and where the teachers would stand using diagrams in the gym, we were stuck on the main content.</p>
<p>To respect the students’ wishes, the teachers didn’t interfere. But this freedom was a disadvantage in this case.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>With fewer people, the opinions of experienced teachers could serve as a valuable reference compared to our own ideas. Even if it’s similar to previous years, the words and opinions of those who have interacted with the current students are valuable.</p>
<p>We need at least two more people, preferably three, if possible.</p>
<p>Yuuto, who bought warm tea from the vending machine, leans against the wall and looks at me. After giving me a meaningful glance as if pondering something, he speaks up.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Well, it’s partly because we can’t tell what you’re thinking.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…Are you here to praise me or something?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It’s nauseating. Please stop.</p>
<p>If this were a shoujo manga, his eyes would sparkle, his face would sparkle, and even his dialogue would sparkle. It’s like a storyline straight out of a bygone era with shoddy artwork.</p>
<p>Mina Yuuna or was it Yuu(na)Mina? I’m just curious about that, can you tell me?<br />&lt;BBX: A bad pun about magical girls Mina and Yuuna being famous, smh&gt;</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Naturally, Yuuto didn’t come all the way to the vending machine just to say something embarrassing like that. I turn my gaze to Yuuto, who is elegantly enjoying his tea time. Then, his voice echoes softly in the corridor.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“It must be tough, preparing for the farewell party.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Not easy.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“It’s enough to get you called out by the teacher during class, right?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Without hesitation, I reply, and Yuuto chuckles while poking fun at me.</p>
<p>He stares at the sip of tea, his slightly dishevelled uniform, brown hair, and the ear with an unusual ear cuff glinting.</p>
<p>…He’s annoyingly put together, looking like a stylish high school boy.</p>
<p>However, surprisingly, his tone gives off a sense of casualness, as if he just wanted to confirm something factual.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“What, did you think I came to help?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Yuuto smirked and said, twisting his mouth.</p>
<p>It’s somehow infuriating that I imagined the scene according to his liking.</p>
<p>I couldn’t come up with any words to retort, thinking that my anxiety must have been evident to others.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I won’t ask for the reason, but you want to do this alone, right?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>With a distance that is understood without words, considering the longer relationship compared to other students.</p>
<p>He neither gets too close nor pushes me away.</p>
<p>The sense of distance with Yuuto that I had almost forgotten.</p>
<p>It made me feel a sense of familiarity with the old me, as the distance with him seemed to have settled.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“However, doing nothing about an interesting problem isn’t an option. Let me give you one piece of advice, Minato.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Raising a finger and puffing his chest slightly, Yuuto looked at me with a slightly pompous air.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“What Minato dislikes is generally liked by most students, and what Minato likes is generally disliked by most students… Even things other than preferences are somewhat similar.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“What’s that… some kind of riddle?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I couldn’t help but ask back, and Yuuto, satisfied, wore a wicked smile and turned away.</p>
<p>He finished the remaining half of his tea, then threw the can into the nearby trash bin like shooting a basketball, and headed back to the classroom alone.</p>
<p>The final smile seemed to anticipate what kind of response I would give to his advice, like a boyish smile.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“What’s up with that guy…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I feel like I understand, but still can’t quite grasp the essence of who Ogiwara Yuuto is.</p>
<p>Doesn’t that way of speaking make me seem like a contrary complainer?</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Well… In general, it might.</p>
<p>Despite being called advice, I still don’t quite understand the true meaning. But it’s almost time for class according to my internal clock.</p>
<p>Using my smartphone, which usually only comes out to check the time, I confirm the time. The bell for the start of class will ring in three minutes.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>…This is bad, I won’t make it unless I run back.</p>
<p>Yuuto may have left stylishly, but he must be dashing back from somewhere out of sight.</p>
<p>I was late because I was caught up in watching him leave.</p>
<p>As I was about to put my smartphone back in my pocket and start running, something caught my attention, and I stopped to stare at my phone.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>[ What Minato dislikes is generally liked by most students, and what Minato likes is generally disliked by most students ]</p>
<p>Yuuto’s words replayed in my mind.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Ah…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As if a tangled thread in my mind had finally unravelled, I felt like I understood the meaning of Yuuto’s words a little.</p>
<p>In that moment, I instinctively tapped a contact on my phone and made a call.</p>
<p>The person answered after a few rings, and behind them, the typical noisy classroom voices flowed in as background music.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Hey, Hino-kun, it’s about your smartphone.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Smartphone? What’s going on all of a sudden?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>That’s right, Yuuto’s words were actually quite spot-on advice.</p>
<p>Thanks to his indirectness, considering that I might dislike blatant cooperation at the moment, I was able to realize something.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Perhaps, even if the true meaning of his words were clearly stated, I might not have taken it seriously and might have thought of various negatives.</p>
<p>So, let’s be grateful. I won’t hesitate to express my gratitude, even resorting to the last resort of Cheese-flavoured Karaage-kun.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I’ll explain to Hino-kun in detail after school, but finding a foothold to solve the current problem might have just been discovered, which made a smile creep onto my lips unintentionally.</p>
