<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align: center">#232</h1>

<hr>
<p>Shizuku and Kirasaka stood next to each other glancing over at a board set up in the middle of the two student council tents.</p>
<p>There, one black line was drawn in the middle to separate the board.</p>

<p>It was made so that students who have purchased the delicacies offered by the student councils of both schools could vote for their favorite based on their opinions.</p>
<p>The rows of stickers lined up in a mess on either side of the line were currently receiving a similar number of votes.</p>

<p>"Hmm...surprisingly the other side is not too far off."</p>

<p>Shizuku puts her hand on her chin and murmurs.</p>
<p>Kirasaka also looked at the number of votes and the current queue in front of the stores, and looked like thinking about something.</p>

<p>I looked at the group of students surrounding the two tents as well, but it appeared the ours had a bit more than theirs.</p>
<p>However, it won't be much at the peak time.</p>

<p>After lunch, it would be okay to take turns and take a break just as the crowds began to shift to the staple food stores.</p>

<p>For some reason, the order was decided between the girls standing discussing&nbsp;&nbsp;something, and Kirasaka was to take a break at the same time as me.</p>
<p>...I want to insist on individual freedom.</p>

<p>However, such opinions are irrelevant to these girls.</p>
<p>They are irregular creatures, sometimes forceful and bold in their actions.</p>
<p>As expected of the princess, the queen, and the general manager.</p>

<p>From now on, I would like to propose to the students the idea of calling them 'The Rulers'.</p>

<p>Perhaps already having their sights set on the Sakura Festival, the pamphlet held in Kirasaka and Shizuku's hands had several circles on it.</p>
<p>...So, are you going to visit all of them?</p>

<p>Time is of course of the essence, but Minato's wallet is going to scream.</p>

<p>Of course, there's no way I'm going to pay for all of them...</p>
<p>Minato-kun quality there, please bear your own expenses.</p>

<p>"Well, after you, I'm off to take a break."</p>

<p>"Have a good time."</p>

<p>The president, on behalf of the student council who are still working in the tent said that.</p>
<p>The rest of the members also smiled and waved at us.</p>

<p>Kirazaka also gives a small nod and walks past me, plucking the sleeve of my uniform with her fingertips.</p>
<p>I won't run away, you know? I'm not stupid, okay?</p>

<p>The question of whether or not the word 'not stupid' conveys the message came to mind, but I'm not going to worry about that.</p>

<p>"Where are we going...?"</p>

<p>"Then..."</p>

<p>I asked Kirasaka, who was still proceeding despite the attention of the male students.</p>
<p>Kirasaka breaks off her words, releases the sleeve of my uniform she was pulling on, and opens the pamphlet.</p>
<p>With her thin white fingertips, she points to a few stores.</p>

<p>"We're going to buy a drink and some light food, and then go to the special place."</p>

<p>"...Special place?"</p>

<p>Was there such a place set up at this festival?</p>
<p>There is no place that comes to mind when I tried to recollect it from my memory.</p>
<p>However, Kirasaka resumed her steps without offering any special explanation.</p>

<p>The way I walked behind her looked as if an ordinary citizen following a celebrity walking down the street.</p>
<p>Well, I do realize that people around me probably see me that way.</p>

<p>It is a problem that naturally follows you when you work with Shizuku, Kirasaka, and others, including Yuuto, who is not here.</p>
<p>It is a waste of mental effort to be concerned about it.</p>

<p>I brush away the depressing glare along with the bangs that are beginning to grow.</p>
<p>...I cannot do the same for the stares, though.</p>


<p>I followed Kirasaka as she went along and arrived at the drink stall.</p>
<p>The sign says 'Tapioca' in large letters, so I didn't have to wonder what the main attraction was.</p>
<p>While I was thinking about what to order from the small menu list, Kirasaka was the first to order.</p>

<p>"Special deluxe coconut milk with brown cane sugar and tapioca topping, please."</p>

<p>"...A weapon? Or is that some kind of special equipment?"</p>

<p>"At this point, it's almost like a spell that can only be understood by women, like 'vegetables with lots of garlic and oil, extra caramelized'..."</p>

<p>I was the only one puzzled, and both the store's receptionist and Kirasaka herself finished handing over the goods as a matter of course.</p>

<p>At any rate, I order something called tapioca milk tea and proceed to the next store.</p>
<p>After purchasing yakisoba, takoyaki and other such thing, our last stop was under a tree in the courtyard.</p>

<p>To put it nicely, this was where Kirasaka and I met.</p>
<p>I looked where Kirasaka was going and noticed it on the way.</p>
<p>Indeed, this is the only place where she would call a special place.</p>
<p>There were many stalls in the nearby plaza, and the place was no longer as quiet as usual.</p>

<p>However, there was one place that was so empty that I felt uncomfortable of us both.</p>

<p>The reason was that a certain gentleman was standing there.</p>
<p>Dressed in a neat black tailcoat and meditating in the midst of the bustle, everyone paid attention to the figure, but he was moving away.</p>
<p>Kirasaka approached the figure and called out to him.</p>

<p>"Thank you, Ji."</p>

<p>"Ojou-sama, please call me if you need anything."</p>

<p>Kuroi-san, whom I have spoken to several times, gives a small bow to Kirasaka and quietly turns back and disappears from sight.</p>
<p>In doing so, he bowed to me as well, and I bowed back to him in a puzzled manner.</p>
<p>His deep facial expression turns into a gentle smile, and I get a glimpse of this person's kindness.</p>

<p>"Here, huh..."</p>

<p>Standing at the base of the tree, I look above and mutter.</p>
<p>The sunlight leaks through the trees, and the sound of leaves rustling in the wind spreads a sense of distance from the noise of the surrounding area.</p>

<p>"I'm not particularly interested in anything but this place."</p>

<p>Willing to sit down directly on the grass in her uniform, Kirasaka lightly taps next to her, urging me to sit down.</p>
<p>Close, I think, and sit down one space away from where she tapped, and started gulping down the drink I had bought.</p>

<p>We did not speak to each other, and a quiet moment passed between us.</p>
<p>I met her at this place at the entrance ceremony, which was sometime a year and a half ago.</p>

<p>Looking back, time passes quickly, and it is difficult to ask myself if it was a fruitful time.</p>
<p>The days were long spent lazily, and now that I have had the opportunity, I am able to stay busy, but this festival will be a turning point for me.</p>

<p>As I was pondering these thoughts, shocked to find that the tapioca in my mouth was almost tasteless, Kirasaka, sitting next to me, said in a voice tinged with melancholy.</p>

<p>"While the students and myself are changing, this place remains the same."</p>

<p>Her hand on the tree's bark was gentle, and the expression on her face seemed to be one of nostalgia.</p>
<p>The meaning of "unchanged" is probably not a change in its physical appearance.</p>
<p>From this place where the tree has its roots, it stands facing the sky without moving.</p>

<p>Even in the absence of the students' interest, this place will never change.</p>
<p>It will be more precious for a long time than the temporary memories that fade and disappear.</p>

<p>"But ...things that we want to change do not work out conveniently, do they?"</p>

<p>"...Sorry for being a tricky creature."</p>

<p>The words themselves sound like a negative statement, but the grinning expression at the corners of her mouth and the teasing tone of her voice prompts me to reply as usual.</p>
<p>I am aware of it, I can't help my own little change and stubbornly twisted personality.</p>

<p>I take a bite of the takoyaki offered to me silently, and the familiar taste of store-bought sauce spreads in my mouth.</p>
<p>The fact that the restaurant does not make any attempts to make the takoyaki more unique makes me feel at ease, which I like.</p>

<p>"As my father said, it's up to our actions rather than anything Shinra-kun does ...so I'd be happy if you just stay the same."</p>

<p>"...Contradictory, isn't it?"</p>

<p>"I want you to change, but I don't want your essence to change as well. I'm aware of my own selfishness."</p>

<p>After a short statement, Kirasaka also puts a takoyaki into her mouth.</p>
<p>I remember her father's words as I leaned my back against a tree that had been vacated for one person.</p>

<p>It's not just me, it's all the people around me that are going to change as well.</p>
<p>I do not doubt it, but I wonder if that really is true.</p>

<p>I think that I won't change, that I can't change.</p>

<p>But as for Kirasaka, even her father has started to get involved, and to be honest, there is one thing that is troublesome.</p>
<p>That is how to call her.</p>

<p>Kirasaka, and the same Kirasaka, two people respond when I call them by their surnames.</p>
<p>However, I'm not an employee to call him director, and calling him father is uncomfortable.</p>

<p>"Rei...Rei-san?"</p>

<p>Would it be more natural to call her the daughter, since I might be seen as a pompous if I called her young lady?</p>
<p>He is the president of a large company, I must be careful not to get my existence erased because of my poor behavior.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"What was that?"</p>

<p>As I mumble and mutter in a small voice, Kirasaka next to me stares at me with wide eyes.</p>
<p>She is surprised, but when she stares at me with eyes so big, I can't help but brace myself.</p>

<p>Well, I am confident that if we fought, I would lose in an instant.</p>
<p>It is more likely the case of me being protected, not the other way around.</p>

<p>"No... nothing."</p>

<p>When I question the gaze directed at me, she quickly looks away.</p>
<p>I won't tell you that the coconut milk drink, which was supposed to be sipped and enjoyed slowly, rapidly lost its volume, reminding me of the suction power of a certain vacuum cleaner.</p>

<p>The unchanging suction power ...seems to be produced by Kirasaka's company.</p>

<p>For a while, Kirasaka turned her body away and did not speak.</p>
<p>After eating the food she had bought with a gulp, she finally stood up silently.</p>

<p>"I'll go back first..."</p>

<p>"...Then, shall we head back?"</p>

<p>Without thinking, I attempted to head back without any other intention and stood up, but she held her palm out as if to stop me.</p>

<p>"No, I'm going to call Kanazaki-san, so you definitely won't come with me."</p>

<p>...Not even eye contact and rejection is that it? Or is it a declaration of farewell?</p>
<p>If that happens, I guess I will have to get her in a good mood again because my life could be in danger.</p>

<p>Although I understand that it is not true, I froze involuntarily at the sudden change in response, and Kirasaka left this place with an agile movement she rarely shows.</p>

<p>She fluttered past a group of students with a stunning maneuvering motion and disappeared from the courtyard.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>From the outside it would look like the picture of a confession made in the atmosphere of a cultural festival, and then cruelly rejected.</p>

<p>A student as bright as Kirasaka suddenly showed such agility that naturally, the eyes of those around her were drawn to me, who was the origin of those movements.</p>

<p>I stealthily moved behind the tree to hide from their gazes.</p>
