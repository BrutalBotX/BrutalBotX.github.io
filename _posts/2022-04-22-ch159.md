<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#159</h1>

<hr>
<p>I put on my shirt as usual and then wore a navy blue tie.</p>
<p>After putting on a blazer, I checked in the mirror to see if there was anything wrong with the outfit.</p>

<p>There weren't any problems; there was the normal Shinra Minato figure.<br>The eyes without any energy showed the day's unnecessary worries.</p>

<p>"...Are you sure you want to wear your school uniform?"</p>

<p>"There isn't any problem with it, in fact uniforms are the most formal attire for a high school student."</p>

<p>"I don't think so..."</p>

<p>Dad replied as he was getting himself ready for the day.</p>
<p>The real reason for this is the fact that there are many people who are interested in the idea of me wearing a suit.</p>

<p>To be honest, my knowledge of arranged marriage meetings is limited to what I have seen on TV dramas.</p>
<p>Putting aside the question of whether the information was correct or not, I certainly felt a little uncomfortable going in my school uniform.</p>

<p>Once again, I checked my appearance in the mirror and walked out through the front door.<br>The strong summer sunlight momentarily blinded my vision, but it soon recovered.</p>

<p>A car was parked in front of the entrance.<br>It was not the Shinra family's car, but a long, black car prepared by Kirasaka for our transportation.</p>

<p>Why do rich people buy long cars?</p>
<p>This also had the potential to be a subject for independent research...</p>


<p>Anyways, right after Kirasaka offered to attend the meeting, she suggested that the car for pick-up and drop-off would be provided by the Kirasaka family.<br>We accepted it leniently and got into the car parked right in front of the entrance.</p>

<p>"Good morning, Shinra-kun ...your eyes appear to be 20% less bright than usual."</p>

<p>"Good morning ...you're not wrong, so I can't really say anything about it."</p>

<p>Kirasaka, who was waiting in the car, was wearing a black dress.<br>The appearance was gleaming as if it fully expressed the fact that she was from a wealthy family.</p>

<p>It is not surprising that she is the daughter of a wealthy family, but after seeing Kirasaka's normal appearance, it was unusual for her to look like a young lady.</p>
<p>After moving my attention next to Kirasaka, I saw Shizuku with an embarrassed look on her face.</p>

<p>She was wearing a white dress similar to Kirasaka.<br>The white color suits Shizuku, so it is only natural that it looks good on her.</p>
<p>If Yuuto saw this, he would go crazy.</p>

<p>When they saw that I had confirmed their appearance, they looked at me expectantly.</p>
<p>I wondered if they wanted me to tell them what I thought of them.</p>

<p>"You two should be in plain clothes."</p>

<p>I told them so, and they let out a sigh of disappointment.</p>
<p>I am amazed at the fact that they are more dressed up than I am. After that my father and mother got into the car.</p>

<p>They are both dressed in formal wear and ready to go.</p>
<p>The car starts to drive in the opposite direction from the road we usually walk on.</p>

<p>It takes only 10 minutes by car to get to the place where the inn is located near the girls' high school that Kaede attends.</p>
<p>This car ride is certainly not a friendly one.</p>

<p>Dad and Mom were looking at the scene outside with serious faces, and Shizuku was checking on her reflection in a hand mirror occasionally.</p>
<p>Only, Kirasaka remained the usual.</p>

<p>"Kaede-chan didn't come, did she?"</p>

<p>Kirasaka muttered that in the quiet atmosphere.</p>

<p>"Oh ...it's not something the whole family would go to, and Kaede wasn't keen on it either."</p>
<p>
</p>
<p>Shizuku, sitting next to Kirasaka, opened her mouth next.</p>

<p>"Minato-kun, there should be a Q&amp;A session as is customary in arranged marriages and the like, so let's do a little prep work!"</p>
<p></p>
<p>"I don't need that kind of preparation..."</p>

<p>I don't know if my words were getting through to her, but Shizuku took out her phone, typed in something, and started asking me questions one after another.</p>

<p>"What are your hobbies?"</p>

<p>"Reading."</p>

<p>"What are your special skills?"</p>

<p>"Napping."</p>

<p>"What would you like to do in the future and why?"</p>

<p>"Working from home... so that I don't have to leave my house and there won't be overtime or time wasted on commute."</p>

<p>"It's all nice because it'll surely give a negative image to the other person!"</p>

<p>Eh, are you sure this is okay?<br>I answered it myself, but I'm too unmotivated to argue with her... there's even a point where the other party would get mad at me.</p>

<p>Kirasaka, who was sitting next to her, just nodded deeply and did not say anything.</p>
<p>My father let out a sigh and my mother watched our exchange with a happy smile on her face.</p>

<p>What is this chaotic situation?<br>This chaos...</p>

<p>I wonder if it's because I still have the childish mind, the one that made me feel a little cool when I first changed the kanji into katakana.<br>While I was thinking such meaningless thoughts, the car gradually slowed down and entered the basement of a building.</p>

<p>My heart started to beat faster from nervousness.<br>I am not the type of person who usually gets nervous, but I can't help it.</p>

<p>I'm also a human.<br>I am not so strong that I won't get nervous when I encounter a situation for the first time.</p>

<p>When the car came to a halt, a guy got out of the driver's seat and opened the door of the back seat.<br>Kirasaka got out first, then my parents, then Shizuku, and finally me.</p>

<p>Three people who appeared to be employees of the inn were waiting for us, and when they saw that we had gotten out, they walked up to us.</p>

<p>"We were waiting for your arrival, Shinra-sama, and I will show you around."</p>

<p>A woman led the way and we went up from the basement to the hall on the first floor.<br>There were a few guests in the hall, and the calmness of the room slightly eased our tension.</p>

<p>"Shinra!"</p>

<p>As we were walking behind the employee, a man called out to us from the front.<br>The man, who looked a little older than my father, smiled and approached my father.</p>

<p>"Senpai, sorry to keep you waiting."</p>

<p>"Never mind, it's just that me and my daughter got here early ...You must be Minato-kun?"</p>

<p>His gaze shifts from dad to me.</p>

<p>This man should be the father of the other party.<br>I bowed and answered him, and then moved my eyes to observe him carefully.</p>

<p>Then, with a smile on his face, he turned to dad and said.</p>

<p>"He's just like you once were, Shinra."</p>
<p>
</p><p>"That's because he's my son."</p>

<p>It was refreshing to see my father speaking in honorifics.<br>It was unusual to see him talking about his work and his relationships with other people, as he did not often talk about them at home.</p>

<p>After a brief conversation, the he cuts to the chase.</p>

<p>"Normally, we would probably talk with the parents as well, but in our case, I don't think that's necessary ...I've prepared a separate room for Minato and my daughter, so I'd like you to talk to her there to your heart's content."</p>

<p>"Just the two of us?"</p>

<p>"I've heard from Kotone-san about your friends, but I'd like you and my daughter to talk alone first. I'll set up a time for all of us to talk later."</p>

<p>After he responded to my question, the five of them walked down the opposite side, leaving only the female employees behind.</p>
<p>When leaving, Shizuku and Kirasaka waved at me and I gave them a small wave in return.</p>


<p>When we stopped in front of one of the rooms, the employees walked away.<br>I could hear my heart beat all the way up to my ears.</p>
<p>My arms were also shaking.</p>

<p>I let go and exhale deeply, then calm down and open the door without hesitation this time.</p>
<p>There is an empty cushion across the desk.</p>

<p>On the other side sat a woman.</p>
<p>I sat down before I could see her face and slowly raised my gaze upward.</p>

<p>And when I saw her face, I was rendered speechless.</p>
