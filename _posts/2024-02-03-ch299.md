<h1 style="text-align: center">#299</h1>
<hr />
<p> </p>
<p>When the class began, it seemed like the usual daily routine had returned during that time… or so I thought.</p>
<p>In the middle front of the classroom, Kanazaki Shizuku directed a gaze filled with anger, or rather, something close to resentment, in my direction. This was unrelated to the ongoing class.</p>
<p>Shielding herself with a textbook from the teacher’s line of sight, she cast a sharp, almost hostile, sidelong glance in my direction.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The reason is self-evident. The sweet, lovey-dovey act performed in the midst of the class naturally spread to those students who arrived later.</p>
<p>Though Shizuku arrived a bit later due to helping Miura in the home economics room, she quickly learned about the situation from the girls. Subsequently, her gaze transformed into something terrifying.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It’s not like I specifically asked her to do that.</p>
<p>Even if I were to tell her such a thing, it wouldn’t improve her mood.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“The princess seems angry.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Not because of some queen’s prank…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Kirasaka jokingly retorted, and I responded with a sigh.</p>
<p>As the chime signaled the end of the class, lunch break began. Students, liberated from the class, started their various activities.</p>
<p>I considered taking out my lunchbox to enjoy lunchtime, stretching my stiff body with a sigh of relief. Suddenly, the sound of approaching footsteps reached my ears.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Minato-kun, let’s have lunch together!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Sure, but…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>With a very eager tone, Shizuku called out and leaned forward. Inadvertently, I arched my back in response, and my gaze naturally turned to the side.</p>
<p>What about Kirasaka?</p>
<p>Normally, she would assertively adopt a posture of sitting together, but this time, she closed her eyes and spoke.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“This time, I’ll leave it to Kanazaki-san. She let me do as I pleased in the morning, after all.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Kirasaka-san can be careful too…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I’m just not as foolishly earnest as you.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>For a moment, a glimpse of friendship was revealed, but still, it was these two.</p>
<p>They smiled, but beneath that smile hid their dark feelings toward each other, making me shudder. Their eyes met for a moment, but Shizuku tugged at my sleeves and got up from her seat.</p>
<p>Even though we are going to eat lunch, I don’t think we’re doing it in the class. In that case, the destination would be the rooftop. I walked behind her without asking anything.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Passing students exchanged words with Shizuku, who responded casually while walking. Once they realized she was leading me, they wore expressions that conveyed understanding but couldn’t comprehend the situation.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Isn’t it about time you let go of my hand?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“No, this is a kind of restraint. I can’t let our precious lunch break be disturbed.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Without changing her expression or paying any attention to the whispers around us, Shizuku advanced. However, her hand holding mine felt tense.</p>
<p>Opening the door to the rooftop and moving to a place where the building shielded us from the wind, we both sat down, leaning against the fence.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shizuku, too, took out her lunchbox with one hand and began her lunch preparations.</p>
<p>I also opened my lunchbox and placed it on my lap, raising my gaze.</p>
<p>Above us, a large cloud spread out, seemingly reflecting my emotional state.</p>
<p>However, I couldn’t let Shizuku deal with the awkwardness.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I clasped my hands together as usual, then began my lunch alongside her.</p>
<p>Next to me, Shizuku confirmed that I reached for my lunch with chopsticks and muttered a word before starting her meal.</p>
<p>Without words, we simply sat shoulder to shoulder and ate together.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Is this really okay?</p>
<p>Will she be satisfied with this? That was what I thought, but unintentionally, the exchanged glances were gentle and cheerful.</p>
<p>After finishing our meal and moistening our throats with green tea, Shizuku eagerly took something out. It was a package different in shape from before, a small box wrapped in passionate red paper.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Here you go, Minato-kun! I did my best this year too!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Thank you, I’ll savour it as I eat.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Yes! Please enjoy it now!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shizuku held out both hands, gesturing as if saying, “Please, go ahead.” As expected, she wanted me to eat it on the spot and give my impressions.</p>
<p>Maybe it was a natural reaction for the creator. Fortunately, I wasn’t already full from the bento. I peeled off the red wrapping, slowly opened the lid, revealing six cute white solid forms shaped like bears.</p>
<p>Even I, with my limited knowledge of colours, could tell. It was white chocolate.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Picking up one and putting it in my mouth, the moment I bit down, a texture reminiscent of a snack attacked me. Before I knew it, I had brought the second one to my mouth.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Is it delicious?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Yeah, the sweetness is just right, and it’s delicious.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Answering truthfully, Shizuku blushed slightly and showed a happy smile.</p>
<p>I wonder if I’ll nosebleed tomorrow from consuming too much sugar.</p>
<p>Well, let’s consider it as if I consumed several days’ worth in a single day, even though I don’t calculate calories. With a decision made, I moved my hands, which hesitated to bring more to my mouth.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>While eating three or four, Shizuku started to tidy up her own lunch.</p>
<p>Once I finish eating this, I’ll clean up and spend the rest of the time at ease.</p>
<p>As I imagined the upcoming time and organized my schedule in my mind, the unusual sound of a student entering the rooftop reached us. The dull sound of the door, laughter, and the sound of indoor shoes hitting the ground mixed together.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Various sounds intertwined, and the footsteps approached us in the direction where Shizuku and I were sitting.</p>
<p>Generally, students stayed away from the rooftop in summer and approached in winter.</p>
<p>Judging by the voices, it was probably a pair of female students. It wasn’t strange for them to come here.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shizuku, without any sign of panic, casually directed her gaze toward the voices. Two students appeared in my field of vision. They had faces I didn’t see often, perhaps students from a class on the third floor.</p>
<p>However, they seemed to know Shizuku well, as they raised their hands and greeted us.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Oh, it’s Kanazaki-san.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Heya!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Hello, sorry for intruding.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shizuku politely responded, and the two girls turned their eyes towards us. Their gaze, from my toes to the tips of my hair, was so scrutinizing that it felt uncomfortable.</p>
<p>It was as if we were being appraised.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>…Actually, they were probably appraising us.</p>
<p>What kind of person is the boy accompanying the beautiful girl next to them?</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Then, the two of them twisted their mouths into a sneer and laughed in a minimal voice that wouldn’t reach Shizuku’s ears.</p>
<p>I won’t get angry about such things anymore. Those emotions were left behind long ago.</p>
<p>Even after passing by us, they looked back multiple times, shaking their shoulders while laughing. The feeling that welled up inside me was guilt.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Maybe I’m the one being laughed at.</p>
<p>But there’s a person who is being laughed at because of me.</p>
<p>That’s the guilt I felt about this fact.</p>
<p>The two girls sat down in a place away from us, but the rooftop was small, divided by fences, so there was still some distance, and their conversation could be heard to some extent.</p>
<p>Even if someone says it’s unnecessary, I connected the earphones I had in my uniform pocket to my smartphone and handed them to Shizuku.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“This is a song I’ve been into recently. Give it a listen.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“It’s rare for Minato-kun to share his preferences.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shizuku tilted her head curiously, suddenly wondering what was going on. She put on the earphones I offered and pressed the play button at a moderate volume, closing her eyes to concentrate on the music.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I wouldn’t be able to introduce something like this.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Indeed… Kanazaki-san might be a bit different too.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Soft laughter and suppressed voices reached my ears.</p>
<p>As I leaned back, the stretched bangs obstructed my view of the sky.</p>
<p>Tastes vary from person to person.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Therefore, I neither deny nor affirm it.</p>
<p>The opinions of unfamiliar girls were the correct answers in the definition of taste.</p>
<p>Because I didn’t care, I let the words around me be, but because of that, someone might get hurt.</p>
<p>In the truest sense, there were a few people who could comfort me.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Minato-kun? I feel like I’ve heard this song before.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Well, there’s more to it… so just keep listening.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Is that so?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Having heard my words, Shizuku returned the earphones to their original position.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>This is a problem I have to solve.</p>
<p>Because of one person named Shinra Minato, someone who should have no reason or need to be laughed at is being laughed at.</p>
<p>There is nothing more humiliating than this.</p>
<p>But even if I understand, even if I think about it, all I can do now is not let the voices of the girls reach Shizuku. Just such a trivial thing.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I wonder if I should cut my hair.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I sighed, blowing away the strands of hair that hung over my eyes, but my voice would never reach anyone.</p>
