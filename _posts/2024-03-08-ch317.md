<h1 style="text-align: center">＃317</h1>
<hr />
<p> </p>
<h4>~Shizuku’s side~</h4>
<p>I somehow understood what he had been brooding over.</p>
<p>Even in casual conversations around us, there sharp words aimed at us.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Because they don’t know him, they can easily throw words at him. But at the core of those words lies the criticism and mistakes made by those around him.</p>
<p>But even if we point them out, nothing changes. Well, saying ‘nothing changes’ might be misleading.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It might be more accurate to say that it invites misunderstanding.</p>
<p>People around us don’t think that we admire him.</p>
<p>They perceive it as childhood friends and female classmates from the seat beside helping out a pitiful and lonely student.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It’s a unspoken understanding that is mistaken, denied, corrected, and never rectified.</p>
<p>We didn’t ask for it; it’s rather bothersome.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>To be told that it’s a mistake to want to be in a place where you truly want to be and be protected.</p>
<p>So, he… Minato-kun made a decision.</p>
<p>He refused to accept help even from us, without relying on me or Kirasaka-san, even refusing the help of his friend, Ogiwara-kun.</p>
<p>When I reached out and offered to help, I sensed it when he declined.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It’s for our sake.</p>
<p>So, while I feel sorry about it, deep down, I’m happy.</p>
<p>Because I know that he values us.</p>
<p>Perhaps others perceive it differently, but I understand because of our long history.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Kirasaka-san probably feels the same way.</p>
<p>…I don’t mind if she seemed more dissatisfied when she was refused than I did.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>What we want to help with, we want to help with.</p>
<p>It’s inevitable for a girl’s feelings.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Until everything is over, watching everything as closely as possible is probably the best choice.</p>
<p>One by one, more and more women gather around him, and I reassure myself that the feeling accumulating in my heart isn’t jealousy, and I make a promise with Kirasaka-san not to sneak away…</p>
<p>…Well, whether she keeps her promise with me is questionable.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>When Nakayama-san, whom we hadn’t interacted with much before, said, “You two are unexpectedly docile” I felt like I could finally let go of something.</p>
<p>Maybe it’s okay to be a little selfish this time.</p>
<p>It’s not just his problem.</p>
<p>It’s our problem.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>So, I’ll talk to Kirasaka-san and revisit the closing accounts of last year’s farewell party, and I’ll tell Minato-kun that we’ll help him again…</p>
<p>At that time, I thought it would be satisfying if Minato-kun could agree and we could all prepare together, but…</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Thank you…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Minato-kun, who said that, had a smile on his face that I hadn’t seen in years.</p>
<p>Perhaps I would have shed tears if Kirasaka-san hadn’t been next to me.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Is that all? Others might say so.</p>
<p>But for me, the meaning of the smile he showed after those few words fills my heart.</p>
<p> </p>
<p> </p>
<h4>~Rei’s side~</h4>
<p>Others are just others.</p>
<p>Classmates are just classmates.</p>
<p>Neighbours are just neighbours.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>But from my perspective, they’re nothing more than nameless extras in the story.</p>
<p>The most important one is the sole protagonist, and the troublesome one is the typical childhood friend female classmate.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>And then, there’s the student council president I admire like an older sister.</p>
<p>Grades, future prospects, even career paths, they’re not issues for me right now.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>What’s most important and precious to me is living with a person named Shinra Minato.</p>
<p>To spend time with him until my interest runs out, colouring my boring days.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>There’s a somewhat bothersome handsome guy wandering around him, but for me, the people around are just that much of a recognition.</p>
<p>But for me, it’s a bit different.</p>
<p>Fundamentally, Minato may hold similar values ​​to mine, but even the nonsensical remarks of the masses weigh on him as his responsibility.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>From the slight bewilderment I felt in our conversations, it might be the first time I’ve had such feelings.</p>
<p>Just like I did, he might see us as important.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>That’s what it means for someone like Minato to compromise his beliefs and thoughts to push things forward.</p>
<p>Initially, I dismissed the need for it when we first met, but now I’m happy that he considers us differently.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>When Minato refused my offer of help, the first emotion that came to mind made me smile unintentionally, and then I felt a bit annoyed.</p>
<p>I smiled because I realized that I had become more of a pure maiden than I thought.</p>
<p>I felt annoyed because him, despite being Minato, was being cocky.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>At the committee meeting, when a girl named Nakayama tried to act like she knew everything, I secretly contemplated whether to give her thorough education so she wouldn’t dare speak to me again. But apparently, after that, his troublesome childhood friend made up her mind.</p>
<p>As soon as we left the classroom, I proposed a plan.</p>
<p>It was a choice to start cooperating from our side.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Of course, I agreed, and we quickly finished the student council work in Minato’s place and began working on our tasks. The day quickly turned into evening.</p>
<p>When Minato unexpectedly waited for us outside the school building and timidly asked for our help in a way we hadn’t anticipated, with unexpected words, expressions, and lack of confidence, I wonder if people would think his words were pitiful?</p>
<p>I didn’t think so, not even the slightest bit.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Going to school for that person, polishing myself for that person.</p>
<p>For me, it was more touching than the love confession of a popular drama’s protagonist to hear those words from the heart.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Seeing the smile he showed afterward, I clasped my hands in front of my chest to calm the racing thoughts in my heart.</p>
<p>It was a smile that only Kanazaki Shizuku had seen before, a smile I had never seen.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>If I could see that smile, I would let go of any trivial grievances.</p>
<p>But… next time.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I resolved in my heart to monopolize his smile by myself.</p>
