<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">Shizuku's concerns</h1>
<hr>
<p>&lt;Author&gt;: This is Shizuku's story.</p>
<hr>
<p>This one will be a bit shorter than that of the other heroines, due to me being focussing on the next term chapters.</p>

<p>Obon (or Bon).</p>
<p>It is a festival held in the summers in Japan to worship the spirits of one's ancestors, and it is one of the seasonal events that come every year in Japanese Buddhist families.</p>

<p>My family is no exception. In August, I used to go home to my hometown to prepare for the event with my paternal grandparents.<br>However, these days, I rarely visit my parents' house, and only visit the nearest grave from my mother's side.</p>

<p>However, I feel bad for my father's side, so I keep in touch with them every year.<br>Mostly, Kaede...</p>


<p>This year, too, mom and dad won't be able to return from overseas, and I don't feel comfortable going to visit their home just as their son and daughter.<br>I was just beginning to think about visiting the graves this year as well...</p>

<p>"I can't believe I'm here..."</p>

<p>"Go home ...I mean, your house is right across the street."</p>

<p>What do you mean, "I'm here" ?</p>
<p></p>

<p>With a white one-piece dress and a straw hat.<br>It's a neat childhood friend trend, but unfortunately, her house is across the street, so the trip would've only took three seconds.</p>

<p>It was an ordinary day, nothing impressive, but I realized it when I saw the bouquet of flowers in her hands.</p>

<p>"You're visiting a grave?"</p>

<p>"Yes. Since I visited my mother's family the other day, I thought I would go with Minato-kun to my father's family grave since he said it would be a good idea this year."</p>

<p>Since our houses are directly across the street from each other and we are taken care of by the same temple, it is not a particularly strange thing to do.<br>In fact, we have visited the graves of both families several times and had dinner afterwards on the same days.</p>

<p>Both of us have started to get busy and the number of times we frequented has decreased, but there is no reason for me to refuse.<br>Unfortunately, Kaede has to leave for school today, so she won't be able to eat with us both siblings together until later.</p>

<p>I changed into appropriate clothes, called Shizuku who was waiting for me in the living room, and left the house.</p>

<p>"I've never been alone with Minato-kun before."</p>

<p>"Most of the time Kaede comes as a set ...and we're also quite far away from your parents' house."</p>

<p>While we talked together, we tried to find a route that would keep us in the shade as much as possible.</p>
<p>In this heat, it's a war of attrition.</p>
<p>In other words, a bean sprout boy like me can easily be dried up by the sun.<br>No wonder why Shizuku wears a straw hat that looks like it belongs in the countryside.</p>


<p>Sure, it's a visual distraction, but it's a great countermeasure.<br>Moreover, in her case, wearing a straw hat does not diminish the level of her appearance.</p>

<p>Rather, she stands out, creating the image of a modest girl.<br>In fact, all the boys who pass by her turned red in a different way, not because of the summer heat but because of her innocent appearance.</p>

<p>...ah, youth.<br>This should be called the Shizuku's youth-destroying version.</p>

<p>High school boys dream during summer vacation.<br>That is to say, they are creatures who earnestly desire to make summer memories with her and so on.</p>

<p>As the content is not based on actual experience, there are no offhanded negative remarks as usual, but I suppose that is true.<br>It is almost torture for me to be detained in a public place all the way in summer for several hours, but I guess many people are eager nonetheless.</p>


<p>On the weekday afternoon, most of the people walking along the street are housewives or students on summer vacation.<br>There were groups of men, groups of girls...</p>

<p>And loners.<br>Occasionally, I would see pairs of men and women, or even multiple people, walking along the street, and sometimes I would see a boy nearby, staring at me.</p>


<p>"After all, summer vacation brings a lot of students to this town."</p>

<p>"...They have rather too much time on their hands..."</p>

<p>We walk at a distance that is not too close, but not too far either, with our gaze directed towards an uphill road.<br>If we climbed up this slope, which went in the direction east of the station, we would find the grounds leading to the temple.</p>

<p>As I let out a light sigh and walked forward, Shizuku followed behind me.</p>

<p>"Our houses are across the street, our family tombs next to each other, and we've also known each other since childhood... Is this a coincidence?"</p>

<p>"It is a coincidence."</p>

<p>She replied without a hitch, unusually mischievous for Shizuku.<br>I wonder if she wants me to call it fate.</p>

<p>But Shizuku, knowing that I would not give such a response, had a blooming smile on her face.</p>

<p>"But if Minato-kun and I become a family, we will share the same grave. ...Which one would you prefer?"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>A silent reply.<br>I don't know what to answer, I even thought it was a distasteful way to break off the subject, but then the tiny fingers of the Shizuku grabbed my pale blue shirt.</p>

<p>Silently, as if we were holding hands, she continued to walk, not letting go of them.<br>I could have shaken her off, but before I could turn my head in the direction she was in, a phrase stuck in my brain.</p>

<p>[We who are extraordinary compete for the ordinary.]</p>

<p>These were the words that the president had uttered at the meeting.<br>If Shizuku's actions were motivated by that statement, then she too has something in her mind.</p>

<p>It would have been easy for me to shake it off in the past, but now my arms are as heavy as lead, blurring my actions and judgment.</p>
<p>I know this is ...guilt.<br>Guilt for not following what she said.</p>

<p>It burdens my heart more heavily than it does other people because I've known her since childhood.</p>

<p>"I wonder if tombstones are expensive..."</p>

<p>"Are you going to make a new one? ...Well, that's typical of Minato."</p>

<p>What just came out were words that strayed us from the subject.<br>Fortunately, she smiled at that and I felt the silence between us go away.</p>


<p>Oh ...I really hate my personality for being like this.</p>



<p></p>

<p>I put my hands on the grave and told my ancestors, whom I had never met, only that I was well.<br>They never responded, of course, and I don't have the peculiarity of being able to see ghosts.</p>

<p>So, I prayed in silence only for a short time, but I think it was still meaningful.<br>The point is, it's all about how you feel.</p>

<p>I interpreted it as if our ancestors were watching over us and that we are expected to spend our days with a positive attitude.</p>

<p>At the next grave, Shizuku was holding hands as well, but it was only for a short while and soon we were lined up in the aisle between the two tombs.</p>

<p>"Shall we go home?"</p>

<p>Saying this, Shizuku continued onward.<br>Then we passed the edge of the grounds, just before we descended the hill we had climbed, to a spot where the parking lot of this temple was located, with no houses, walls, or anything to give us a panoramic view of what was below us.</p>
<p>
</p><p>At the edge of my field of vision, I could see Sakuranaoka Academy, the school that we attended.<br>As I looked around the elementary school, middle school, and the girls' high school that Kaede attended, and also saw that our house was somewhere around there, I heard a quiet murmur next to me.</p>

<p>"I hope the second term will be a fun one for you too, Minato-kun."</p>

<p>"It'll be bothersome."</p>

<p>Kirasaka also said something similar.<br>I had no idea why they both made similar remarks, but I responded to her in the same way.</p>

<p>"Lately, Minato-kun has been interacting with people more and more, which is a positive development, in my opinion."</p>

<p>"Are you my mother?"</p>

<p>Or my teacher?<br>But I unknowingly made a tsukkomi to Shizuku, who was speaking on behalf of the person who was watching over my growth.</p>

<p>I was told that she should refrain from cheesy comments because it would make the character misunderstood. ...I forgot who it was, but...</p>


<p>"More people, more conversations...---."</p>
<p>
</p><p>The last words were drowned out by the sound of the temple bells ringing.<br>All I knew was a fragile expression on her face and what she muttered.</p>

<p>"Come on, let's go home!"</p>

<p>The next moment, her expression brightened as if nothing had happened, and this time, she began to walk as if she were hugging my arm, and I involuntarily started to follow her.</p>
<p>I couldn't say anything about what she was trying to say, as she refused to listen back.</p>






<p>"More people, more conversations... and I feel like... Minato-kun's heart is drifting further away from us."</p>
