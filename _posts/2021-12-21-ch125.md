<h1 style="text-align:center;">#125</h1>
<p> </p>
<hr />
<p> </p>
<p>The school building was filled with sunshine that was too strong to be called warm, summer was finally in full swing.</p>
<p>The students' spirits were rising right along with it.</p>

<p>This was proof of the importance of summer to the students.</p>
<p>However, on the other hand, the teachers seemed to be less motivated.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Even though it's a long vacation for students, for teachers, it doesn't matter because they are working adults.</p>

<p>Even if students don't come, they'll still have work to do.</p>
<p>I can't help but admire the spirit of a corporate slave that continues to repeat the same thing everyday.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>If I could, I would like to stay at home all my life and live in peace.</p>
<p>However, reality will not allow that.</p>

<p>Next year would be my last, and the entrance examinations would be right around the corner.</p>
<p>Naturally, I will have less time to relax and I am not sure if I will be able to enjoy my summer vacation due to its burden.　</p>

<p>The sophomores who have established relationships with the people around them, are probably the ones who can enjoy the summer vacation the most.　</p>

<p>However, many students may push aside the temptation to play and work hard on their studies a year before.</p>
<p>This way, they'll be able to relax themselves a bit during the next term.</p>

<p>In fact, there were some students around me who were talking about the same thing.</p>

<p>"You know there's a summer course coming up."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"I'll be staying for three days."</p>

<p>What kind of punishment is that, staying over for a summer course?</p>
<p>Do you think it's OK to do everything overnights?</p>

<p>It is better to study for a short time efficiently according to the Shinra family, as concentration and motivation decrease with time.</p>

<p>However, I'll be spending my summer vacation in an air-conditioned room watching TV.</p>

<p>I will not leave the house.</p>
<p>If I do have to, it'll only be for shopping, I've made up my mind.</p>

<p>But lately, nothing has been going according to my plans even if I make up my mind. SO I decided to make a plan to go outside.</p>
<p>If I do that, it might turn around and I might just be able to laze at home.</p>


<p>The reason my mind was moving to such a trivial thought since there were no people around my seat.</p>
<p>Apparently, there are no students in this class who are desperate enough to invite a mere classmate who is neither a friend nor an acquaintance.</p>

<p>I don't have a problem with this kind of thing, since I would never go even if I were to be invited.</p>
<p>This is the perfect scenario.</p>

<p>On the other hand, unlike me, Yuuto and Shizuku were busy dealing with invitations in the middle of the classroom.</p>
<p>They were being invited to movies, amusement parks, summer resorts and whatnot.</p>


<p>"...How can y'all go out in this heat?"</p>

<p>My mumbled voice dissipated in the chatter of the classroom and the noise of the cicadas from the outside.</p>
<p>Really, what kind of energy drink do you guys drink?</p>

<p>In a classroom with no air conditioning, the students were excited about the holidays coming up which was a few days away, and the time continued to pass.</p>


<p>In the midst of all this, Kirasaka was still staring at the paperback she had brought with her.</p>
<p>In the hot classroom, she read the book in a cool manner.</p>

<p>I'm not sure if she noticed or knew it all along, but when I was looking towards her, she muttered.</p>

<p>"It's hot already... If you stare at me like that, I'm going to get a heat stroke."</p>

<p>"You can't get heat stroke from a human gaze."</p>

<p>Is there laser coming out of my eyes?</p>
<p>The expression on her face is not as convincing as her words.</p>

<p>I tried to avert my gaze from her, but she continued the conversation.</p>

<p>"I just want to make sure ...you'll be at the clock tower at seventeen o'clock on the weekend, right?"</p>

<p>"Don't go on deciding my schedule without my consent! ...I didn't promise you anything for the weekend."</p>

<p>There are no plans according to my brain calendar.</p>
<p>In other words, I'll spend my time peacefully at home.</p>

<p>I quickly denied Kirasaka's remarks as if I had some other work to do.</p>
<p>I guess she already expected this response and didn't even move her line of sight.</p>

<p>However, her next words were not directed at me, but at someone else.</p>

<p>"That's right, Kanazaki-san."</p>

<p>"Minato-kun! You are free this weekend, right!?"</p>

<p>...That's strange.</p>
<p>Shizuku, who was supposed to be surrounded by the students in the center of the classroom, is now leaning forward with her hands on my desk.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Moreover, she said something to me that sounded like "You are free this weekend", didn't she?</p>

<p>Before I could answer, Shizuku said what would be the name for this arc.</p>

<p>"Let's go to the fireworks festival!"</p>

<p>Fireworks festival...</p>
<p>So, summer has finally arrived in this rural town.</p>

<p> Summer is coming for Minato and his friends.</p>
