<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">164</h1>

<hr />
<p>The same day.<br />This was an unexpected encounter, brought about by coincidence.</p>

<p>After Minato and the others departed from their home, Kaede, who remained alone at home, was working diligently on the household chores to relieve her restless mood.<br />In front of the Shinra household where Kaede was, was a girl standing.</p>

<p>"I happened to be near by, so ...no, it would be more natural to talk normally..."</p>

<p>The girl, who hesitated to press the intercom, muttered something, and walked restlessly right and left, in an overly suspicious manner.<br />Kaede, who was hanging laundry in the yard, watched the scene for a while, then approached the girl, who had never left the front door, and called out to her</p>

<p>"Ano ...can I help you?"</p>

<p>"Wawawa, yesh, yesh!"</p>

<p>Suddenly, the girl, who was called from behind, blurted out what she was muttering.<br />The girl with her cheeks red with shame, turned back in the direction where the voice came from ...and gasped when she saw the appearance of Kaede.</p>

<p>There stood a beautiful young girl with black hair and a face with a hint of childishness, but there was no mistaking it that she was a beauty.</p>
<p>In addition, she was dressed in short sleeves and short pants in the midsummer heat, which seemed both casual and friendly.</p>

<p>"Cute."</p>

<p>It was a simple feat to distract Shiraishi Momiji, the girl who was visiting the Shinra family today.<br />To begin with, she has always been too vulnerable to unexpected developments.</p>

<p>The presence of such a beautiful girl in Shinra Minato's house was in itself an unexpected event.</p>

<p>"Thank you... so then, how can I help you?"</p>

<p>Kaede, who never lost her attitude while dealing with guests, accepted Shiraishi's compliment and asked her again.</p>

<p>"Ah... right, I wanted to have a talk with Shinra-senpai today..."</p>

<p>"...Another girl."</p>

<p>"Yes?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>One more woman whom I do not know of, came to meet Nii-san.<br />The mere mention of an arranged marriage had just sent Kaede's grumpiness gauge soaring, and this further accelerated that.</p>
<p>However, Kaede put on a facial expression so that others wouldn't notice.</p>

<p>"I'm sorry, but my brother is out. If you would like, I could pass on a message to him."</p>

<p>"I see, he's out... eh, you're his sister?"</p>

<p>"Yes, my name is Shinra Kaede."</p>

<p>With a startled look on her face, Shiraishi confirmed Kaede's appearance several times.<br />She seemed to be completely unaware of it till now, and there was a brief moment of silence.</p>

<p>But it was not long before Shiraishi's demeanor changed abruptly.</p>

<p>"I'm sorry, my name is Shiraishi Momiji, a first-year student at Sakuranaoka Academy. I'm here today to consult regarding a matter of student council with Shinra-senpai."</p>

<p>"...I will let my brother know about Shiraishi-san's visit when he gets back." </p>

<p>It was only a moment, but Shiraishi did not miss the brief pause before she spoke.</p>

<p>"The gestures you make when you think, the little pauses you take during conversations, they're just like your brother's."</p>

<p>"We are siblings."</p>

<p>She replied it as a matter-of-fact, smiling.<br />After that, they exchanged some information with each other.</p>

<p>Reactions to various topics were shown in response to the topics and they changed their tones and facial expressions accordingly.</p>
<p>The two of them were fully utilizing high-powered brains trying to figure out how to deal with someone they had never met before.</p>

<p>They stood at the entrance with a smile on their faces.<br />From the outside, it would just look like they were having a friendly conversation in front of the entrance.</p>
<p> </p>

<p>"By the way, where did senpai go? I heard he would be at home for the summer vacation."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>At that one sentence, Kaede's expression turned grim.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The slightest bit of jealousy for her older brother took over just for a moment.<br />Why does Nii-san tell his vacation schedule to her junior whom he had known just for a small period of time?</p>

<p>"It's an arranged marriage."</p>

<p>"Hii~iiii, an arranged marriage... arranged marriage! ...a gal-game?"</p>

<p>"Did you say something?"</p>

<p>Her actual thoughts leaked out, and Shiraishi shook her head vigorously to express her denial.<br />It might be a natural reaction to hear a high school student in their summer vacation hearing something like this.</p>
<p>However, the latter part of the statement was limited to Shiraishi.</p>

<p>"It's not a gal-game, okay?"</p>

<p>"You heard me!?"</p>

<p>Shiraishi was feeling relieved thinking that she wasn't heard, and then she tried to cover it up with a few words.<br />Just a few moments later, she felt embarrassed, she also reaffirmed that she was Shinra Minato's sister indeed.</p>
<p>She was completely been played.</p>

<p>For Shiraishi, the senior named Shinra Minato was an ordinary student, but strangely had good connections. She regarded him as a senior with a twisted personality, yet he did not take advantage of anyone.<br />However, his younger sister is so good-looking and probably has a bright mind.</p>

<p>In addition to this, she also has no small resemblance to her older brother.<br />Shiraishi and Kaede, they shared the same generation and gender.</p>

<p>This is what gave Shiraishi a sense of dislike toward Kaede.<br />I'm not trying to assign superiority or inferiority, but it's not surprising to have negative feelings towards people who are better than we are.</p>

<p>"I will visit you again at a later date."</p>

<p>"Yes, it would be convenient for my brother if you could contact me in advance."</p>

<p>With these last words, Shiraishi turned her back and walked away from the Shinra household.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Absolutely... I alone is enough for my brother as someone younger!"</p>

<p>Kaede muttered her complaint, directed more towards her brother than towards Shiraishi. <br />This was an accidental encounter between two people of the same age.</p>

<p>It was not until Minato returned home exhausted that he learned of it.</p>
