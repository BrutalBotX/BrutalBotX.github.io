<p>&lt;Author&gt; : An update! It's a little longer this time.</p>
<hr />
<p> </p>
<p>I was carrying a good number of bags in both my hands.</p>

<p>I was walking, trying to hold myself from the smell coming from below that stimulated my appetite.<br />Yuuto who was beside me was carrying more number of bags than I did.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The size of each bag was not a big problem.<br />But there were just too many!</p>

<p>Takoyaki, yakitori, okonomiyaki, yakisoba.<br />There were also candied apples and chocolate bananas, probably for the sake of dessert.</p>

<p>There were also a number of other bags that I had no idea what they held.<br />To be honest, why on earth would they need buy so much food that we could never possibly eat?<br />The reason was the girl who stood at the front of our group. She bought every bit of food she could find.</p>

<p>"I wonder if I should buy that too..."</p>

<p>"Ah, ha ha..."</p>



<p>Kirasaka, who bought cotton candy handing money to the stall owner, handed it to Yuuto.<br />He received it with a bitter smile and turned his gaze towards me to ask for help.</p>

<p>This situation was a bit difficult to handle since it was an act of pure curiosity, and not only me but also the people around me felt the same.<br />However, my stomach was about to burst beyond its capacity, so I grabbed her shoulders before she could set her next target.</p>

<p>"It's time to go home ...The more people there are, the harder it will be to move."</p>

<p>There were already people in every direction, and considering the time it would take to set off the fireworks and reach home, it was better to retreat.<br />In response, Kirasaka put away her wallet with a reluctant look on her face.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"You can't help it, I'll leave it at that for today."</p>


<p>"...Today? Will there be a next time...?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shizuku and Kaede also let out a breath of relief at Kirasaka's words.</p>



<p>There were too many people to turn around and go back the way we came from. So it would be quicker to go back home through the side streets.</p>
<p>This time, I led the way.</p>
<p>The path was sparsely populated, and after a little while, we were able to reach an open road where we shouldn't bump into anyone.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"It's not too late for the festival, considering that there are a lot more people coming."</p>

<p>Yuuto muttered to himself as he looked at the people walking along the main road behind him.<br />Indeed, it was still past six o'clock.</p>

<p>It was just after six o'clock, the time when the number of people would increase the most, but the streets were already filled with people.<br />It was a festival, so of course cars and motorcycles were not allowed to enter.</p>

<p>Some people took advantage of this and sat on the sidewalks.<br />It was a good choice to leave early.</p>

<p>I didn't want to walk in the middle of all this.</p>

<p>Occasionally, I saw a familiar face of the locals and saw Yuuto and Kaede waving, but I never stopped to talk to anyone.<br />Well... that was for me, and not them, if Shizuku and Yuuto didn't stop to talk, there shouldn't have been any delays.</p>



<p>We walked through the streets that I didn't usually go through, finding the way to the familiar ones and finally reached my house. Then what? I just laid my body down on the sofa.<br />Even in your locality, events can be more tiring than expected.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I was not the only one, everyone was sighing deeply.<br />But Kaede got up before anyone else and prepared drinks for everyone.</p>

<p>"I'll go set the food."</p>

<p>"Oh, I'll help too."</p>

<p>"Thank you!"</p>



<p>Shizuku and Yuuto laid out the food they had bought on the folding table which had already been set in the yard.<br />Kirasaka also headed to the kitchen to help Kaede, while I was left alone to ponder.</p>



<p>There's nothing to do...<br />There were people in the yard and also in the kitchen.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>A short brainstorming led to the decision to change into loungewear for the time being.</p>





<p>Taking advantage of the fact that it was my home, I changed into my casuals and returned to the living room, where the preparations were already complete.<br />Kaede and Yuuto were sitting on the chairs in the garden, while Shizuku and Kirasaka have moved their chairs next to the window.</p>

<p>"Nii-san, it's about to start!"</p>

<p>"Yeah"</p>

<p>At the sound of Kaede's hurried voice, I rushed a bit to sit by the window.<br />The time was exactly seven o'clock, and countless fireworks were to go off.</p>

<p>There was just silence as we waited for the right moment.<br />As soon as we heard the sound of an explosion and a loud cheer from afar, bright sparks formed and spread in the night sky.<span class="EOP"> </span></p>

<p>The vibrations of the sound was slightly felt on my skin.<br />I'm sure the neighboring towns could also see these huge fireworks.</p>

<p>It's a privilege to live in this town just to be able to see them up close.</p>

<p>"Oh! This year's fireworks are spectacular again."</p>

<p>"It's beautiful."</p>

<p>First, Yuuto and Kaede exclaimed excitedly.</p>

<p>"Beautiful..."</p>

<p>"Yes, it is...."</p>

<p>Next, Kirasaka and then Shizuku muttered to each other.<br />They were too quiet to be heard by the others in the garden.</p>

<p>They looked up at the night sky as they let out their true feelings.<br />It probably didn't even register in their minds that they had the same impression.</p>
<p>After seeing the reactions of those around me, I returned my gaze to the fireworks.<br />A number of fireworks were being set off above our heads in various colors and shapes.</p>

<p>It was hard to believe that they were all made by the same person.<br />Craftsmanship at its finest.</p>

<p>They looked so beautiful and fragile that I didn't even notice the distant noise and the hot weather.<br />I forgot about time and just watched the scene as it disappeared and the next one formed in the blink of an eye.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I wondered how long had it been.<br />The show was getting more and more colorful and vigorous by the minute, and then an oversized shot was launched to end the show.</p>

<p>Sparks of many colors brightly spread and then colored the night sky.<br />The town, which was supposed to be dark and lightless, lit up for a moment.</p>

<p>It was just for a moment but I was able to see everyone's faces clearly.</p>



<p>This was the signal of the start of our summer vacation, and the reminder that we were more than halfway through the year.<br />I wondered under what circumstances I would see these fireworks next year.</p>

<p>Will it be with this group again, or just Kaede and I?</p>



<p>No one knows what the future holds.<br />Still, I couldn't help but think about it.</p>

<p>At a time like this, a high school boy might say, "I'm going to see it with my girlfriend next year."<br />But unfortunately, I can't quite relate to that idea.</p>

<p>The only thing that comes to mind is that I would like to see it in a place where there are no crowds.</p>



<p>But for now, all I could do was burn this fleeting scene into my eyes so that I wouldn't miss it.</p>



<p>And so, this year's festival ended with a <em>boom</em>, and our festival ended in the yard of Shinra household.</p>







<p>[...Well, it was another great year.]</p>

<p>In the living room, the sound of the TV was audible, and we were all letting out our impressions.<br />Kaede and Yuuto, who still hadn't cooled down from their excitement, were watching the video they had taken with their phones as a memento.</p>

<p>I returned to my place on the sofa and watched the scene while drinking my slightly cooled coffee.</p>

<p>At the end of the living room, Shizuku and Kirasaka were talking about something that was unusual for them, as they spread out my family album.</p>

<p>"...What are you guys looking at?"</p>

<p>"This? I were wondering if there were any pictures taken during the old fireworks display."</p>

<p>Aah, that...<br />It's true that we used to have dinner and watch fireworks with the Kanazaki family in our yard in the past.</p>

<p>...But I don't remember there being any photos from that time. The two of them quickly returned their gaze to the album.</p>

<p>There were pictures of Kaede and I side by side, like a growth log.<br />It's so embarrassing.</p>

<p>I knew from experience that there was no point in trying to stop them, so I decided to have some food that I had forgotten about during the fireworks.</p>













<p>"I didn't see any pictures from the fireworks display."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Kirasaka did not react to Shizuku's words.<br />She just kept her gaze fixed on the album.</p>

<p>"Kirasaka-san?"</p>

<p>Shizuku asked, when she felt uncomfortable when she looked at the girl next to her, Kirasaka muttered sadly.</p>

<p>"He's not smiling ...in any of the pictures.</p>

<p>(TL/N - The POVs here are changing a lot from here on)</p>

<p>&lt;Narrator&gt;</p>
<p>None of the photos of Minato on the screen were smiling.<br />The mere sight of him in the picture made Kirasaka feel lonely.</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>At these words, Shizuku couldn't help but be speechless.<br />Then, she turned her gaze from Kirasaka to "him" who was eating a meal a little ahead.</p>
<p> </p>

<p>This is the first time I've ever seen such a thing.<br />There were a few photos in which he smiled but...<br />...All of them are either fake or bitter smiles, and none looked like they really enjoyed themselves.</p>

<p>It was pure loneliness, and Shizuku knew that.<br />She smiled.</p>


<p>I have to melt the ice in his heart that no one has ever been able to melt.　It takes a lot of patience to like that person.</p>

<p>I know it's not easy.<br />And I had already made up my mind long ago.</p>

<p>That's why to provoke and confirm whether she was ready or not, I asked.</p>

<p>"Do you want to run away?"</p>

<p>"I'm serious, I've never run away before and I won't ever have to."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>&lt;Narrator&gt;</p>
<p>These words came back immediately.<br />Shizuku smiled as if relieved by this, and Kirasaka also smiled this time.</p>

<p>"...We're taking a picture, both of you."</p>

<p>Minato called out to the two as they faced each other from the yard, where he had somehow moved to.<br />Kaede and Yuuto were already preparing for the picture beforehand.</p>

<p>"Shall we go?"</p>

<p>"Sure."</p>

<p>The two stood up and rushed to Minato's side.<br />And then, as if to sandwich them, Kaede and Yuuto also lined up near them.</p>


<p>The digital camera on the table started flashed several times.<br />And the pictures that were taken showed the five of them clearly.</p>


<p>The four of them seemed to be having a good time around Minato, who was still not smiling in the true sense as in the previous photos.</p>
<p>The boundary between Minato and the four of them had not yet been crossed...</p>
