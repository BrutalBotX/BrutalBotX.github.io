<p>This is a continuation of the side story.</p>
<p>==============================================================================</p>
<p>　"Onsen." (TL/N - Hot Springs)</p>
<p>　What is the first thing that comes to your mind when you hear this word?</p>
<p>　Is it a bathtub full of steam, or a woman in a yukata?</p>
<p>　Individual may have a different picture in mind.</p>
<p>　For me, the first thing that came to mind was an old man with a white towel on his head.</p>
<p>　I had no weird intentions, it was simply the first scene that came to mind.</p>
<p></p>
<p>　I had been forcibly taken away from school by Kirasaka, and was driving down the Odawara Bypass in Kanagawa Prefecture.</p>
<p>　The car driven by Kuroi-san was a limousine, a typical car for rich people.</p>
<p>　Since he had given me a ride in a black one-box car in the past, I had assumed that it would be the same car this time, but it was actually this limousine that came to pick me up.</p>
<p>　Needless to say, I was so overwhelmed by the situation that I couldn't say a word...</p>
<p>　Anyway, we are now on our way to Hakone, but the air in the car is heavy.</p>
<p>　The air in front of us was so heavy that everyone was hesitant to say a word.</p>
<p>　The reason for this is quite simple.</p>
<p>　Rei Kirasaka and Shizuku Kanzaki were sitting face to face, playing a serious game of cards.</p>
<p>　The card game, well, it's playing cards, but actually they are currently facing off in a game of Old Maid.</p>
<p>　Me and Yuuto had already finished all the cards in our hands early, but surprisingly, these two were still there.</p>
<p>　If my prediction was correct, the Joker was in Shizuku's hand, and if Kirasaka drew a card other than the Joker, she would win, and if she drew any other card, she would be in trouble.</p>
<p>　In such a situation, Kirasaka draws the card on the right without hesitation.</p>
<p>"Oh, Kirasaka-san is easily caught."</p>
<p>"...This vixen..."</p>
<p>　"...Oi,oi."</p>
<p>　I'm not sure why they are playing such a serious game.</p>
<p>Kirasaka, who made the wrong choice, seems to have been frustrated by having been bad-mouthed, but on the other hand Shizuku was smiling.</p>
<p>　It's funny ...if I remember correctly, card related games are supposed to be games that are played for light fun...</p>
<p>　It's a good mindset to be serious about everything, but you can't be serious about this kind of game.</p>
<p>　However, when Yuuto and I finished, the atmosphere suddenly changed.</p>
<p>　The emotions completely drained from their faces, and a silence reigned as if they were trying to read each other's minds with their poker faces on.</p>
<p>　They exchanged their cards several times, until Kirasaka now held the Joker, but these two are truly incompatible.</p>
<p>　They don't want to lose to the each other even if they kills time like this, just like facing a competitior in a serious match.</p>
<p>"I've never seen such a heavy hand of cards before."</p>
<p>"Don't worry, ...neither have I."</p>
<p>　Yuuto, who was sitting next to me, muttered in a small voice.<br>　In the end, I couldn't escape the forced entertainment of Kirasaka, so I presented one condition.</p>
<p>　That is, to let Yuuto, who is right in front of me, accompany me.<br>　Although I could enjoy the hot spring alone, I thought it would be better to bring him along, considering the other parties with whom I was going.</p>
<p>　However, I think I've been seeing a good amount of bitter smiles on Yuuto's face lately.</p>
<p>　It's hard to ask, though, because I'm aware that the three of us are the ones who are responsible for it.<br>　The women exchanged jokers in the same way repeatedly more than a dozen times.</p>
<p>　Before the game ended, Kuroi-san called out to us.</p>
<p>"Ojou-sama, everyone, we will be arriving in about five minutes."</p>
<p>"So ...let's leave this game to be decided at table tennis after the onsen."</p>
<p>"I hope ...you won't cry over losing?"</p>
<p>　The two of them gave up on the game here, and put their cards on the table to call a truce.<br>　I looked out the window of the car and saw a landscape that was different from the town we lived in.</p>
<p></p>
<p>　My family had been here before, but it had been a while since I had been to a hot spring resort, and I felt my spirits lift a little.<br>　At the same time, I remembered Kaede's phone call a while ago.<br>　Kaede was answering the phone as usual, but she was sulking a little.</p>
<p>　I could tell because we were siblings.<br>　It was only her voice, but I could tell that she had a frustrated expression on the other end of the phone.</p>
<p>　Naturally, it's normal to be envious when your brother goes to the hot-springs alone.</p>
<p>　When I told her that I would give up my next day off to go with her to a place of her choice as an apology, her tone changed and it was good to know there were no problems for the time being.<br>　If I didn't have some kind of compromise plan, I might not be able to eat for a few days, which would be an absolute disaster.<br>　With my stomach in my sister's hand, it appeared that the hierarchical relationship in my family is already decided.</p>
<p>　Anyway, it was confirmed that I would have to give up my day off for the second week in a row.<br>　My resting place is getting farther and farther away.</p>
<p>　The car driven by Kuroi-san stopped when it reached the entrance of the building instead of the parking lot.<br>　There were already several employees waiting in line at the entrance, and one man opened the back seat of the parked car.</p>
<p></p>
<p>　As I was feeling a little suspicious about his behavior, Kirasaka was the first to get out.</p>
<p>"We've been expecting you, Rei-ojousama."</p>
<p>"I'm sorry to bother you all of a sudden, but I'm going to borrow your facilities for a little while."</p>
<p>"Don't tell me ...this place is also a part of Kirasaka's company?"</p>
<p>　After getting out of the car, I was struck by the conversation that was taking place.<br>　It's very easy to imagine the answer to this question, as the white-haired man who was probably the director bowed his head several times and was being polite to Kirasaka, a student.</p>
<p>　Kirasaka answered the question that seemed to have no need for an answer.</p>
<p>"Yes, I want to go to the hot spring, but I don't like crowds, so I asked for a private bath."</p>
<p>"Apologize to the people who were planning to come here today!"</p>
<p>"Don't worry, I've already sent the people who had reservations to other restaurants."</p>
<p></p>
<p>　I wonder how much money this woman is spending on an after-school, spur-of-the-moment hot-springs day.</p>
<p>　However, it's possible that we won't be able enjoy the hot springs because of the regret.</p>
<p>I'm not a substitute for Kirasaka, who went ahead, but I bowed to the employees many times and then followed.</p>
