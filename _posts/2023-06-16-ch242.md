<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align: center">#242</h1>

<hr />
<p>There are few rules for group formation for the school trip.</p>
<p>The students are divided into girls and boys and each is paired with one of their classmates of their choice.</p>
<p>The numbers are then assigned to the pair, and a lottery is drawn at the end to combine the male and female pairs.</p>

<p>The number of participants must be at least four.</p>

<p>There are some other detailed rules, such as the need to assign a person in charge of each group, who must be able to contact the teachers, but this is not a problem when deciding on the groups.</p>

<p>This is roughly about all of the rules.</p>
<p>After that, each group will decide on its own destination and enjoy the school trip.</p>

<p>"That's all for the explanation. ...If anyone has any questions, please ask the teacher separately."</p>

<p>After reading the last sentence in front of my classmates, I walked out from in front of the teacher's table to my seat.</p>
<p>The classroom, which was so noisy at first has turned quieter, making it easier for me to explain it at last.</p>

<p>With no one standing in front of the students, the silence in the classroom gradually turns into a pleasant chatting atmosphere.</p>
<p>For the students, this is where the fun begins.</p>

<p>The school trip, the biggest event exclusive for second-year students.</p>
<p>It is rare to find a student who is not looking forward to it.</p>

<p>The students were sitting in their seats, which is back to its lively atmosphere, wondering who is going to be in the group with whom.　</p>

<p>"...You're really good at making enemies, aren't you?"</p>

<p>Kirasaka muttered while sitting.</p>
<p>She had already taken out a paperback from her desk and seemed uninterested in deciding on a group.</p>
<p>Just as one would expect from Kirasaka, that's all I can say.　</p>

<p>I was trying to enjoy the situation in the classroom so I suppressed the feeling of wanting to retort to what she said and instead let out a sigh.</p>

<p>The teacher said so because of the student council, but it shouldn't be a position where I would be handled all the troublesome work.</p>
<p>I was tempted to make the same kind of comment to the teacher, but since I was younger, I decided not to.</p>

<p>"Groups... have you decided whom you're pairing up with?"</p>

<p>The pupils in the class stood up and gathered with the students they were each going to be paired with.</p>
<p>Regardless of gender, the scene remains the same.</p>
<p>However, there are a number of male students who are glancing in Kirasaka's direction.</p>

<p>The rule of group formation requires that the groups be made up of both male and female students.</p>
<p>For those who have a liking for Kirasaka, there is no better opportunity than this.</p>

<p>Naturally, they must be hoping to be in the same group as Kirasaka.</p>
<p>As I was thinking about this while looking at my surroundings, Kirasaka snapped shut the paperback she was holding in her hand.</p>

<p>She then turns her eyes toward me and tilts her head as if to say, 'What are you talking about?'</p>

<p>“What are you talking about? …Isn’t it decided that I will be in the same group as Shinra-kun?”　</p>

<p>"Did you even listen to me...?"</p>

<p>Isn't it obvious? It's a statement that completely ignores what I was explaining to my classmates.</p>

<p>I was impressed that she could say such a thing so confidently.</p>
<p>Kirasaka, who was staring at me with her mouth open, checks the handout in her hand.</p>

<p>The form contains a detailed description of what I had explained to everyone.</p>
<p>Immediately her expression turned calm and she put her fingertips to her chin and made a thoughtful face.</p>

<p>Honestly, when it comes to gender pairings, we have to rely on the luck of the hour.</p>
<p>Even with her brilliant mind, there is no way to deal with it, but still, Kirasaka stands up slowly.</p>


<p>As she walked toward the center of the class, where there was a lot of noise to decide who would be in the group, the students passing by were staring at her, wondering what was going on.</p>

<p>I was one of them too.</p>
<p>As I watched, wondering what she was going to do, she stopped next to Shizuku at the center of the class.</p>

<p>"I need to talk to you for a moment."</p>

<p>Kirasaka pointed to the back of the classroom.</p>
<p>She wanted to talk to her at a distance where our classmates couldn't hear them.</p>

<p>Shizuku, realizing the meaning, moved to the back of the classroom, though being a little surprised.</p>
<p>While all eyes of my classmates were on the girls, I sat in my seat and wondered what I should do about my group. </p>

<p>Good luck isn't a word that means anything when it comes to group formation.</p>
<p>I wondered if there was anyone in the class who would be strange enough to invite me to join them, as I had been making a poor impression on my classmates lately.</p>

<p>As I thought about this, I looked around the classroom again and saw that Yuuto had casually slipped out of the center of the class and sat down in the seat in front of me. </p>

<p>"Minato, do you have any ideas about where you want to go?"</p>

<p>I was stunned by the way he spoke so excitedly with a travel magazine in his hand, which our homeroom teacher had brought along for reference.</p>
<p>Yuuto, who noticed my gaze, asked a question curiously.</p>

<p>"Perhaps you're not interested in Okinawa?"</p>

<p>"No, before that, I would like to ask you why you're just assuming that you're going to be in a group with me."</p>

<p>On top of the materials he brought with him, there is a sheet of paper on which the names of the group members are written.</p>
<p>On it, my name is written in the column of the group leader, and his name is written below it.</p>

<p>The classmates are looking for Yuuto, who has disappeared from his seat in an instant, acting in a sneaky and peculiar manner.</p>
<p>I don't like the fact that their eyes finally landed on us, and they seemed a little unconvinced that it was me.</p>

<p>"What!? You don't want to team up?"</p>

<p>I let out a sigh as he asked back in surprise.</p>
<p>Since there is no other person to pair up with, I have no choice but to prepare myself for being resented by my classmates.</p>

<p>Without replying back, I put my hand on the material Yuuto brought.</p>
<p>As we both silently searched for a place to go with each other, our homeroom teacher returned from the staff room.</p>

<p>In one hand he is holding a lottery box made of cardboard.</p>
<p>As he placed the box on the teacher's desk, he spoke.</p>

<p>"The students who decided on their pairs are asked to turn in their forms, and the lottery will be held when all the forms have been collected."</p>

<p>The students nodded and responded in various ways to the brief explanation.</p>
<p>When Yuuto finished speaking, he quickly handed the forms that he brought with him to the homeroom teacher.</p>

<p>This confirmed that Yuuto and I would be in the same group.</p>
<p>The boys may have been dissatisfied with this, but it was Yuuto's opinion and no one interfered.</p>


<p>After all the classmates' forms were submitted, the homeroom teacher first announced the boys' group.</p>

<p>"Shinra and Ogiwara, group D."</p>

<p>A female student, acting as the scribe, writes Yuuto's and my name on the blackboard, and a 'D' under it.</p>
<p>The female students started to get a little noisy.</p>

<p>After the boys, the girls' groups are to be announced.</p>
<p>The group that draws D will be in the same group as us.</p>

<p>After all the draws for the boys were over, the pairings for the girls were announced.</p>
<p>I was watching the class from my seat, but Kirasaka next to me and Shizuku a little ahead of me were waiting for the results with their hands clasped together.</p>

<p>...Is it okay to make such a serious face?</p>
<p>It is the difference in the expectations for school trips that makes me think that it is just another free day.</p>

<p>"For girls Kanazaki and Kirazaka's group is..."</p>

<p>The homeroom teacher gets the boys in a bit of an uproar when he says it like that.</p>
<p>Probably he was shocked to find out that they were in the same group, even though they were not close friends.</p>
<p>At the same time, the possibility of being in the same group with these two students was also a sign of anticipation.</p>

<p>I wondered what they were talking about back there... were they talking about being in the same group?</p>

<p>It's unforeseen...</p>
<p>With this thought in mind, I waited for the homeroom teacher's next words.</p>

<p>"they will be in group B."</p>

<p>The homeroom teacher drew a piece of paper with the letter 'B' written on it, and the boys who were in the same group cheered as soon as the homeroom teacher announced it.</p>

<p>But on the contrary, they both reacted in exactly the opposite manner, dropping their heads onto the desk and looking discouraged and exhausted.</p>

<p>Shizuku looked back at me with teary eyes and shook her head as if to say that she wanted to give up.</p>
<p>There was nothing I could do about it.</p>

<p>I wondered if I should say something to Kirasaka next to me, but when I turned my gaze to the side, she muttered quietly with emotionless eyes, as if she were at the end of the world.</p>

<p>"I won't be going on the school trip..."</p>

<p>"Go..."</p>

<p>By no means is it my or Yuuto's fault.</p>

<p>Not that there was anything wrong with that, but for some reason I decided to keep it close to my chest that I felt bad for both of them.</p>
