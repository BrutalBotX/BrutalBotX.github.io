<h1>#292</h1>
<hr />
<p> </p>
<p>The unique atmosphere of after school, with the shouts of sports clubs from outside, occasionally hearing laughter, and all of it wrapped in a profound silence.</p>
<p>Some might find it a lonely atmosphere, but I quite like this unique feeling. When I want to think about something or just spend time alone vaguely, I can confidently say that, while I’m not a student who loudly proclaims love for school, I do like it after school.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I let Shizuku head to the student council room first, and Kirasaka mentioned that she would be in the library looking at books on making sweets until our activities for today are over.</p>
<p>Yuuto, seemingly tasked with some shopping by his mother, went home early today. So, the only one left in the classroom is me.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Watching the energetic first-year students sitting on the grass in the courtyard, engrossed in conversation with friends, I occasionally recall Miura’s words passing through my mind.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…I wonder if they’re getting excited about Valentine’s too.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I murmured as I gazed at the students below from my seat by the window.</p>
<p>Three female students were sitting on the grass, and perhaps they were discussing what kind of chocolate to give to male students.</p>
<p>This, too, is a page of youth.</p>
<p>Right now, it’s unbearably enjoyable, and both the past and the future are nothing more than unnecessary problems.</p>
<p>This moment is everything, and that’s what we, as students, are revelling in.</p>
<p>Such scenes were reflected in my eyes.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…Shall we go, then?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I do feel like enjoying a little more of my time alone, but there’s student council work waiting for us after this.</p>
<p>While I’m adept at slacking off, I’m terrible at shirking responsibility.</p>
<p>It leaves a bad aftertaste.</p>
<p>Coming face to face with others and coming up with excuses for feeling guilty is a complete waste of time.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Taking my bag off the desk, I closed the door of the now empty classroom and headed to the student council room.</p>
<p>Passing by the faculty room of one building, climbing the stairs, I opened the heavy door of the student council room with practiced hands.</p>
<p>The cold doorknob stole the warmth from my hand, and in return, warm air from the opened room embraced my whole body.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Good work, Shinra-kun.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“You’re late. The meeting can’t start without you.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Koizumi, the student council president, greeted me with a smile from the farthest seat, while Miura, from the seat right in front of his, gave me a slightly stern remark with a wry smile.</p>
<p>At the seat across from where I would sit, Hino-kun stood up, considerately brewing warm tea, and Shizuku pulled out my chair with a smile.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Sorry… I was just lost in thought.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“You’re always thinking about something. I’m a bit worried about your future.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“It’s okay. I was just thinking about how to repel my sister’s boyfriend when she gets one.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“That’s dangerous.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Casually chatting with Koizumi, I apologized for being late and took a seat. Steam rose from the tea offered from my left rear, and at the same time, I noticed the trembling arm.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Sister… H-H-Has your s-sister got a boyfriend!? Th-that’s impossible!?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“She didn’t. Or rather, it has nothing to do with you, Hino-kun.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Hino-kun, who strangely admires my sister, opened his mouth with a look of shock and despair, but when I denied it, he breathed a sigh of relief.</p>
<p>What a weird person… but I’ve known that for a while.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>With everyone now seated and everything prepared, Koizumi cleared his throat as a signal.</p>
<p>It was a sign that the regular student council meeting for today was starting.</p>
<p>With Koizumi’s words as a cue, the meeting began, and Vice President Shiraishi took on the role of facilitator. Shizuku wrote important items on the whiteboard in beautiful handwriting, Miura tapped the calculator while looking at sheets with numbers, and Koizumi groaned thoughtfully.</p>
<p>Hino-kun, mindful of everyone’s drinks, handed out materials and writing utensils as needed.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>After reading through the contents written by Shizuku, I pointed out unnecessary items that had been continued for reasons like tradition.</p>
<p>A new form of student council.</p>
<p>In the past, there was a tendency to neglect individual thinking due to the absolute presence of President. To compensate for that, everyone is now encouraged to think, speak, and act.</p>
<p>The form of the student council that Koizumi is creating is finally coming together.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Then, let’s propose to the teachers that we cut the decoration costs for the third-year send-off party and allocate that budget to gifts, even if it’s a small amount.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I’ll go to the staff room right now. Shiraishi-san, could you come with me?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Huh? Oh, y-yes, I understand.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Miura called out to Shiraishi, holding a paper with corrected figures for the budget. Shiraishi seemed surprised but quickly stood up and followed Miura.</p>
<p>With the ones being sent off behind them, the two disappeared from the student council room. Simultaneously, a sense of a short break settled in the room.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“It’s time to send off the third-years… It’s sad that President is graduating.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Koizumi murmured. His expression couldn’t hide the genuine sadness, and it seemed like he was even resisting the very idea of the event he was in-charge of.</p>
<p>However, those words were left unspoken. Time and age cannot be stopped by anyone’s will.</p>
<p>Refusing it, denying it, that moment will come.</p>
<p>At least what we can do is to show a strong attitude, even if it’s a bluff, so that the seniors, President, won’t leave with sadness, regret, or worries.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Just show that you’ll take care of the school after her with dignity… That’s probably what she wants.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Sad farewells are not desired. Whether it’s the first-year students with short connections or the second-year students who spent a lot of time together, this is the last opportunity for everyone to meet the third-year students in a proper farewell event.</p>
<p>If the student council in charge of the event has a sad mood, it won’t be solemn at all.</p>
<p>When I conveyed this to Koizumi, he nodded slightly in agreement.</p>
<p>Feeling a slightly melancholic atmosphere, Hino-kun stood up boisterously.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“I’ll bring some tea snacks! Would you prefer Japanese or Western sweets?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It was a question for everyone, but naturally, my eyes and Shizuku’s eyes turned to Koizumi.</p>
<p>Honestly, both Shizuku and I didn’t really care… or so we thought. At that moment, it hit me.</p>
<p>It was a question for everyone, but naturally, my and Shizuku’s gaze turned to Koizumi.</p>
<p>Honestly, both Shizuku and I didn’t really care… or so we thought.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Hino-kun, do you have any chocolate?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Chocolate? Um, wait a moment.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Hino-kun murmured as he searched inside a cupboard at the back of the student council room.</p>
<p>This was the chance. If there was chocolate, we could make it today’s snack and smoothly transition to asking what kind of chocolate he liked.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Shizuku, who was sitting next to me, also noticed, and she turned her face towards me, nodding with sparkling eyes.</p>
<p>…As expected of Minato-kun, always coming up with good ideas. That’s right, it’s me, who came up with this brilliant idea.</p>
<p>Contrary to our expectations, Hino-kun made a troubled sound as he rummaged through the cupboard.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Oh, I don’t have any… now that I think about it, there used to be some, but I haven’t seen any lately.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Ah… about that.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As Hino-kun muttered, my ingenious plan seemed to be rejected. At that moment, Koizumi spoke with an apologetic tone.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Actually, I’m not good with chocolate… Recently, I’ve been in charge of buying snacks, and since I don’t like it, I haven’t bought any… I’m sorry.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>With Koizumi’s apology, Shizuku and I exchanged glances with a sense of disappointment.</p>
<p>Inaudibly, I whispered to Shizuku.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“It’s messed up now, isn’t it?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Yes, it seems we did… Miura-san, it seems your increased chocolate repertoire won’t do any good here.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Normally, I would be a little nervous at the flowery scent and body temperature coming from my childhood friend in front of me, but this time it didn’t happen.</p>
<p>Miura-san… didn’t know that he didn’t like chocolate.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>When Miura returned to the student council room, she looked at me and Shizuku with a puzzled expression, wondering what had occurred.</p>
