<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0">#220</h1>

<hr>
<p>From the vehicle that took me away from the after-school preparation activities, I looked at the unfamiliar cityscape, which was different from what I'm used to seeing.</p>

<p>I had heard that Kirasaka's house was in the opposite direction to mine.</p>
<p>But I never had the chance to go there, and it was also her villa that I visited as a part-timer during the summer holidays, not her actual home.</p>

<p>Even that villa was as luxurious as I expected it to be, so secretly I'm wondering what kind of a mansion her house would be.</p>
<p>I peeked at President and Kirasaka, who were accompanying me, looked calm as usual.</p>

<p>President has been familiar with Kirasaka's place for a long time, so it is only natural that she is not nervous.</p>
<p>Away from the Sakuranaoka Academy, surprisingly, the car stopped when we had proceeded close to the mountains.</p>

<p>A wooden gate surrounded by a fine stone wall fence appeared there, a short distance from the residential area, blocking the car's way.</p>

<p>"Miss, I'll put the car around the back, this way."</p>

<p>"Thank you, Grandpa."</p>

<p>After Kirasaka had a short conversation with the driver, Kuroi-san, I got out of the car as if urged to do so and just stood in front of the gate in a daze.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>It's huge.</p>
<p>Yes, the gate alone is probably more than the width of a car, and the length is well over two meters.</p>
<p>It may be my own assumption, but it is surprising because I had the image that rich people's houses tended to be luxurious, Western-style buildings.</p>

<p>The three of us got out of the vehicle and the limousine we were riding in went on its way somewhere.</p>
<p>Kirasaka walks up to the gate, knocks lightly a few times and the gate slowly opens with a heavy thud.</p>

<p>"Welcome home, miss."</p>
<p></p>
<p>"I'm home, is father in his study?"</p>

<p>After asking the woman at the other end of the gate, Kirasaka goes on ahead.</p>
<p>President was also walking behind her, but did not seem to be wondering anything.</p>

<p>Am I the only one confused by this situation?</p>
<p>First of all, what about the gate, and what about the women waiting at the end of the opening?</p>
<p>A maid, a housekeeper?</p>

<p>Besides, I look at the garden past the gate, and it is huge.</p>
<p>Beautifully landscaped garden plants and a large pond.</p>

<p>The side of the cobbled path I am now walking on was strewn with white pebbles, creating a white scene.</p>
<p>A picturesque mansion is just ahead of my gaze, making it clear what the owner of this house likes to do.</p>

<p>Continuing along the cobblestones, my gaze shifts to the side just before the front door.</p>

<p>There is a trench-like depression from where bonsai trees are neatly lined up, perhaps a hobby of Kirasaka's father, and water flows through it as if it were a replica of a river.</p>

<p>The house is full of things that would be strange to find in an ordinary home, and to be honest, I'm a little taken aback, but because it's Kirasaka's house, I somehow managed to endure it without reacting in an over-dramatic way.</p>

<p>No, normally when you see a house like this, you want to say something out loud.</p>

<p>With Kirasaka leading the way, we stop in front of the entrance and she opens the heavy front door.</p>
<p>The wide-open front door welcomes us, and at the end of it is Kuroi-san, who was driving the car just a few minutes ago.</p>

<p>"I have already reported to Master that you have arrived, miss."</p>

<p>"Right, then Grandpa can go back."</p>

<p>When Kirasaka tells him briefly, Kuroi-san gives a small bow and leaves the scene.</p>
<p>Fearfully, I stepped through the front door onto the floor of the Kirasaka household and proceeded down the old-fashioned corridor.</p>

<p>After advancing down the long corridor, Kirasaka stops in front of a door at the innermost part of the mansion and knocks on the door of the room.</p>

<p>"It's Rei."</p>

<p>There is no response to this call from inside.</p>
<p>Kirasaka puts her hand on the door and opens it without any sign of hesitation.</p>

<p>"President, I beg your pardon then!"</p>

<p>A middle-aged man in an expensive-looking suit bowed deeply as we entered the room.</p>
<p>He left the room at the same time as the time we entered.</p>

<p>His expression and the sound of his voice showed that he was in a hurry.</p>
<p>...I'm still wondering why I've been called here.</p>

<p>Inwardly, I couldn't stop sweating, an uncomfortable feeling.</p>
<p>Watching the backs of the man who had left, and standing alone at the entrance, the two people ahead of me went inside the room without paying attention to him.</p>

<p>"Father, I've brought them both here."</p>

<p>"Welcome back Rei, sorry for asking so suddenly."</p>

<p>After the exchange of conversation between father and daughter, his gaze turns towards us.</p>
<p>President bowed her head in a familiar manner shortly after.</p>

<p>"Long time no see, Oji-sama*."<br><span class="EOP">&nbsp;&lt;TL : *Uncle&gt;</span></p>
<p>"Akane has also become a beautiful woman in the time since I last saw her."</p>
<p>
</p><p>"Thank you very much."</p>
<p>
</p><p>The two, who had known each other for a long time, exchanged compliments.</p>
<p>Then Kirasaka's father's gaze turned to me.</p>
<p>I gulped and looked into his eyes.</p>

<p>"We haven't seen each other since your work experience, Shinra Minato-kun."</p>

<p>"...Thank you for your help on that occasion."</p>

<p>Kirasaka Reiya.</p>
<p>Kirasaka Rei's father and the president of one of Japan's largest companies.</p>
<p>A figure who seemed strangely at home in such a secluded country town was standing in front of me with a scowling smile on his face.</p>

<p>I entered the room as prompted and sat down on the guest's sofa.<br>President, too, could sense a slight stiffness in the expression on his face.</p>

<p>It's only natural since we don't know the reason for being summoned to this room.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The room is called a study, and the walls are lined with various books.<br>The amount of books is so great that counting them can become a chore.</p>
<p>In such a room, Kirasaka Reiya was sitting on an expensive-looking chair, sorting out the paperwork at hand.</p>

<p>"A joint cultural festival with the Kikyo Girls' Academy is an interesting experiment."</p>

<p>Said the father as Kirasaka served drinks to me and President, and sat himself down opposite me.</p>
<p>I turned my gaze to Kirasaka to see if he had received information from her daughter, but she shook her head and denied my expectations.</p>

<p>"Did you know?"</p>

<p>"The shopping district is also involved, so naturally..."</p>

<p>In response to the President's question, Kirasaka Reiya gave a short answer.</p>
<p>I remember overhearing that this person was contributing to the shopping district, or something like that.</p>

<p>If you become as big a figure as this person, your information network is immeasurable.</p>
<p>I guess information comes in even if you don't want it to.</p>

<p>"I've heard and grasped a little about the joint overview from my daughter. I think Akane thought about it at one point, but I called her in with a suggestion about securing the means of transport."</p>

<p>President's expression changes at that comment.</p>
<p>Certainly, the only side that has not secured a means of transport at the scene is the Sakuranaoka Academy, and the other side was asked if it could not be secured prior to the event.</p>

<p>However, they came to the view that it would be difficult to do so due to budgetary problems, but if they say they have called that matter, that means there is a possibility to prepare for it.</p>
<p>As a student council, we don't want to miss this opportunity.</p>

<p>"However, there is someone I would like to have a word with, not to say a condition, and that's why I asked Rei to invite you."</p>

<p>The eyes that should have been looking down to his hands for work are raised and are directed at me.</p>
<p>The reason why hasn't he just invited the President?</p>

<p>The reason is that it's the continuation of the work experience.</p>
<p>In a situation where President and Kirasaka are quietly looking on, I quietly exhale.</p>
<p>Unlike before, my nervousness jumps because I know that this person is much bigger than I imagined.</p>
<p>As if enjoying this state of mind and all of my gestures, Kirasaka Reiya stands up and sits down next to her daughter.</p>

<p>...I sighed inwardly, thinking that I had been spotted by a troublesome family, with both parents and children being fond of unusual people.</p>
