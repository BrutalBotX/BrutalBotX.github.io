<h1 style="text-align: center">#296</h1>
<hr />
<p> </p>
<p>“Happy Valentine!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>In the Shinra household, cheerful voices overlapped as Shizuku and Mom handed me two beautifully wrapped gifts early in the morning.</p>
<p>It was February 14th, Valentine’s Day.</p>
<p>In the world, it’s a day when women give chocolates to men. Recently, it has become common to give chocolates to same-sex friends as a sign of friendship, but the unchanged enthusiasm still colors not only the surroundings but also the streets.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>And our house is no exception.</p>
<p>The small packages handed to me by my sister and mother were filled with chocolates from the corner to the corner, adorned with plenty of heart shapes.</p>
<p>If you’re not a sweet tooth, just this much could lead to heartburn.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Thanks…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It had become an annual tradition, but it’s always nice to receive something. After expressing my gratitude to the two, I turned my gaze to the other family member sitting on the sofa in the living room.</p>
<p>My father, who usually works overseas, was sitting at home today, reading the newspaper with an innocent expression.</p>
<p>The reason was simple; he had been eagerly anticipating Valentine’s chocolate from his beloved wife and daughter. Even in his busy schedule, this event was an indispensable and crucial affair for him as a father.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“What…?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Nothing.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I unintentionally asked when I felt his gaze, but as if nothing had happened, he returned his eyes to the newspaper, and the words that came out of his mouth were short.</p>
<p>It seemed that his mood was a bit off, probably because of what I was holding in my hands.</p>
<p>On the table in front of my father, there was a similarly wrapped confectionery.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>In terms of order, I don’t think there’s anything strange, considering that my father was awake early to receive it. However…</p>
<p>To my questioning gaze, Kaede spoke as if representing our father’s feelings.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Dad is sulking because your chocolate is bigger, Nii-san.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Is he a child…?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Come on…</p>
<p>Since it’s homemade, there might naturally be some difference in size. I’m starting to feel like the one sulking here is the one approaching forty.</p>
<p>As I sighed, Kaede raised her eyebrows, murmured in a low voice, and showed a slight smile.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“You’ll understand when you become a father. Whether you find a partner or not is uncertain, though.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“You’re saying one too many things. Dad was probably the type picked up by Mom.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Also, this father has children.</p>
<p>Regardless of the outcome, he was picked up by Mom, so I don’t want to hear anything about a partner from him.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Come on, both of you, no fighting in the morning.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As a chilly atmosphere lingered between father and son from the beginning of the day, Mom, who intervened, changed the air completely. Just like that, with a single word from Mom, I averted my gaze, and she moved towards the table after pouring vegetable juice into a glass from the refrigerator.</p>
<p>Observing the received gift once again, Mom added some information and opened her mouth.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Kaede was so determined this year, she even asked your father for chocolates from overseas.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Oh, really…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Even as my sister, admiration leaked out for her enthusiasm. However, there didn’t seem to be a reason to devote more effort than usual this year, as it wasn’t a celebratory year.</p>
<p>Looking at Kaede, who was praising herself, I easily got the answer from my sister.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“There seem to be many rivals this year—Shizuku-san, Rei-san, and even Akane-san. If that’s the case, I have to do my best too!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“We’re a family, so it should be priceless.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“After all, not wanting to lose to others is the heart of a maiden! Now, Nii-san, eat it!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Kaede urged me to eat with her palm pushing my hand from below towards my mouth. I involuntarily flinched.</p>
<p>My stomach, speaking in the manner of a teenage guy, was saying it might survive with minor injuries, but it was definitely claiming that this wasn’t the first thing it wanted this morning.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Starting the day with a sugar boost… At least after breakfast, please.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>While feeling a bit guilty against my sparkling-eyed sister, I considered begging for vegetable juice at least if breakfast was a no-go. As I returned my words, Mom, unsurprisingly, wasn’t silent.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“No sweets in the morning.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Of course…</p>
<p>Not many parents would allow their children to eat sweets before having the breakfast their mother prepared.</p>
<p>Taking the package into my palm and placing it gently on the counter nearby, I moved the chair where Kaede and I were sitting back, and Dad moved to the seat opposite us.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Recently, it seems like my Nii-san has been neglecting family service. So, this imouto wants her brother to show some family love before going to school.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Yeah, yeah…”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As I sat down, shoulder to shoulder with my sister, I raised the glass to my mouth with my left hand, while using my right hand to gently pat Kaede’s head, showing a somewhat perfunctory attitude towards my sister’s complaints. This might seem like a small thing, but surprisingly, it’s quite effective.</p>
<p>Smiling with raised corners of her mouth and closed eyes, Kaede presented a passive gaze, as if a cat were being petted. Oh, brother, my sister might be too cheeky, and I might be a bit worried about her future.</p>
<p>Will she be swindled by a bad person? It looks like a lifetime employment for the big brother is needed to protect her surroundings.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Oh, if you’re going to provide a service to your sister, you should also be filial to your parents!”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…Why do we have to perform a rigid family friendship drama on a morning with school?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I unintentionally muttered my honest feelings in response to Mom, who leaned forward from the table, showing her competitive spirit. Unleashing a movie-like drama on family love right from the early morning is an excess of sugar compared to chocolates.</p>
<p>Finally, after sighing, I emptied the glass of the drink and picked up chopsticks to have breakfast. Kaede then leaned her head on my shoulder, making it a bit difficult to eat.</p>
<p>Eating like this is quite challenging.</p>
<p>However, this is Minato-kun’s quality; not pushing her away.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“What’s up?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>While reaching for the fried egg with my chopsticks, I asked. The eternal debate of whether to use soy sauce or sauce on a fried egg could be the subject of an everlasting thesis.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Hmm, did you feel happy to receive chocolate from me, big brother?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Don’t ask the obvious. Is there a big brother who wouldn’t be happy to receive chocolate from his sister?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Then… more than from Shizuku-san and the others?”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“…It’s wrong to compare. There’s gratitude, but there’s no need for comparison.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As I answered Kaede’s question, my sister smiled satisfied. It seemed like she was expressing with her expression that the expected answer had been received.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Since I’m having breakfast, move away.”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>“Just a little more~”</p>
<p> </p>
<p>…The food is getting cold.</p>
<p>Even so, the undeniable evidence that I can’t push her away is my sister’s sweetness.</p>
<p> </p>
