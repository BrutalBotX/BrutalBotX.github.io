<hr>
<p>Day Two</p>
<p>The day started without any incident.</p>

<p>The girls had left some light snacks for us to eat at dinner yesterday, so after we took them, we gathered in the student council room.</p>

<p>My sleeping habits were 30% worse than usual, but I didn't mind and took my seat.</p>

<p>"Minato-kun, your sleeping habits are terrible, you know?"</p>

<p>"...Yeah, I guess it is."</p>

<p>The hair that waved left and to the right was in my line of sight, and to be honest, it was a little annoying.<br>As I was thinking about going for a haircut sometime, Shizuku took a comb out of her hand bag and without saying a word, started to style my hair.</p>

<p>I refused to brush it away with my hand a few times, but she grabbed my head stubbornly, so I decided to follow her quietly.<br>Shizuku, who was humming happily, repeatedly adjusted my hair, walked around to the front, and fixed it a little if it wasn't to her liking.</p>

<p>Finally, when she was satisfied with the way my hair looked, she smiled and patted my head.</p>

<p>"There we go! You're done."</p>

<p>"Sorry ...Thanks."</p>

<p>"No problem."</p>

<p>Then Shizuku sat down on the seat next to me.<br>Same as yesterday, Kirasaka sat on the opposite side of her fixed seat with an indifferent look.</p>

<p>Kirasaka who was sitting there with a dignified expression on her face, still looking down at the books she had brought with her.</p>

<p>After confirming that all of the student council members were seated, the president began to speak.</p>

<p>"Did everyone sleep well last night? Let's work hard for another day."</p>

<p>After the president finished her short speech, each of us begins to complete the remaining tasks.<br>I followed their lead and was about to take out my study materials from my bag to finish the remaining assignments.</p>

<p>"---But before that, I have something to tell everyone."</p>

<p>With that, the atmosphere in the room completely changed.<br>The atmosphere in the classroom, which until now had been somewhat relaxed due to the fact that it was morning, turned tense and suffocating.</p>

<p>Hino opens his empty eyes, and Shizuku straightened her back and turned to face the president.<br>The only one who remained unchanged was Kirasaka.</p>

<p>I also straightened my posture a bit and turned my gaze towards the president.</p>

<p>"I think you would've been aware of this, but I've been discussing something with Koizumi the other day.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Koizumi listened to the President's words with an expression that still remained uncertain.<br>I think it's time to hear the truth behind this strange feeling.</p>
<p>When I thought about it, I felt a simultaneous instinct to hurry up the president and at the same time not want to get involved in anything troublesome.</p>
<p>If I could, I would probably be happier if I never asked.<br>But as long as I was here, I couldn't do that.</p>

<p>As I waited for the next words, the president began to talk about the core of the problem.</p>

<p>"It's about the student council election."</p>

<p>"...Hmm? Elections?"</p>

<p>Of course I know about student council elections.<br>I even voted for our president last year, so it's no surprise.</p>

<p>But if it's about that, it's not much of a problem, is it?</p>

<p>I'm not sure if I or Hino-kun will still be in the student council next year, but Miura and Koizumi will definitely be active as the core of the student council.<br>The only problem I can think of with that is that it's a common story that there are no new candidates for the council.</p>

<p>If that's the case, it makes a little sense why Koizumi's expression doesn't brighten.<br>However, there was something that bothered me.</p>

<p>If that's the case, there's no need for them to talk in private.<br>It should be okay to talk openly about the fact that we don't have any potential candidates either.</p>

<p>That's why it is not the essence of this problem.</p>

<p>President looked at Koizumi once, and when she saw that Koizumi nodded, she quietly announced.</p>

<p>"A candidate for president has emerged besides Koizumi."</p>




<p>
</p>
<p>The student council is an organization made up of several people who represent the students of the school.</p>
<p>The name and form of the organization varies slightly from school to school.</p>

<p>However, they all consist of a single representative, the student council president, who is assisted by students.<br>Our school is no different.</p>

<p>In the current student council, there are five members: Akane Hiiragi as the president, Koizumi as the vice president, Miura as the treasurer, me as the president's assistant, and Hino-kun as the general secretary.</p>

<p>However, since I and Hino-kun joined the council due to the president's special exception, the other three members were carefully selected through elections.</p>
<p>However, unlike real world elections, this is a simplified, smaller version.</p>

<p>In a short period of time, candidates are given the opportunity to campaign, or in simpler terms, to promote themselves.<br>During the first day of school, they greet students in front of the school gate to let them know who they are and their names.</p>

<p>Then, during the school assembly, they briefly take the stage and speak to the students about their aspirations.</p>

<p>For most of the students, this event is probably irrelevant.<br>Last year, it was one of those events that I didn't really care much about either.</p>

<p>Well, it wasn't really an election last year, since there was no other candidate because the monstrous Akane Hiiragi.<br>However, I vaguely remember Koizumi and Miura also standing on the stage and talking.</p>

<p>A lot of elections are like popularity contests, and Koizumi is a popular student because he is well known, calm, and friendly.<br>That's why I had assumed that Koizumi would be the next student body president.</p>

<p>In fact, when the president first introduced him, he said that he was going to be the student body president.<br>He said this because of his past achievements as the vice president.</p>

<p>That's why it was unexpected that someone would appear as the president's candidate.</p>

<p>More importantly, the other candidate wasn't from the student council.</p>
<p>And the most significant problem is that he must have had a chance to beat Koizumi in the election.</p>


<p>For a moment, I can think of three students who fit that description.<br>Shizuku Kanzaki, Rei Kirasaka, and Yuuto Ogiwara are the three.</p>

<p>I'm sorry to say this to Koizumi, but if these three were your rivals, I have no doubt that they would have won.</p>
<p>That's how simple and scary student elections can be.</p>

<p>However, I don't think the three of them will be running for office.<br>Shizuku and Kirasaka are both here.</p>

<p>If Yuuto were to run for office, he would have at least told me about it.</p>

<p>That's why I never expected this.<br>President, myself, and above all, Koizumi.</p>


<p>In the quiet of the classroom, president announced the name of the candidate with her eyes closed.</p>

<p>"Shiraishi Momiji, a a freshman at Sakuranooka Academy."</p>



<p>Shiraishi Momiji-san... right?</p>
<p>...Yeah, no, doesn't ring a bell.</p>
