<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading1" style="text-align: center">246<span> </span></h1>
<hr />
<p> </p>
<p>The students of Sakuranaoka Academy, after arriving at Naha Airport, boarded a bus arranged by the school, heading towards Shuri Castle.</p>
<p>Along the way, the bus guide told us about the history and various information about Okinawa.</p>
<p>As for the general information, each student does their own research before going on the school trip.<br />However, the guide, who spoke eloquently about detailed local knowledge and other details, was a pleasure to listen to.</p>
<p>However, there were many who were already starting to doze off.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Sitting by the window inside the bus, I gazed at the Okinawan scenery. <br class="zw-br" />It was noticeably different from Kanagawa, where I lived, in terms of architecture and shops.</p>
<p>While the airport area was modern and developed, taking a drive through the city revealed distinct differences. <br class="zw-br" />The predominant use of red in the color scheme, the presence of Shisa statues in various places, and even the appearance of convenience stores surprised me.</p>
<p>Even the major convenience store chain, which usually had a blue theme, had changed its exterior to a dark brown here.</p>
<p>The cityscape, which is different from the usual, is interesting to see as a tourist.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>A school trip's primary purpose was for students to visit historically significant places and learn from firsthand experiences.</p>
<p>Reflecting on my middle school trip to Kyoto and Nara, I couldn't recall any particularly enjoyable memories. <br class="zw-br" />However, I vividly remembered feeling awe at historical and cultural sites, realizing the significance of history and technology.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>During that trip, we didn't have any extra worries, and Yuuto chatted happily while Shizuku was also unusually excited.</p>
<p>Travel had a way of liberating people's hearts.</p>
<p>Without exception, this time is the same.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Some students asked questions during the bus guide's explanation, not necessarily because they were deeply interested but because of sheer excitement.</p>
<p>Brightening the mood inside the bus, making conversations lively, and being at the center of discussions was enjoyable for them.</p>
<p>I looked at Yuuto, who was sitting next to me, handing me the gum he had brought with him, and I silently accepted it.<br class="zw-br" />Smiling, he too tossed one into his mouth.</p>
<p>Turning his gaze towards our classmates, Yuuto leaned slightly forward and called out to the students from the central aisle in front of us.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Do you both want some gum too?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"It's alright."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"I'll refrain."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Although their responses were lost amid the bus's chatter, they reached us clearly.</p>
<p>I couldn't see his expression, but I couldn't help but smile bitterly as he was immediately refused.</p>
<p>Reluctantly, Yuuto straightens his posture and lets out a breath as he looks outside the window.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>We're close to our destination.</p>
<p>Since the name of Shurijo Castle can be seen here and there on signboards, etc., it is likely that the crowd will last for a while.</p>
<p>However, this closeness also represents human relationships.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>If there are no designated seats on a train, the seating will naturally be decided by faction or influence.</p>
<p>Quiet students in the front, noisy students in the back.</p>
<p>High school students are simply divided into two like that.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>When we were getting on the bus, Yuuto and his friends, regardless of gender, were calling out to them from behind, but we sat down a little in front of them.</p>
<p>Nakayama and Miyashita were at the back of the line, as well as many mischievous students with bright hair.</p>
<p>They could be described as 'sunny' students. <br class="zw-br" />In terms of the school's social hierarchy, these upper-tier students carried themselves with confidence and were making a commotion.</p>
<p>Some of the students sitting in front of them glanced back with slightly furrowed brows, but none of them uttered a word of complaint.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>It was an unspoken agreement.</p>
<p>As long as the teacher didn't find a reason to intervene, this was a lawless zone...</p>
<p>But speculatively speaking, our bus seemed somewhat quieter than the others.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The reason was straightforward.</p>
<p>There were three students on this bus who held significant influence and power.</p>
<p>Shizuku, Yuuto, and Kirasaka.</p>
<p>As long as these three were around, the students in the back couldn't get away with being too rowdy.</p>
<p>The classmates who wanted to make this trip a great memory by involving these three had already stumbled in securing seats on this bus.</p>


<p>"We'll be arriving shortly."</p>
<p>The female guide announced, causing the students to momentarily raise their voices in excitement.</p>
<p>They quickly stored their valuables and minimal writing tools in their uniform pockets, preparing for the upcoming stop.</p>
<p>Once the bus parked at Shuri Castle's designated parking area, each class gathered together and began walking toward the site.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"...It's a Real Shisa."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"It's the real thing, the real Shisa."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The Shisa guardian statues sitting just in front of the large gate were weathered due to the elements.</p>
<p>But that was part of their charm. Yeah.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I didn't miss the moment when Kirasaka passed by.</p>
<p>Thinking of sending a photo to Kaede, I activated the camera on my smartphone for the first time in a while and took a picture.</p>
<p>After capturing all the permissible areas on camera, it was finally time to proceed to Shuri Castle's main hall.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Passing through the gate and entering what appeared to be a large hall, the view opened up to reveal a collection of predominantly red buildings.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"From now on, it's the Shuri Castle..."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>As we looked at the historical significance of the buildings before us, the guide provided detailed explanations, including the time when it was designated as a World Heritage site.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"It's amazing... I wish I could show this to Kaede-chan."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Her school is either going to Kyoto or Hokkaido, right?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>My sister wouldn't have the chance to see this scenery unless she comes on a trip herself.</p>
<p>Shizuku, who was next to her and knew about it, muttered a little sadly.</p>
<p>Kirasaka, who was also nearby, seemed uninterested, probably because she had a vacation home.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"How about all of us coming again next time?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Troublesome..."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Going to the airport, enduring a long journey, exploring an unfamiliar place...</p>
<p>It all depended on whether you found it enjoyable or not.</p>
<p>Honestly, I didn't like traveling far, even within the country.</p>
<p>Or rather, I didn't want to leave my house at all.</p>
<p>I aspired to be the ultimate shut-in and wished for a future with self-employment and remote work.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>However, my response was likely already anticipated, and no one paid it any mind. <br class="zw-br" />So, Shizuku asked Kirasaka the same question without acknowledging my opinion.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"How about you, Kirasaka-san?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"If Kaede-chan wants to go, I'll make arrangements for the flights and accommodations. It's only natural to do this much for my future little sister."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Then it's settled, except for the part about your future sister."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>...What about my opinion?</p>
<p>Well, I won't give you my sister.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Even if I say something like a father in a drama, I have the confidence to declare in front of her boyfriend that I wouldn't hand her over to him.</p>
<p>I even had a plan ready, like taking her outside and then letting her go home alone as a way to test her. It was all perfectly thought out.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The two exchanged a brief, sharp gaze but quickly returned to their usual expressions.</p>
<p>Although I said it's a hassle, I don't intend to return back without making any memories, so I just tried to remember the scene in front of me.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>After enjoying it enough, I noticed that a group of people had gathered at the edge of my field of vision.</p>
<p>Mostly led by Yuuto, many students were engaged in taking photographs.</p>
<p>...Is he an idol?</p>
<p> </p>
<p>While making my way to a place with explanations about Shurijo Castle, I thought of listening in on their conversation, but it turned out to be something trivial.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Aya, I want to take a picture with Ogiwara-kun, so please take a photo for us."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"I want to take one next—"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Hurry up! We're running out of time, you know."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Nakayama grabbed Yuuto's arm and led him to the best photo spot.</p>
<p>As for Miyashita... or rather, her name is Aya, I just found out.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Miyashita, seemed like she was about to ask for a turn, but Nakayama promptly urged her to hurry up.</p>
<p>Although it was a slight change in her expression, she showed a dissatisfied look.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>However, Nakayama didn't pay any attention to such a small change.</p>
<p>Even though they were friends, there was already a clear hierarchy between the two.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Yuuto, feeling uncertain about whether he should intervene in their exchange, turned his gaze toward the camera.</p>
<p>I was behind them, and our gazes naturally met with Yuuto's.</p>
<p>In his gaze that seemed to seek assistance, I couldn't help but let out a sigh.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>If our relationship had been shallower, I would have had to wonder about the meaning behind those eyes.</p>
<p>Even though I knew there was a need and that it wouldn't lead to anything good for me, I walked over to where the three of them were.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Okay, cheese... I got it, Yuu(Nakayama). This time—"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Thank you! Everyone moved ahead, didn't they?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Nakayama received her smartphone back from Miyashita and went on to follow her classmates to the next destination.</p>
<p>As she sadly watched their departing figures, Nakayama held her own smartphone in her hand.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"...Yuuto, hurry up."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>I said as I stopped next to Miyashita.</p>
<p>I felt a momentarily displeased look directed at me from beside me, but I'm currently the ideal classmate.</p>
<p>I held out my hand and told her.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Ah, thank you..."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Miyashita tidied her black hair and then handed me her smartphone.</p>
<p>Standing next to Yuuto, she adjusted her clothing and blushed slightly while turning her gaze towards me.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Fortunately, she was using the latest model of the smartphone I use, so there were no issues with the operation.</p>
<p>It was difficult to fit both of them in the frame with the building, so as I repeatedly shrunk the screen, Shizuku and Kirasaka peered at the same screen as if sandwiching our faces.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"A little farther apart might be better."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Is this okay?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>In response to Shizuku's comment, Kirasaka adjusted the screen with her white fingers.</p>
<p>In accordance with that, I called out...</p>
<p> </p>
<p>By the way, I've never really done photography of people before. <br class="zw-br" />Do I just say 'cheese'? I heard it can vary by region.</p>
<p>Since everyone seemed puzzled by my lack of pressing the capture button, I felt their eyes on me, so I decided to say something appropriate.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"Maltese."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"A dog?"</p>
<p> </p>
<p>"It's a dog."</p>
<p> </p>
<p>The words I randomly chose received comments from both sides. For a moment, Yuuto and Miyashita had a vacant look on their faces, but then they smiled happily.</p>
<p>The captured expressions in the camera were photos of those smiling faces.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>...Maybe I should become a photographer.</p>
<p>The cameraman accidentally took a shot that was beyond his ability.</p>
