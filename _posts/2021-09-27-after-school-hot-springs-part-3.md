<p>The next story will be a new chapter.</p>
<p>==============================================================================</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Following Kirasaka, we visited a hot spring, or perhaps it would be more appropriate to call it an inn.</p>
<p>The inn was nostalgic, but spotless and tidy.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>　It was easy to imagine that the employees took care of the maintenance on a daily basis.</p>
<p>It was as if the parents of Kirasaka were involved with the facility.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Since it was after school when we visited the inn, we didn't relax in the lobby, but instead split up into men and women and headed for the bathhouse.</p>
<p>I went into the men's bath with Yuuto in tow.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>The first thought that came to mind was, "Is this really okay to do such a luxurious thing?"</p>
<p>"There's an open-air bath too!? I'll go there later."</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"...Yeah."</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Maybe Yuuto next to me didn't care, he said with a little excitement with his eyes shining.</p>
<p>Well, for today, let's just think of it as coming to relax our bodies and not dig too deep into unnecessary things.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I decided to enjoy the bath, which was completely empty except for me and Yuuto.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>As I finished washing my body and took my first dip in the bath, Yuuto muttered to me.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"In this kind of pattern, hearing the conversation over from the women's bath or ...is standard.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"You've been reading too many manga..."</p>
<p>All we could hear was the sound of hot water being poured and our conversation.</p>
<p>The fact that it was just the two of us in such a large hot spring made me feel uncomfortable.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>There was also a sauna and a water bath, but I didn't like them much.</p>
<p>After warming up for a while, we went to the outdoor bath.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"It's so nice"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"..."</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I leaned back and looked up at the sky as I soaked in the hot water.</p>
<p>As summer approaches and the hours of daylight increase, the sky is bright, though still tinged with vermilion.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>It felt strange to me that I was here on a weekday, on my way home from school.</p>
<p>If I had come on a holiday, I would have been able to relax from the bottom of my heart.</p>
<p>If I listened carefully, I could even hear the sound of the river flowing.</p>
<p>Maybe I'll come back next time with Kaede when Mom comes home.</p>
<p>If I do that, I have to ask Kuroi-san about the way to this place.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>In the worst case scenario, I can ask Kirasaka and she should tell me right away.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"I'm going to ...leave now"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"Why don't you go a little slower, Minato?"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I feel like I'm going to have a completely long and exhausting bath at this point.</p>
<p>After saying a few words to Yuuto, I left the bathhouse.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>When I went out to the changing room, I found a simple change of clothes that hadn't been there when I came in.</p>
<p>There was also a yukata for rent, but considering that we would be leaving in a bit, it would be a double effort to change.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>So I thought I could just change into my uniform again, but I wondered if Kuroi-san had prepared this for me.</p>
<p>The Т-shirt, which was just the right size, had a pattern printed on it with letters.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;[</span>Ren LOVE] (Tl/n - 怜LOVE)</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"...Can you wear that!"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I immediately threw it on the floor.</p>
<p>When did Kirasaka prepare such a stupid thing!?</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>As I let out a sigh of dismay, I saw another shirt under it.</p>
<p>I unfolded the other shirt, truly amazed at how normally she prepared another one after it.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>[Shizuku LOVE] (Tl/n - 雫愛)</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"...It's just so I have to wear it!"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>It's a pincer attack.</p>
<p>There she was, alone in the changing room, angrily complaining about this prank that even Shizuku was played on.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>As it turned out, there were no clothes available for me to change into, so I put on my uniform and went out to the lobby.</p>
<p>There, I found massage machines, vending machines, a simple dining area, and a place where they could play table tennis and other games.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"You should give up now"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"Kirasaka-san, you're the one who keeps pestering me over and over again"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>The two of them, who were sweating a little after wearing their yukata to the hot spring, were playing table tennis against each other as they had talked about at the school.</p>
<p>I don't know what makes them so passionate, but they can't afford to lose.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I don't know the rules, but I wonder if they're still playing at deuce.</p>
<p><em>(Tl/n - I ain't a ping pong expert so here's what I found on the internet - "In tennis, a deuce is a tie that must be broken by someone scoring two points in a row.")</em></p>
<p>Kuroi-san was acting as a referee, smiling beside them, but I wondered what she was enjoying.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I bought a cup of coffee milk a little further away and watched them play their game.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"Oh, so you're doing it!"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"I don't think the game is over, though."</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Yuuto appeared with a towel on his head and sat down on the chair next to me, wiping his scraggly wet hair.</p>
<p>He also has a cup of coffee milk in his hand.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Coffee milk after a hot spring is good, isn't it?</p>
<p>You could say it's a classic.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I turned my gaze away from Yuuto for a moment, and returned it to the ping-pong table.</p>
<p>I don't know anything about the techniques, skills, rules, or format of the game except for the simple ones we had in class, but I still know something at least.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>It was hard to believe that they were beginners, they were just playing a very advanced game.</p>
<p>To a serious ping-pong player, it's probably just a rally.</p>
<p><em>(Tl/n - Consecutive back and forth shots between players)</em></p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>But to me, their ability to keep rallying at such a high speed is nothing short of amazing.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I can even think of the phrase "waste of talent"</p>
<p>For a while, Yuuto and I stood side by side, waiting for the girls to finish their game.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>It's a good way to pass the time after a bath.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Both of them were absorbed in the game with a serious look in their eyes, as if they were participating in a tournament.</p>
<p>About ten minutes after we started watching the game, we ran out of drinks and were wondering what to buy next when Kuroi-san, who was supposed to be a referee, came up to us and offered us some tea.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"Thank you very much. "</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I thanked him and accepted the tea.</p>
<p>Next to me, Yuuto also accepted the tea.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Kuroi-san smiled and turned his eyes to them, especially Kirasaka.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"The young lady really seems to be enjoying herself these days"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"I suppose..."</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>She even has a change of clothes for people with her name on it.</p>
<p>I think she's enjoying this situation well enough.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Hearing my reply, Ms. Kuroi gave a small bow to me.</p>
<p>"Everything has happened since I met Shinra-sama ...Thank you."</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"...Please stop"</p>
<p>I stood up and asked Kuroi-san to raise his head.</p>
<p>I'm not sure if I'm going to be able to do it.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"...because you met me or whatever, that's just purely a consequence"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Even if you had met me, you might have ended up differently than you are now at some juncture.</p>
<p>It's easy to look at the outcome and say that it was inevitable.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>It's against my way of thinking to assume that the current situation was created by a series of coincidences and that she met just one person.</p>
<p>As Kuroi-san said, I'm sure that we're more than a little involved in the reason why Kiraizaka is enjoying her current life.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>But I can't just nod my head and say it as if I'm the only one.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I'm not sure if he understand my intentions, but Kuroi-san still smiles.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"I know you're going to say that, Shinra-sama, but please consider this just a thank you from an old man"</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>"....Well, if that's the case..."</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I'm not sure if he understands how twisted people like me can be, so he thanked me in a way that was hard to refuse.</p>
<p>I'll take it as an honest thank you for now.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>From that point on, I watched the girls, including Kuroi-san.</p>
<p>The two of them certainly have the worst chemistry.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>When their eyes meet, they glare at each other, it's hard to win in a battle of wills like this.</p>
<p>Their specs are similar, so it should be unavoidable.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>However, it may be that they are somehow similar to each other.</p>
<p>Because of the relationship between dogs and monkeys, it was possible to get involved in earnest because the eyes were focused only on the bad parts of the other party.</p>
<p><em>(Tl/n - 'Dogs &amp; monkeys' is a phrase meaning having bad relationship with each other.)</em></p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>There is even a word for it 'bad friends'.</p>
<p>I think Kuroi-san should be thanking Shizuku, not me.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>I look out the window of the strange building, where despite being a large facility, there are only the four of us, Kuroi-san, and the employees.</p>
<p>The sun had completely set, and another day was coming to an end.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Once the girls' game is over, we'll go home and start another normal day tomorrow.</p>
<p>It was a nice break and a change of pace.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>It was a bit of a break and a change of pace, but even though it's been a normal day for me since I was promoted to the second year, it's been different from other years.</p>
<p>Also, there could be unexpected events and happenings waiting for us.</p>
<p><span class="EOP">&nbsp;</span>Aware that my supposedly refreshed mood was easily sinking, I just rested my body in this place for now.</p>

<p>This is the end of the side-story and the next story will be a new chapter.</p>
<p></p>
<p>I was wondering if I should write about the result of the ping-pong match between Shizuku and Ren, but I decided to end it without writing about it this time.</p>
<p>I'll try my best to update the story so that you can enjoy the development from the next episode.</p>
