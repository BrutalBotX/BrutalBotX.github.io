<h1 class="zw-paragraph heading0" style="text-align:center;">#145</h1>


<hr>
<p>Why am I going to help Shiraishi?</p>
<p>It should be rather weird to help someone with whom I have no relationship with.</p>
<p>Still, if I were to give a reason for helping her, it would be because of the two faces that flashed in my mind.</p>

<p>I can't say for sure that I have learned from my past mistakes.<br>So, let's just call it a whim.</p>

<p>It's not that I have any personal feelings about helping Shiraishi.<br>Let's just say that for now.</p>

<p>"I-is that true?"</p>
<p></p>
<p>"Calm down ...we're just talking about possibilities."</p>

<p>When I told her that there were ways other than the student council that I could help, Shiraishi's eyes lit up and she tried to move the conversation forward.</p>
<p>I put my left hand out in front of me to stop her.</p>
<p>There was something I wanted to confirm before proceeding with this conversation.</p>

<p>If I didn't make that clear, this method might end up making her get her hopes up.</p>

<p>It was still early in the summer vacation.<br>There is still about a month to go before the first day of school.</p>

<p>The student council elections will start in the second semester, so if we fo by the method I'm thinking of, there's no problem even if we don't act immediately.</p>

<p>"Let's call it a day ...I have a few things I need to look into."</p>

<p>"B-but you haven't told any specifics yet..."</p>

<p>"Talking about an idea without certainty might only raise expectations. ...so that's it for today."</p>

<p>I managed to persuade Shiraishi, who looked unhappy and unconvinced, and then left.<br>Shiraishi went home, and I walked once more to the student council room of Sakuranaoka Academy.</p>

<p>When we parted ways, I gave her my phone number and asked her to call me later when she was free, so she shouldn't have any problems getting in touch with me.</p>
<p>No, I thought about exchanging contact information first, but for some reason I was reluctant to ask for a girl's contact info, so I just gave her my phone number instead, which was extremely inefficient, needless to say.</p>


<p>Summer days are long.<br>The sun was just a little over my head, and the kids were still running around.</p>
<p>Perhaps <strong>that</strong> person is still in that room.<br>Probably sitting there like the master of that room.</p>

<p>It isn't certain, but I know.</p>

<p>I thought it was just a rumor, but it was only a few minutes ago that I came to the conclusion that president knew the name of every student who attended the school, and I wondered if it was possible for her to even know something that I was unaware of.</p>
<p>That's exactly what I noticed when I was having a conversation with Shiraishi at the coffee shop.</p>

<p>I thought it was an excessive speculation, but I still couldn't stop thinking about it.</p>

<p>In spite of racking my brain, progress was slow.<br>There was no point in running to reach the student council room if I couldn't make a calm decision.</p>
<p>Slowly but surely, I walked back to the school in about twenty minutes.</p>

<p>The school was still quiet.<br>There were no students in my line of sight.</p>

<p>I went back the way I came and arrived in front of the room I wanted.</p>
<p>I knocked on the heavy iron door, making a loud sound, and opened it without waiting for an answer.<br>The air in the room was cool and pleasant from the hot and humid summer temperature.</p>

<p>I closed the door quickly to keep the cool air inside, and turned my gaze to where the boss this place was.</p>
<p>There she was, after all.</p>

<p>"Shinra, you're back earlier than I expected."</p>

<p>"I don't remember saying that I was coming back. ...Well, I guess the president already knew that I would come back."</p>

<p>Akane Hiiragi, the head of the student council, was sitting on her seat with the same smile on her face as when she sent me off.<br>She was seated just like the last boss. I sat down at my seat in the student council room, which was empty except for the president, and looked at her.</p>

<p>"So, how did the conversation go?"</p>

<p>"Well..."</p>

<p>I knew without being told that she was referring to the conversation between me and Shiraishi.<br>In order to explain, I had to find out how much president knew before I could go any further.</p>

<p>"How much does president know?"</p>

<p>"How much... well, at least I don't know what Shiraishi and Shinra talked about today."</p>

<p>In other words, she know everything else.<br>She knows the true character of Shiraishi.</p>

<p>"Does that mean the way she was acting when she came to the student council room yesterday was also an act?</p>

<p>The surprised look on her face, the slightly angry tone of her voice, if it was all an act, it was a genuine performance.</p>
<p>I couldn't see through it.</p>

<p>"I'm proud to say that I'm a little better at this than most of you..."</p>

<p>From the expression with a bitter smile, president probably didn't mean to know about it either. <br>Sometimes a position requires us to hear or know something that we don't really mean to.</p>

<p>And when we didn't see it coming, it was our fault too.<br>I'm not going to say anything because president had her own ideas and intentions.</p>
<p>
</p><p>"I'm investigating what kind of student Shiraishi is, not to mention her lifestyle and her academics ...I immediately noticed that she was acting differently than her usual self."</p>
<p>
</p><p>"So ...do you know what motivated her to run for the position?"</p>

<p>"No, as I said earlier, I only know what I heard yesterday."</p>

<p>She asked again, 'What did you talk about today?'</p>
<p></p>
<p>How much should I tell her?<br>I had to sort through the information and find the correct words.</p>
<p>It's going to take some time.</p>

<p>"No problem, we have plenty of time."</p>

<p>President smiled and said so.</p>
<p>If I talk too much, it will be difficult to understand.</p>
<p>The first thing I said was my idea of how I would lend her a hand.</p>

<p>"I'm thinking of having Shiraishi run for the head of the executive committee of the Sakura Festival*." <br>(TL/N - Cherry Blossom Festival)</p>
<p>President nodded happily at these words.</p>
<p></p>
