After school & hot springs PART - 2

BrutalBot September 16, 2021

 

This is a continuation of the side story.

"Onsen." (TL/N - Hot Springs)

What is the first thing that comes to your mind when you hear this word?

Is it a bathtub full of steam, or a woman in a yukata? Everyone has a different image in their mind.

For me, the first thing that came to mind was an old man with a white towel on his head. No thoughts behind it, it was simply the first scene that came to mind.

I had been forcibly taken away from school by Kirasaka, and found myself being driven down the Odawara Bypass in Kanagawa Prefecture.

The car driven by Kuroi-san was a limousine, a must have for the rich people troupe.

Since he had given me a ride in a black van in the past, I had assumed that it would be the same car this time, but it was actually limousine that came to pick me up.

Needless to say, I was so overwhelmed by the situation that I couldn't say a word... Anyway, we are now on our way to Hakone, but the air in the car is heavy.

The atmosphere in front of me was so heavy that anyone was hesitant to utter a word.

The reason for this is quite simple.

Kirasaka Rei and Kanazaki Shizuku were sitting face to face, playing a serious game of Old Maid.

The card game, well, it's playing cards, but actually they are currently facing off in a game of Old Maid.

Me and Yuuto had already finished all the cards in our hands early, but surprisingly, these two were still at it.

If my prediction was correct, the Joker was in Shizuku's hand, and if Kirasaka drew a card other than the Joker, she would win, and if she drew the other card, she would be in trouble.

In such a situation, Kirasaka draws the card on the right without hesitation.

"Oh, Kirasaka-san, you fall for things so easily."

"...This vixen..."

...Oi,oi.

I'm not sure why they are taking this so seriously.

Kirasaka, who made the wrong choice, seems to have been frustrated by having been bad-mouthed, but on the other hand Shizuku was smiling.

It's funny ...if I remember correctly, card are supposed to be played for fun...

It's good to be serious at things, but you can't be this serious about this kind of game.

However, when Yuuto and I finished earlier, the atmosphere changed suddenly. The emotions completely drained from their faces, and silence took place as if they were trying to read each other's minds with their poker faces on.

They exchanged their cards several times, until Kirasaka now held the Joker, but these two are truly incompatible.

Even in a game like this perhaps they simply don't want to lose to one another, as they wore the grim, firm expressions of true gamblers.

"I've never seen such a heavy game of old maid before."

"Heh, ...it's my first time seeing one either."

Yuuto, who was sitting next to me, muttered in a faint voice.


In the end, I was not able to escape being dragged along Kirasaka, so I presented one condition to come along.

It was, to let Yuuto, who is right in front of me, accompany me.
Although I could enjoy the hot spring alone, I thought it would be better to bring him along, considering the other people with whom I was going.

However, I think I've been seeing a good amount of bitter smiles on Yuuto's face lately.

It's hard to ask, though, because I'm aware that the three of us are the ones who are responsible for it. The girls exchanged jokers in the same way repeatedly more than a dozen times.

Before the game ended, Kuroi-san called out to us.

"Ojou-sama, everyone, we will be arriving in about five minutes."

"So ...let's leave this game to be decided at table tennis after the onsen."

"I look forward to it... Just try not to cry when you lose, alright?

The two of them gave up on the game here, and put their cards on the table to call a temporary truce. I looked out the window of the car and saw a landscape that was different from the town we lived in.

 

My family had been here before, but it had been a while since I had been to a hot spring resort, and I felt my spirits lift a little.
At the same time, I remembered Kaede's phone call a while ago. Kaede answered the phone as usual, but she was sulking a little.

And naturally so. It was only her voice, but I could tell that she had a frustrated expression on the other end.

Naturally, it's normal to be envious when your brother goes to the hot springs alone.

When I told her that I would give up my next day off to go with her to a place of her choice as an apology, her tone changed and it was good to know there were no problems for the time being.

If I haven't come up with this kind of a compromise, I might not be able to eat for a few days, which would have been an absolute disaster.


With my stomach at the hands of my sister's mercy, it appeared that the hierarchical relationship in my family was already decided.

Anyway, it was confirmed that I would have to give up my day off for the second week in a row. My plan to rest is getting farther and farther away.

The car driven by Kuroi-san stopped when it reached the entrance of the building instead of the parking lot.

There were already several employees waiting in line at the entrance, and one of them opened the back seat of our parked car.

 

As I was feeling a little suspicious about his behavior, Kirasaka was the first to get out.

"We've been expecting you, Rei-ojousama."

"I'm sorry to bother you all of a sudden, but I'm going to borrow your facilities for a little while."

"Don't tell me ...this place is also a part of Kirasaka's company?"

After getting out of the car, I was struck by the conversation that was taking place.
It's very easy to imagine the answer to this question, as the white-haired man who was probably the director bowed his head several times and was being polite to Kirasaka, a student.

Kirasaka answered the question that seemed to have no need for an answer.

"Yes, I want to go to the hot spring, but I don't like crowds, so I asked for it to be reserved just for us."

"Apologize to the people who were planning to come here today!"

"Don't worry, I've already sent the people who had reservations to other establishments."

 

I wonder how much money this woman is spending on an after-school, spur-of-the-moment hot springs day.

However, it's possible that we won't be able enjoy the hot springs because of the guilt.

As if to make amends for Kirasaka who was already strolling nonchalantly inside. I bowed deeply to the staff members several times before following after her.
