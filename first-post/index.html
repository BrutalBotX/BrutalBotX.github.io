Picking Up 'Ordinary Me & Extraordinary Them' & some more info

 

Yo guys! BrutalBot here.

It surely has been a while since I posted something, not that most of you guys would know of.

Well let me just introduce myself here, on this new site. Just an ordinary student here passing his time. Just so you know I dunno Japanese(XD), but I've read countless manga, LNs & WNs and hentai.

Oh! Also I don't have any previous experience with websites & translating novels. But I do have some experience scanlating manga. I won't be able to guarantee the accuracy but try my best to match the original content. Also English ain't my first language.

I just wanna give back to the community which provided me with such materials to enjoy by doing some myself in my free time.

So I decided to go with 'Ordinary I & Extraordinary Me', one of my favorites, since the previous translator has dropped it (Read more here). All the previous translators have my thanks for translating this far.

I am not too good at expressing myself so I will keep it short why I chose to translate this, 'SLOW PROGRESSION' .
That's enough to pick a project, right?

I'll post the next chapter, in this case it is a side story with 3 parts in a few hours, I'll also try to finish other two parts within this month or possibly the first week of the next, updates will be slow for now for IRL reasons. Updates should be more stable from the second week of the next month.

Well I am also a reader & like you I also hate sites with ads, bad translations & dirty looking sites.
So I will keep in mind to never let you down in terms of quality and will try to keep the site clean & simple.

...Well there may be some issues, like Wordpress putting in ads to cover up the costs for maintaining this site. Just use an AdBlock or something.

Well... I won't be asking for you all to support unnecessarily since I decided to do this on my own, but if there are some people who wanna waste their money, I won't mind :P


Oh! And before I forget I know most of you all hate bright colored sites like this one, so if I save enough money to buy the paid plan, I'll add the dark mode (or you can just go ahead and use some extensions).

Well that's all for this post, thanks for reading.


Btw, here's my NU Romcom list if you want to try some more novels while waiting for the next chapters (haven't updated for a while): https://www.novelupdates.com/viewlist/29261/
